53 of 379 tracked neobanks · verified from public filings and product docs · updated 2026-08-13

short answer53 of the 379 neobanks tracked here are self-custodial neobanks. These are software, not institutions: you hold the keys and the company cannot freeze, lend or lose your balance. The largest by disclosed users are Phantom, MiniPay and Zengo; all 53 are listed below with custody, license and geography.

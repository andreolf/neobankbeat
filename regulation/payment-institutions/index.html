Neobanks authorised as payment institutions

13 of 381 tracked neobanks · verified from public filings and product docs · updated 2026-08-28

short answer13 of the 381 neobanks tracked here are payment institutions. A payment institution may move money and issue payment instruments but may not hold deposits or issue e-money in its own right. They include Aspire, Beyon Money, CoinJar, Cora and Deblock; all 13 are listed below with custody, license and geography.

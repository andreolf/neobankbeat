33 of 379 tracked neobanks · verified from public filings and product docs · updated 2026-08-17

short answer33 of the 379 neobanks tracked here are card-only KYC neobanks. The wallet itself needs no verification, but the card does, because the card is issued by a licensed partner that carries the regulatory obligation. They include 1inch Card, Avalanche Card, Avici, Bitget Wallet and Bleap; all 33 are listed below with custody, license and geography.

The wallet itself needs no verification, but the card does, because the card is issued by a licensed partner that carries the regulatory obligation. In practice this means you can hold and spend on-chain freely, and verify once if you want a physical card.

334 of 381 tracked neobanks · verified from public filings and product docs · updated 2026-09-11

short answer334 of the 381 neobanks tracked here are full-KYC neobanks. Opening one of these means handing over a government ID — usually a passport or driving licence scan, very often a selfie taken against it — to a regulated company that is then obliged to keep it. The largest by disclosed users are WeBank, PhonePe, Nubank, GCash and Mercado Pago; all 334 are listed below with custody, license and geography.

Opening one of these means handing over a government ID — usually a passport or driving licence scan, very often a selfie taken against it — to a regulated company that is then obliged to keep it. Anti-money-laundering rules across the UK, EU and US require firms to retain identification records for years after an account closes, so the copy does not disappear when you do. That obligation is also why these firms answer requests from law enforcement and government agencies: a documented disclosure process is part of holding the licence. Worth knowing before you open your fifth account.

Common questions

Neobanks that require identity verification: how many are there?

334 of the 381 neobanks tracked here are full-KYC neobanks. Every entry is verified active and listed on this page with its custody model, license detail and the regions it serves.

Which are the biggest full-KYC neobanks?

By self-reported users, WeBank, PhonePe, Nubank, GCash and Mercado Pago. Only 20 of these 334 disclose a user figure, so treat any ranking by size as partial rather than complete.

What does this category actually mean?

Opening one of these means handing over a government ID — usually a passport or driving licence scan, very often a selfie taken against it — to a regulated company that is then obliged to keep it. Anti-money-laundering rules across the UK, EU and US require firms to retain identification records for years after an account closes, so the copy does not disappear when you do. That obligation is also why these firms answer requests from law enforcement and government agencies: a documented disclosure process is part of holding the licence. Worth knowing before you open your fifth account.

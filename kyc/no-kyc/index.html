Neobanks that work without identity verification

14 of 381 tracked neobanks · verified from public filings and product docs · updated 2026-09-04

short answer14 of the 381 neobanks tracked here are no-KYC neobanks. These require no identity check because they never take custody of your funds — they are self-custodial software, so there is no account to open and no balance to hold on your behalf. The largest by disclosed users are Phantom, MiniPay and Zengo; all 14 are listed below with custody, license and geography.

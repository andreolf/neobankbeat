How many neobanks work in Switzerland?

We have verified country-level availability for 8 of 368 tracked neobanks in Switzerland. Another set may serve the wider Europe region without a confirmed in-country flag — those appear only when region overlap is the best match, and we label them as such.

How are matches scored?

Country fit first, then audience (personal vs SMB), your stated priorities, custody preference, card requirement and crypto use. Disclosed user counts break ties only — only a fraction of neobanks publish user numbers.

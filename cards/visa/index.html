Common questions

Neobanks that issue a Visa card: how many are there?

184 of the 381 neobanks tracked here are Visa neobanks. Every entry is verified active and listed on this page with its custody model, license detail and the regions it serves.

Which are the biggest Visa neobanks?

By self-reported users, GCash, Mercado Pago, Cash App, PicPay and Revolut. Only 11 of these 184 disclose a user figure, so treat any ranking by size as partial rather than complete.

What does this category actually mean?

The network on the card is chosen by whoever issues it, which for most neobanks is a sponsor bank or a BIN sponsor rather than the brand on the app. It matters for acceptance abroad and for which benefits apply, but it tells you nothing about who holds your money.

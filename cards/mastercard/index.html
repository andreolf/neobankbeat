150 of the 379 neobanks tracked here are Mastercard neobanks. Every entry is verified active and listed on this page with its custody model, license detail and the regions it serves.

Which are the biggest Mastercard neobanks?

By self-reported users, Nubank, Mercado Pago, OPay, Revolut and Banco Inter. Only 8 of these 150 disclose a user figure, so treat any ranking by size as partial rather than complete.

What does this category actually mean?

The network on the card is chosen by whoever issues it, which for most neobanks is a sponsor bank or a BIN sponsor rather than the brand on the app. Some products run both schemes depending on the market, so they appear on the Visa list too.

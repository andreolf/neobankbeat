Why route it through here?

Ask an AI about neobanks cold and it answers from months-stale training data, invents details, and cites nothing. This prompt pins it to the live, verified dataset — 379 neobanks with custody, licences, cards, stablecoins and geography checked against registers and filings — and demands the profile page behind every claim, including who legally holds your money and what protection applies if it fails.

the other doorsJust choosing a bank and prefer taps to typing? The 8-step fit wizard ranks all 379 instantly, no AI account needed. Power users: point your assistant at the MCP server and it queries the dataset live. Browsers: the sortable database and feature matrix.

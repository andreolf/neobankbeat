Qonto vs Revolut

traditional vs hybrid · from the open dataset of 379 tracked neobanks · no affiliate links, ever

short answerQonto is a traditional neobank and Revolut is hybrid. Qonto is built for SMB & startups; Revolut holds its own banking license and is the larger platform (~50M users). Compare custody, license, FX markup and fees field by field below.

Qonto: 500K+ SME clients across FR/DE/ES/IT.

Revolut: 50M+ users; crypto trading; stablecoin in FCA sandbox 2026.

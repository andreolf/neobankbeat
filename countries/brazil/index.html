short answer11 of the 379 neobanks tracked here are headquartered in Brazil. Headquarters is a complete field here; availability is not, so the two are counted separately below. The largest by disclosed users are Nubank, PicPay, Banco Inter and PagBank; all 11 are listed below, then the 16 available there without being based there.

These 11 are headquartered in Brazil — parsed from the registered base of each company, so the count is complete. Availability is a different and much harder question: it is verified for 21 products in Brazil so far, out of 379 tracked, so a neobank missing from the second table below has not been ruled out — it has not been checked.

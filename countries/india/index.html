Neobanks in India

12 of 379 tracked neobanks · verified from public filings and product docs · updated 2026-08-17

short answer12 of the 379 neobanks tracked here are headquartered in India. Headquarters is a complete field here; availability is not, so the two are counted separately below. They include Airtel Payments Bank, FamPay, Freo, Jio Payments Bank and Jupiter; all 12 are listed below, then the 5 available there without being based there.

These 12 are headquartered in India — parsed from the registered base of each company, so the count is complete. Availability is a different and much harder question: it is verified for 7 products in India so far, out of 379 tracked, so a neobank missing from the second table below has not been ruled out — it has not been checked. Corrections are welcome on GitHub.

Chipper Cash

hybrid · San Francisco, US · est. 2018 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerChipper Cash is a fiat-and-crypto neobank, headquartered in San Francisco, US and founded in 2018. Your money is held custodially, so Chipper Cash or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as an e-money institution (Licensed e-money/payments across Africa (CBN, BoG, BoU, BNR); 40+ US money-transmitter licenses; FCA EMI (UK)). It issues a Visa virtual and physical card. It is available in 8 countries, across Africa, North America and Europe.

Ugandan-Ghanaian founders built pan-African P2P; survived the FTX-investor winter and refocused on cards + crypto.

What is Chipper Cash?

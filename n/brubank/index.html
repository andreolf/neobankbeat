Common questions about Brubank

What is Brubank?

Brubank is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Buenos Aires, AR and founded in 2017. Your money is held custodially, so Brubank or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (Bank license (BCRA)).

Does Brubank have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa debit card. It is available in Latin America.

Does Brubank require KYC?

Yes. Brubank requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a licensed bank.

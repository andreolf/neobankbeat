Common questions about TBC UZ

What is TBC UZ?

TBC UZ is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Tashkent, UZ and founded in 2020. Your money is held custodially, so TBC UZ or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (Bank (TBC Group)).

Does TBC UZ have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Mastercard debit card. It is available in Europe.

Does TBC UZ require KYC?

Yes. TBC UZ requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a licensed bank.

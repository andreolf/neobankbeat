Common questions about Klarna

What is Klarna?

Klarna is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Stockholm, SE and founded in 2005. Your money is held custodially, so Klarna or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (Swedish banking license).

Does Klarna have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa debit and bnpl card. It is available in 6 countries, across Europe.

Does Klarna require KYC?

Yes. Klarna requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a licensed bank.

What does Klarna cost?

From the verified fields: cashback Rewards club; yield Savings (region). Cashback and yield are "up to" figures that change constantly and often depend on a paid plan — confirm current terms with Klarna before relying on them.

Casas Bahia Pay

traditional · São Paulo, BR · est. 2018 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerCasas Bahia Pay is a fiat neobank built for underbanked, headquartered in São Paulo, BR and founded in 2018. Your money is held custodially, so Casas Bahia Pay or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as an e-money institution (Banco Central-authorised e-money issuer (Banqi Instituição de Pagamento Ltda); prepaid Mastercard; loans via group credit fintech BNQI SCD). It issues a Mastercard prepaid card. It is available in Latin America.

The retailer's own account for shoppers banks overlook: no proof of income, cash deposits at the till, and the store carnê paid from the same app.

Casas Bahia's retail account (ex-banQi, renamed 2026): free Pix, prepaid Mastercard, cash in and out at stores.

Movii

traditional · Bogotá, CO · est. 2018 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerMovii is a fiat neobank built for underbanked, headquartered in Bogotá, CO and founded in 2018. Your money is held custodially, so Movii or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as an e-money institution (SEDPE e-money license (Superintendencia Financiera, Colombia)). It issues a Mastercard prepaid card. It is available in Latin America.

Common questions about Movii

What is Movii?

Movii is a fiat neobank built for underbanked, headquartered in Bogotá, CO and founded in 2018. Your money is held custodially, so Movii or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as an e-money institution (SEDPE e-money license (Superintendencia Financiera, Colombia)).

Does Movii have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Mastercard prepaid card. It is available in Latin America.

Does Movii require KYC?

Yes. Movii requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a e-money institution.

BFinance

hybrid · Prague, CZ · est. 2023 · open in the directory → · Deutsch →

short answerBFinance is a fiat-and-crypto neobank, headquartered in Prague, CZ and founded in 2023. Your money is held custodially, so BFinance or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It holds VASP / MSB / crypto licenses (Czech VASP registration; Marqeta-issued cards). It issues a Visa and Mastercard virtual and physical card. It is available in 3 countries, across all 7 world regions tracked here.

A card programme that lives entirely inside a Telegram bot — crude, fee-heavy, and reportedly moving ~$20M a month anyway.

Telegram-bot cards; ~$20M/mo volume; $10 issue + 2% top-up fees.

Common questions about Bitpanda

What is Bitpanda?

Bitpanda is a fiat-and-crypto neobank, headquartered in Vienna, AT and founded in 2014. Your money is held custodially, so Bitpanda or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a MiCA CASP (EU) (MiCA (AT); BaFin licenses).

Does Bitpanda have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa debit card. It is available in 6 countries, across Europe.

Does Bitpanda require KYC?

Yes. Bitpanda requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a mica casp (eu).

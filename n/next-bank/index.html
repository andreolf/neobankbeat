Common questions about NEXT Bank

What is NEXT Bank?

NEXT Bank is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Taipei, TW and founded in 2022. Your money is held custodially, so NEXT Bank or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (Virtual bank license (TW FSC)).

Does NEXT Bank have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues no card of its own. It is available in Asia.

Does NEXT Bank require KYC?

Yes. NEXT Bank requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a licensed bank.

Monese

traditional · London, UK · est. 2013 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerMonese is a fiat neobank built for immigrants & migrants, headquartered in London, UK and founded in 2013. Your money is held custodially, so Monese or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as an e-money institution (FCA-authorised e-money institution (FRN 900960); UK accounts as agent of PrePay Technologies; owned by Pockit). It issues a Mastercard debit card. It is available in 2 countries, across Europe.

An Estonian founder rejected by UK banks built no-credit-history onboarding for migrants; consumer app later folded into B2B platform XYB.

No-credit-history onboarding; consumer app scaled back to B2B (XYB).

Who owns Buenbit?

hybrid · Buenos Aires, AR · VASP / MSB / crypto licenses · custody: Custodial

short answerBuenbit is regulated as a crypto-asset service under VASP / MSB / crypto licenses, not a bank. It is not a deposit-taking institution; how balances are held depends on its custody model (Custodial).

Is Buenbit a licensed bank?

No — it is licensed as a crypto / VASP service, not a bank. License detail: VASP — registered PSAV with CNV (Argentina); stablecoin-focused exchange + card.

Where is your money held?

Custody model: Custodial.

Who's behind Buenbit?

Buenbit was founded by Federico Ogue (2018), headquartered in Buenos Aires, AR.

Is my money safe with Buenbit?

Buenbit is not a bank, so deposit-guarantee schemes do not apply. Crypto and cash balances are handled under its VASP/e-money obligations — check its terms for exactly how client assets are segregated and safeguarded.

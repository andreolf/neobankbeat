Common questions about One Zero

What is One Zero?

One Zero is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Tel Aviv, IL and founded in 2019. Your money is held custodially, so One Zero or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (Israeli banking license).

Does One Zero have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Mastercard debit card. It is available in MENA.

Does One Zero require KYC?

Yes. One Zero requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a licensed bank.

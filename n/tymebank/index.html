Common questions about TymeBank

What is TymeBank?

TymeBank is a fiat neobank built for underbanked, headquartered in Johannesburg, ZA and founded in 2018. Your money is held custodially, so TymeBank or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (SA banking license).

Does TymeBank have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa debit card. It is available in Africa.

Does TymeBank require KYC?

Yes. TymeBank requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a licensed bank.

What does TymeBank cost?

From the verified fields: cashback Smart Shopper; yield Up to ~10% GoalSave. Cashback and yield are "up to" figures that change constantly and often depend on a paid plan — confirm current terms with TymeBank before relying on them.

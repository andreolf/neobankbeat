Kontist

traditional · Berlin, DE · est. 2016 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerKontist is a fiat neobank built for freelancers & creators, headquartered in Berlin, DE and founded in 2016. Your money is held custodially, so Kontist or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (Partner bank (Solaris SE, BaFin-licensed); German IBAN accounts). It issues a Visa business debit card. It is available in Europe.

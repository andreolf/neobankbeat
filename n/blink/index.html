Blink

traditional · Amman, JO · est. 2022 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerBlink is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Amman, JO and founded in 2022. Your money is held custodially, so Blink or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (Bank-owned neobank on Capital Bank of Jordan's banking license). It issues no card of its own. It is available in MENA.

Launched in 2022 on Codebase Technologies' Digibanc platform with a swipe-based, social-media-style UI for Jordan's underbanked youth.

Jordan's first digital-only neobank by Capital Bank of Jordan — app-based account opening with a Jordanian ID in about 15 minutes, an instant virtual debit card and instant credit cards.

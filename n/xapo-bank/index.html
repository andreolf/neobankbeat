Xapo Bank

hybrid · Gibraltar · est. 2013 · open in the directory →

short answerXapo Bank is a fiat-and-crypto neobank, headquartered in Gibraltar and founded in 2013. Your money is held custodially, so Xapo Bank or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (Gibraltar banking license). It issues a Mastercard debit card. It is available in 4 countries, across all 7 world regions tracked here.

Casares — patient zero of Silicon Valley bitcoin — built a private bank for BTC wealth after his family lost everything to Argentine devaluations.

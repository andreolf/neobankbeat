NG.cash

traditional · Rio de Janeiro, BR · est. 2021 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerNG.cash is a fiat neobank built for gen z & students, headquartered in Rio de Janeiro, BR and founded in 2021. Your money is held custodially, so NG.cash or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as an e-money institution (Payment institution (Banco Central do Brasil, e-money issuer license, May 2025)). It issues a Mastercard debit card. It is available in Latin America.

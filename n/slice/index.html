Common questions about slice

What is slice?

slice is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Bengaluru, IN and founded in 2016. Your money is held custodially, so slice or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (Small finance bank (via NESFB merger)).

Does slice have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa card and upi. It is available in Asia.

Does slice require KYC?

Yes. slice requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a licensed bank.

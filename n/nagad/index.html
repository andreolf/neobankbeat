Nagad

traditional · Dhaka, BD · est. 2019 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerNagad is a fiat neobank built for underbanked, headquartered in Dhaka, BD and founded in 2019. Your money is held custodially, so Nagad or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (Mobile money license (Bangladesh Bank MFS, with Bangladesh Post Office); 2024 digital-bank license under review). It issues no card of its own. It is available in Asia.

Common questions about Nagad

What is Nagad?

Nagad is a fiat neobank built for underbanked, headquartered in Dhaka, BD and founded in 2019. Your money is held custodially, so Nagad or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (Mobile money license (Bangladesh Bank MFS, with Bangladesh Post Office); 2024 digital-bank license under review).

Does Nagad have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues no card of its own. It is available in Asia.

Does Nagad require KYC?

Yes. Nagad requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a licensed bank.

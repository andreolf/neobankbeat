Common questions about Chase UK

What is Chase UK?

Chase UK is a fiat neobank, headquartered in London, UK and founded in 2021. Your money is held custodially, so Chase UK or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (UK subsidiary of JPMorgan).

Does Chase UK have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa debit card. It is available in Europe.

Does Chase UK require KYC?

Yes. Chase UK requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a partner-bank model.

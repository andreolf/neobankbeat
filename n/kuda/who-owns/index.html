Who owns Kuda?

traditional · Lagos, NG · Licensed bank · custody: Custodial

short answerKuda holds its own banking license, so Kuda itself is the bank — customer deposits sit on its own regulated balance sheet rather than with a third-party sponsor. Kuda's most notable disclosed backers include Valar Ventures, Target Global and SBI Group.

Is Kuda a licensed bank?

Yes — it holds its own banking license. License detail: Microfinance bank (NG).

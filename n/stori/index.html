Common questions about Stori

What is Stori?

Stori is a fiat neobank built for underbanked, headquartered in Mexico City, MX and founded in 2018. Your money is held custodially, so Stori or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (Sofipo (MX)).

Does Stori have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Mastercard credit card. It is available in Latin America.

Does Stori require KYC?

Yes. Stori requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a licensed bank.

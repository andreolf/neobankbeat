Common questions about One

What is One?

One is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Sacramento, US and founded in 2019. Your money is held custodially, so One or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (Partner bank (Coastal Community)).

Does One have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa debit card. It is available in North America.

Does One require KYC?

Yes. One requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a partner-bank model.

What does One cost?

From the verified fields: cashback Up to 3% (Walmart); yield ~4% APY savings. Cashback and yield are "up to" figures that change constantly and often depend on a paid plan — confirm current terms with One before relying on them.

short answerHaventree Bank is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Toronto, CA and founded in 2018. Your money is held custodially, so Haventree Bank or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (Schedule I Canadian bank (CDIC member)). It issues no card of its own. It is available in North America.

A broker-channel alternative mortgage lender that built its own platform to go direct-to-consumer as a complementary savings home.

Nickel

traditional · Paris, FR · est. 2014 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerNickel is a fiat neobank built for underbanked, headquartered in Paris, FR and founded in 2014. Your money is held custodially, so Nickel or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (Payment institution (Financière des Paiements Électroniques, ACPR no. 16598); BNP Paribas subsidiary). It issues a Mastercard debit card. It is available in 5 countries, across Europe.

Opened accounts through 6,500 tobacco shops — banking where French banks refused to go.

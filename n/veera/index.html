Veera

web3-native · Singapore, SG · est. 2023 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerVeera is a self-custodial, on-chain-first neobank, headquartered in Singapore, SG and founded in 2023. You hold the keys: Veera never takes custody of your funds. It is software rather than a licensed institution (Self-custodial; passkey-secured non-exportable keys). It issues no card of its own. It is available in all 7 world regions tracked here.

A serial founder with three exits is folding wallet, broker and bank into one self-custodial app — multichain vaults across 40 chains, tokenized gold and equities, and a card waitlist at 30K before the VEERA token even launched.

Onchain neobank: earn, invest (tokenized equities, gold, RWAs), borrow and spend self-custodially; 2M+ users in 187 countries, 30K cards.

Common questions about Veera

What is Veera?

Veera is a self-custodial, on-chain-first neobank, headquartered in Singapore, SG and founded in 2023. You hold the keys: Veera never takes custody of your funds. It is software rather than a licensed institution (Self-custodial; passkey-secured non-exportable keys).

Does Veera have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues no card of its own. It is available in all 7 world regions tracked here.

Does Veera require KYC?

Partly. You can use Veera itself without verifying your identity, but ordering its card requires KYC.

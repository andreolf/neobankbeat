Your money is held custodially, so TNEX or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. Common questions about TNEX

What is TNEX?

TNEX is a fiat neobank built for gen z & students, headquartered in Hanoi, VN and founded in 2020. Your money is held custodially, so TNEX or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (Operates under Maritime Bank's (Vietnam) banking license as its digital bank).

Does TNEX have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues no card of its own. It is available in Asia.

Does TNEX require KYC?

Yes. TNEX requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a licensed bank.

short answerTrade Republic is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Berlin, DE and founded in 2015. Your money is held custodially, so Trade Republic or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (German full banking license (2023)). Common questions about Trade Republic

What is Trade Republic?

Trade Republic is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Berlin, DE and founded in 2015. Your money is held custodially, so Trade Republic or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (German full banking license (2023)).

Does Trade Republic have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa debit card. It is available in 9 countries, across Europe.

Does Trade Republic require KYC?

Yes. Trade Republic requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a licensed bank.

What does Trade Republic cost?

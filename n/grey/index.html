Grey

traditional · Lagos, NG · est. 2020 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerGrey is a fiat neobank built for freelancers & creators, headquartered in Lagos, NG and founded in 2020. Your money is held custodially, so Grey or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It holds VASP / MSB / crypto licenses (MSB registrations (FinCEN US, FINTRAC Canada); accounts via licensed banking partners). It issues a Mastercard virtual usd cards. It is available in 2 countries, across Africa.

USD/GBP/EUR accounts for African freelancers.

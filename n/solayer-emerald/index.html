Common questions about Solayer Emerald

What is Solayer Emerald?

Solayer Emerald is a self-custodial, on-chain-first neobank, founded in 2024. You hold the keys: Solayer Emerald never takes custody of your funds. It is software rather than a licensed institution (Self-custodial; Visa principal program).

Does Solayer Emerald have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa virtual and physical card, and identity checks apply to the card but not to the wallet. It is available in all 7 world regions tracked here.

Does Solayer Emerald require KYC?

Partly. You can use Solayer Emerald itself without verifying your identity, but ordering its card requires KYC.

What does Solayer Emerald cost?

From the verified fields: cashback Rewards; yield Restaking-linked (sUSD). Cashback and yield are "up to" figures that change constantly and often depend on a paid plan — confirm current terms with Solayer Emerald before relying on them.

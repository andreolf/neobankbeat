Common questions about Bleap

What is Bleap?

Bleap is a self-custodial, on-chain-first neobank, headquartered in London, UK and founded in 2024. You hold the keys: Bleap never takes custody of your funds. It is software rather than a licensed institution (MPC self-custody; MC principal member).

Does Bleap have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Mastercard virtual and physical card, and identity checks apply to the card but not to the wallet. It is available in all 7 world regions tracked here.

Does Bleap require KYC?

Partly. You can use Bleap itself without verifying your identity, but ordering its card requires KYC.

From the verified fields: cashback Up to 20% promo USDC. Cashback and yield are "up to" figures that change constantly and often depend on a paid plan — confirm current terms with Bleap before relying on them.

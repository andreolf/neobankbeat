short answerMonzo is a fiat neobank, headquartered in London, UK and founded in 2015. Your money is held custodially, so Monzo or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (UK banking license). It issues a Mastercard debit card. It is available in 2 countries, across Europe.

The hot-coral card that made banking a brand; born from the Mondo prepaid beta and a record 96-second crowdfund.

Common questions about Monzo

What is Monzo?

Monzo is a fiat neobank, headquartered in London, UK and founded in 2015. Your money is held custodially, so Monzo or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (UK banking license).

Does Monzo have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Mastercard debit card. It is available in 2 countries, across Europe.

Does Monzo require KYC?

Yes. Monzo requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a licensed bank.

What does Monzo cost?

From the verified fields: FX abroad — 0% foreign transaction fee; Mastercard exchange rate, all tiers (as of 2026-07); yield ~4% savings pots. Cashback and yield are "up to" figures that change constantly and often depend on a paid plan — confirm current terms with Monzo before relying on them.

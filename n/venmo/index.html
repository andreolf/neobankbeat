short answerVenmo is a fiat-and-crypto neobank, headquartered in New York, US and founded in 2009. Your money is held custodially, so Venmo or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It holds VASP / MSB / crypto licenses (PayPal's US state licenses (money transmitter, NMLS 910457); crypto via NYDFS trust company PayPal Digital). It issues a Visa and Mastercard debit and credit card. It is available in North America.

Common questions about Venmo

What is Venmo?

Venmo is a fiat-and-crypto neobank, headquartered in New York, US and founded in 2009. Your money is held custodially, so Venmo or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It holds VASP / MSB / crypto licenses (PayPal's US state licenses (money transmitter, NMLS 910457); crypto via NYDFS trust company PayPal Digital).

It issues a Visa and Mastercard debit and credit card. It is available in North America.

Does Venmo require KYC?

Oraan

traditional · Karachi, PK · est. 2018 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerOraan is a fiat neobank built for women, headquartered in Karachi, PK and founded in 2018. Your money is held custodially, so Oraan or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. Its regulatory structure is mixed (SECP-registered; NBFC (non-bank finance company) license from SECP Pakistan for lending; digital ROSCAs). It issues no card of its own. It is available in Asia.

Digitized Pakistan's women-led savings committees (ROSCAs) into a regulated product.

Women-first fintech digitizing ROSCAs.

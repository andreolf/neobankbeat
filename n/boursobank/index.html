BoursoBank

short answerBoursoBank is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Paris, FR and founded in 1995. Your money is held custodially, so BoursoBank or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (Bank (Société Générale)). It issues a Visa debit and credit card. It is available in Europe.

France's largest online bank; 7M+ (SocGen).

Common questions about Holyheld

What is Holyheld?

Holyheld is a self-custodial, on-chain-first neobank built for freelancers & creators, headquartered in London, UK and founded in 2020. You hold the keys: Holyheld never takes custody of your funds. It is regulated as an e-money institution (E-money partners (EU); non-custodial top-ups).

Does Holyheld have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Mastercard virtual and physical card, and identity checks apply to the card but not to the wallet. It is available in Europe.

Does Holyheld require KYC?

Partly. You can use Holyheld itself without verifying your identity, but ordering its card requires KYC.

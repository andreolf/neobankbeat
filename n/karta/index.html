Karta

web3-native · Redwood City, US · est. 2020 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerKarta is a self-custodial, on-chain-first neobank built for travel & digital nomads, headquartered in Redwood City, US and founded in 2020. You hold the keys: Karta never takes custody of your funds. It is software rather than a licensed institution (Self-custodial (MPC via Privy); Visa issued by Rain). It issues a Visa virtual and physical card, and identity checks apply to the card but not to the wallet. It is available in all 7 world regions tracked here.

25K+ monthly cardholders spending from their own wallet, reconciled on Tempo in under a second.

Telegram-first self-custody card (Rain-issued); settles on Tempo; no US.

Common questions about Karta

What is Karta?

Karta is a self-custodial, on-chain-first neobank built for travel & digital nomads, headquartered in Redwood City, US and founded in 2020. You hold the keys: Karta never takes custody of your funds. It is software rather than a licensed institution (Self-custodial (MPC via Privy); Visa issued by Rain).

Does Karta have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa virtual and physical card, and identity checks apply to the card but not to the wallet. It is available in all 7 world regions tracked here.

Does Karta require KYC?

Partly. You can use Karta itself without verifying your identity, but ordering its card requires KYC.

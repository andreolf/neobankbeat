Deblock

web3-native · Paris, FR · est. 2022 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerDeblock is a self-custodial, on-chain-first neobank, headquartered in Paris, FR and founded in 2022. You hold the keys: Deblock never takes custody of your funds. It is regulated as a payment institution (PSAN + payment institution (ACPR)). It issues a Visa virtual and physical card. It is available in 2 countries, across Europe.

A French-licensed current account where the crypto side stays in your own non-custodial wallet — the clearest mixed-custody design in Europe.

Common questions about Deblock

What is Deblock?

Deblock is a self-custodial, on-chain-first neobank, headquartered in Paris, FR and founded in 2022. You hold the keys: Deblock never takes custody of your funds. It is regulated as a payment institution (PSAN + payment institution (ACPR)).

Does Deblock have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa virtual and physical card. It is available in 2 countries, across Europe.

Does Deblock require KYC?

Yes. Deblock requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a payment institution.

What does Deblock cost?

From the verified fields: cashback Tiers; yield ~4% EUR (tiers). Cashback and yield are "up to" figures that change constantly and often depend on a paid plan — confirm current terms with Deblock before relying on them.

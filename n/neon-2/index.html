Common questions about neon

What is neon?

neon is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Zurich, CH and founded in 2017. Your money is held custodially, so neon or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (Partner: Hypothekarbank Lenzburg).

Does neon have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Mastercard debit card. It is available in Europe.

Does neon require KYC?

Yes. neon requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a partner-bank model.

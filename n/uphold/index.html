Uphold

hybrid · New York, US · est. 2015 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español →

short answerUphold is a fiat-and-crypto neobank, headquartered in New York, US and founded in 2015. Your money is held custodially, so Uphold or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It holds VASP / MSB / crypto licenses (FinCEN MSB + state money-transmitter licenses (US); FCA-registered cryptoasset firm (UK)). It issues a Visa and Mastercard debit card. It is available in 6 countries, across all 7 world regions tracked here.

Multi-asset: crypto, metals, FX.

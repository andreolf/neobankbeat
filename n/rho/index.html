Rho

traditional · New York, US · est. 2018 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerRho is a fiat neobank built for SMB & startups, headquartered in New York, US and founded in 2018. Your money is held custodially, so Rho or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (Partner bank (Webster Bank, N.A.); savings swept across 400+ FDIC banks via ADM). It issues a Mastercard corporate card. It is available in North America.

Common questions about Rho

What is Rho?

Rho is a fiat neobank built for SMB & startups, headquartered in New York, US and founded in 2018. Your money is held custodially, so Rho or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (Partner bank (Webster Bank, N.A.); savings swept across 400+ FDIC banks via ADM).

Does Rho have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Mastercard corporate card. It is available in North America.

Does Rho require KYC?

Yes. Rho requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a partner-bank model.

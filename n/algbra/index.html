short answerAlgbra is a fiat neobank built for faith-based, headquartered in London, UK and founded in 2020. Your money is held custodially, so Algbra or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as an e-money institution (E-money institution (FCA, FRN 952360, UK)). It issues a Mastercard debit card. It is available in Europe.

Pride Bank

traditional · São Paulo, BR · est. 2019 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerPride Bank is a fiat neobank built for LGBTQ+, headquartered in São Paulo, BR and founded in 2019. Your money is held custodially, so Pride Bank or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (Not a bank; payment arrangement on Digital Banks' rails (BaaS, Brazil)). It issues a Mastercard debit card. It is available in Latin America.

Brazil's digital bank for the LGBTQ+ community, born from exclusion at traditional branches.

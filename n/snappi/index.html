Common questions about Snappi

What is Snappi?

Snappi is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Athens, GR and founded in 2024. Your money is held custodially, so Snappi or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (Full ECB banking license (via Bank of Greece, Jun 2024); Piraeus-backed digital bank).

Does Snappi have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa debit card. It is available in Europe.

Does Snappi require KYC?

Yes. Snappi requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a licensed bank.

Munify

traditional · Cairo, EG · est. 2024 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerMunify is a fiat neobank built for immigrants & migrants, headquartered in Cairo, EG and founded in 2024. Your money is held custodially, so Munify or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (US accounts via partner banks; in-principle DFSA license approval (UAE, Nov 2025)). It issues a Mastercard debit card. It is available in MENA.

Common questions about Munify

What is Munify?

Munify is a fiat neobank built for immigrants & migrants, headquartered in Cairo, EG and founded in 2024. Your money is held custodially, so Munify or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (US accounts via partner banks; in-principle DFSA license approval (UAE, Nov 2025)).

It issues a Mastercard debit card. It is available in MENA.

Does Munify require KYC?

Yes. Munify requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a partner-bank model.

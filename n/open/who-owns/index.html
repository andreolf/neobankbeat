Open profile

Who owns Open?

traditional · Bengaluru, IN · Partner-bank model · custody: Custodial

short answerOpen is not itself a bank. It runs on a partner-bank (BaaS) model, where a licensed sponsor bank holds the deposits behind the app while Open builds the product and interface. Open's most notable disclosed backers include Temasek, Tiger Global, 3one4 Capital and IIFL.

Is Open a licensed bank?

No — it runs on a sponsor bank (BaaS model). License detail: Partner banks (ICICI, Axis, Yes Bank +); RBI-licensed payment aggregator.

Where is your money held?

Custody model: Custodial.

Open was founded by Anish Achuthan + 3 (2017), headquartered in Bengaluru, IN.

Is my money safe with Open?

Deposits are held by Open's sponsor bank, so any insurance flows through that bank rather than Open. Check which sponsor holds your balance and whether it is passed through for per-customer coverage.

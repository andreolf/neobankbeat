Common questions about Tandem Bank

What is Tandem Bank?

Tandem Bank is a fiat neobank built for climate, headquartered in London, UK and founded in 2013. Your money is held custodially, so Tandem Bank or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (UK banking license).

Does Tandem Bank have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues no card of its own. It is available in Europe.

Does Tandem Bank require KYC?

Yes. Tandem Bank requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a licensed bank.

eToro Money

hybrid · Tel Aviv, IL · est. 2007 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answereToro Money is a fiat-and-crypto neobank, headquartered in Tel Aviv, IL and founded in 2007. Your money is held custodially, so eToro Money or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as an e-money institution (Broker-led; e-money institution (eToro Money UK, FCA FRN 900203; eToro Money Malta, MFSA)). It issues a Visa debit card. It is available in Europe.

Common questions about eToro Money

What is eToro Money?

eToro Money is a fiat-and-crypto neobank, headquartered in Tel Aviv, IL and founded in 2007. Your money is held custodially, so eToro Money or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as an e-money institution (Broker-led; e-money institution (eToro Money UK, FCA FRN 900203; eToro Money Malta, MFSA)).

Does eToro Money have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa debit card. It is available in Europe.

Does eToro Money require KYC?

Yes. eToro Money requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a e-money institution.

What does eToro Money cost?

From the verified fields: FX abroad — 0% FX fee; interbank rate, no markup on supported currencies (UK/EU) (as of 2026-07); yield Staking. Cashback and yield are "up to" figures that change constantly and often depend on a paid plan — confirm current terms with eToro Money before relying on them.

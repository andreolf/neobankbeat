Common questions about Bank Zero

What is Bank Zero?

Bank Zero is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Johannesburg, ZA and founded in 2018. Your money is held custodially, so Bank Zero or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a licensed bank (Mutual bank license (SA)).

Does Bank Zero have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Mastercard debit card. It is available in Africa.

Does Bank Zero require KYC?

Yes. Bank Zero requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a licensed bank.

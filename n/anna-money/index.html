Common questions about ANNA Money

What is ANNA Money?

ANNA Money is a fiat neobank built for SMB & startups, headquartered in Cardiff, UK and founded in 2017. Your money is held custodially, so ANNA Money or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as an e-money institution (E-money).

Does ANNA Money have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Mastercard business debit card. It is available in 2 countries, across Europe.

Does ANNA Money require KYC?

Yes. ANNA Money requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a e-money institution.

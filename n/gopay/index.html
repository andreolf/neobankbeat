GoPay

traditional · Jakarta, ID · est. 2016 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerGoPay is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Jakarta, ID and founded in 2016. Your money is held custodially, so GoPay or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as an e-money institution (E-money issuer licensed by Bank Indonesia (PT Dompet Anak Bangsa, since 2014); PJP Category 1; Bank Jago partner). It issues no card of its own. It is available in Asia.

Common questions about GoPay

What is GoPay?

GoPay is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Jakarta, ID and founded in 2016. Your money is held custodially, so GoPay or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as an e-money institution (E-money issuer licensed by Bank Indonesia (PT Dompet Anak Bangsa, since 2014); PJP Category 1; Bank Jago partner).

Does GoPay have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues no card of its own. It is available in Asia.

Does GoPay require KYC?

Yes. GoPay requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a e-money institution.

Common questions about KuCard

What is KuCard?

KuCard is a fiat-and-crypto neobank, founded in 2017. Your money is held custodially, so KuCard or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a MiCA CASP (EU) (Card issued by Wallester AS (Estonian payment institution); KuCoin EU holds Austrian MiCA CASP license).

Does KuCard have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa debit card. It is available in all 7 world regions tracked here.

Does KuCard require KYC?

Yes. KuCard requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a mica casp (eu).

What does KuCard cost?

From the verified fields: cashback Rewards; yield Earn. Cashback and yield are "up to" figures that change constantly and often depend on a paid plan — confirm current terms with KuCard before relying on them.

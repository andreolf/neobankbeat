Common questions about Strike

What is Strike?

Strike is a fiat-and-crypto neobank, headquartered in Chicago, US and founded in 2019. Your money is held custodially, so Strike or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It holds VASP / MSB / crypto licenses (Money transmitter licenses + NY BitLicense (NYDFS, 2026); Zap Solutions, Inc.).

Does Strike have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa debit (us) card. It is available in 9 countries, across all 7 world regions tracked here.

Does Strike require KYC?

Yes. Strike requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a vasp / msb / crypto licenses.

What does Strike cost?

From the verified fields: cashback BTC rewards. Cashback and yield are "up to" figures that change constantly and often depend on a paid plan — confirm current terms with Strike before relying on them.

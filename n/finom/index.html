Finom

traditional · Amsterdam, NL · est. 2019 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerFinom is a fiat neobank built for SMB & startups, headquartered in Amsterdam, NL and founded in 2019. Your money is held custodially, so Finom or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as an e-money institution (E-money institution (FINOM Payments B.V., DNB Netherlands); EU/EEA passporting). It issues a Visa business debit card. It is available in Europe.

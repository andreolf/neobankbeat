short answerReady is a self-custodial, on-chain-first neobank, headquartered in London, UK and founded in 2018. You hold the keys: Ready never takes custody of your funds. It is software rather than a licensed institution (Self-custodial software; card via Kulipa (non-EEA cut Jun 2026; issuer wound down Jul 2026)). It issues a Visa metal and virtual card, and identity checks apply to the card but not to the wallet. It is available in 3 countries, across all 7 world regions tracked here.

Argent pioneered smart-account wallets and social recovery; rebranded to Ready to become an onchain bank.

Formerly Argent; card suspended Jul 2026 — issuer Kulipa wound down.

Common questions about Ready

What is Ready?

Ready is a self-custodial, on-chain-first neobank, headquartered in London, UK and founded in 2018. You hold the keys: Ready never takes custody of your funds. It is software rather than a licensed institution (Self-custodial software; card via Kulipa (non-EEA cut Jun 2026; issuer wound down Jul 2026)).

Does Ready have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa metal and virtual card, and identity checks apply to the card but not to the wallet. It is available in 3 countries, across all 7 world regions tracked here.

Does Ready require KYC?

Partly. You can use Ready itself without verifying your identity, but ordering its card requires KYC.

What does Ready cost?

From the verified fields: cashback 3% in STRK; yield Staking + BTCFi. Cashback and yield are "up to" figures that change constantly and often depend on a paid plan — confirm current terms with Ready before relying on them.

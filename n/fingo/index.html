short answerFingo is a fiat neobank built for gen z & students, headquartered in Nairobi, KE and founded in 2021. Common questions about Fingo

What is Fingo?

Fingo is a fiat neobank built for gen z & students, headquartered in Nairobi, KE and founded in 2021. Your money is held custodially, so Fingo or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (Partner bank (Ecobank Kenya); Central Bank of Kenya-approved product).

Does Fingo have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues no card of its own. It is available in Africa.

Does Fingo require KYC?

Yes. Fingo requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a partner-bank model.

Kontigo

web3-native · — · est. 2024 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerKontigo is a self-custodial, on-chain-first neobank, founded in 2024. Your money is held custodially, so Kontigo or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (Self-custodial MPC wallet (USDC); no license of its own; fiat rails via partners). It issues a Visa virtual and physical card. It is available in 3 countries, across Latin America.

Ex-Venmo/Nubank/Rappi team hit $30M annualized revenue, $1B payment volume and 1M users in under 12 months — with six engineers and one designer.

USDC neobank for Venezuela + LatAm corridors.

Common questions about Kontigo

What is Kontigo?

Kontigo is a self-custodial, on-chain-first neobank, founded in 2024. Your money is held custodially, so Kontigo or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (Self-custodial MPC wallet (USDC); no license of its own; fiat rails via partners).

Does Kontigo have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues a Visa virtual and physical card. It is available in 3 countries, across Latin America.

Does Kontigo require KYC?

Yes. Kontigo requires identity verification (KYC) to open an account, as a partner-bank model.

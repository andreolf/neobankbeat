Common questions about Eco (Beam)

What is Eco (Beam)?

Eco (Beam) is a self-custodial, on-chain-first neobank, headquartered in New York, US and founded in 2023. You hold the keys: Eco (Beam) never takes custody of your funds. It is software rather than a licensed institution (Self-custodial (Eco protocol)).

Does Eco (Beam) have a card, and where can you use it?

It issues no card of its own and requires no identity check. It is available in North America.

Does Eco (Beam) require KYC?

No. Eco (Beam) works without identity verification, which is possible because it is self-custodial — it never holds your funds.

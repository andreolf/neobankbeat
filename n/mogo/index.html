Mogo

hybrid · Vancouver, CA · est. 2003 · open in the directory →

short answerMogo is a fiat-and-crypto neobank, headquartered in Vancouver, CA and founded in 2003. Your money is held custodially, so Mogo or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It holds VASP / MSB / crypto licences (Cards issued by Peoples Trust Company; MogoTrade is a CIRO-regulated broker (Canada)). It issues a Visa prepaid card. It is available in North America.

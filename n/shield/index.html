Shield

hybrid · Miami, US · est. 2022 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerShield is a fiat-and-crypto neobank built for SMB & startups, headquartered in Miami, US and founded in 2022. Your money is held custodially, so Shield or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (Partner banks (Cross River Bank, Lead Bank) via licensed partners incl. Bridge (NMLS 2450917); FinCEN-registered MSB; card issued by First Internet Bank of Indiana via CapitalOS). It issues a Visa business credit (pay-in-full) card. It is available in North America.

Built for US exporters whose overseas buyers pay in USDT: the stablecoin arrives, the business gets same-day USD.

US business accounts for exporters and wholesalers: accept USDT from overseas buyers, settle same-day in USD.

The ledger
Split between a program manager and the sponsor bank — the modular setup that broke in Synapse.
If it fails
Any claim runs through the sponsor bank, and only works if the two ledgers reconcile.

Common questions about Shield

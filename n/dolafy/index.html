Dolafy

hybrid · Brazil, BR · est. 2025 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerDolafy is a fiat-and-crypto neobank built for SMB & startups, headquartered in Brazil, BR and founded in 2025. Your money is held custodially, so Dolafy or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It operates on a partner-bank model (Partner bank: Lead Bank (issuer); Bridge, a Stripe company (program manager)). It issues a Visa prepaid card. It is available in Latin America, North America, Europe and MENA.

Bootstrapped by Eduardo Borges and two co-founders under AETERNUM CAPITAL LTDA, with no outside investors.

Multi-currency (USD/EUR/BRL/AED/GBP) accounts for global businesses with custodial stablecoins and a Visa prepaid business card.

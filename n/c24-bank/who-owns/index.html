C24 Bank profile

Who owns C24 Bank?

traditional · Frankfurt, DE · Partner-bank model · custody: Custodial

short answerC24 Bank is not itself a bank. It runs on a partner-bank (BaaS) model, where a licensed sponsor bank holds the deposits behind the app while C24 Bank builds the product and interface.

Is C24 Bank a licensed bank?

No — it runs on a sponsor bank (BaaS model). License detail: Full German banking license (BaFin); CHECK24 subsidiary.

Where is your money held?

Custody model: Custodial.

No sponsor bank or infrastructure provider is publicly documented for C24 Bank yet. Know one? Suggest it.

Is my money safe with C24 Bank?

Deposits are held by C24 Bank's sponsor bank, so any insurance flows through that bank rather than C24 Bank. Check which sponsor holds your balance and whether it is passed through for per-customer coverage.

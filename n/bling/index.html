Bling

short answerBling is a fiat neobank built for gen alpha · kids & family, headquartered in Berlin, DE and founded in 2021. Your money is held custodially, so Bling or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as an e-money institution (E-money distributor of Treezor (ACPR-licensed French EMI, Société Générale group)). It issues a Visa debit card. It is available in Europe.

Common questions about Bling

What is Bling?

Bling is a fiat neobank built for gen alpha · kids & family, headquartered in Berlin, DE and founded in 2021. Your money is held custodially, so Bling or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as an e-money institution (E-money distributor of Treezor (ACPR-licensed French EMI, Société Générale group)).

Does Bling require KYC?

Mercado Pago

hybrid · Buenos Aires, AR · est. 2003 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerMercado Pago is a fiat-and-crypto neobank, headquartered in Buenos Aires, AR and founded in 2003. Your money is held custodially, so Mercado Pago or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. Its license is pending, so it runs on a partner-bank model today (Payment/fintech licenses region-wide; pursuing MX bank license). It issues a Visa and Mastercard debit and credit card. It is available in 7 countries, across Latin America.

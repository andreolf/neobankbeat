ZaloPay

traditional · Ho Chi Minh City, VN · est. 2016 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerZaloPay is a fiat neobank, headquartered in Ho Chi Minh City, VN and founded in 2016. Your money is held custodially, so ZaloPay or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a payment institution (Licensed payment institution (SBV intermediary payment services license 04/GP-NHNN); Zion/VNG e-wallet). It issues a Visa wallet and card. It is available in Asia.

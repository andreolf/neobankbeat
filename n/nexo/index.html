Nexo

hybrid · Zug, CH · est. 2018 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerNexo is a fiat-and-crypto neobank, headquartered in Zug, CH and founded in 2018. Your money is held custodially, so Nexo or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. It is regulated as a MiCA CASP (EU) (EU VASP registrations; MiCA CASP application pending; EEA served via MiCA-licensed Tangany + DLT Finance). It issues a Visa and Mastercard dual credit and debit card. It is available in 5 countries, across all 7 world regions tracked here.

Crypto credit lines; rewards in credit mode.

Khazna

traditional · Cairo, EG · est. 2020 · open in the directory → · Deutsch → · Italiano → · Français → · Español → · Português →

short answerKhazna is a fiat neobank built for underbanked, headquartered in Cairo, EG and founded in 2020. Your money is held custodially, so Khazna or its partner bank holds the balance and you hold a claim on it. Its license is pending, so it runs on a partner-bank model today (Pursuing Central Bank of Egypt digital-bank license (target mid-2026); lends via FRA-regulated entities). It issues no card of its own. It is available in Africa.

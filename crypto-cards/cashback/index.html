Crypto cards with cashback

71 of 397 tracked neobanks · verified from public filings and product docs · updated 2026-11-15

short answer71 of the 107 crypto cards tracked here publish a cashback rate. Rewards are usually paid in the issuer’s own token or points, and the rate shown is an upper bound. The largest by disclosed users are Mercado Pago, Cash App, PicPay, Revolut and Maya; all 71 are listed below with custody, network, cashback, yield and KYC.

Cashback on a crypto card is rarely cash. It is usually paid in the issuer’s own token or in points, often only on a paid tier or above a staking threshold, and the headline rate is an upper bound. The rate shown for each card is the issuer’s "up to" figure as published; what a typical user earns is lower, and a reward paid in a volatile token can be worth less by the time it is spent.

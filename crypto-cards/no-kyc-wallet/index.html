Crypto cards on wallets that need no KYC

31 of 397 tracked neobanks · verified from public filings and product docs · updated 2026-11-16

short answer31 of the 107 crypto cards tracked here come from a wallet that needs no KYC. The wallet works without verification; ordering the card still requires an identity check by the issuing partner. They include 1inch Card, Avalanche Card, Avici, Bitget Wallet and Bleap; all 31 are listed below with custody, network, cashback, yield and KYC.

There is no mainstream card you can order with no identity check: the card is issued on Visa or Mastercard by a regulated partner, and that partner has to verify the cardholder. What these products offer is narrower and still useful. The wallet itself works without verification, so you can hold, receive and send on-chain freely, and you verify once, only if you want the card.

Self-custodial crypto cards

38 of 397 tracked neobanks · verified from public filings and product docs · updated 2026-11-15

short answer38 of the 107 crypto cards tracked here are self-custodial. You hold the keys until the moment of payment, so the issuer cannot freeze or lend the balance. They include 1inch Card, Avalanche Card, Avici, Bitget Wallet and Bleap; all 38 are listed below with custody, network, cashback, yield and KYC.

On these cards the crypto stays in a wallet whose keys you hold until the moment you pay. The issuer cannot freeze or lend the balance, and if the company fails your funds are not in its estate. The cost is that recovery is yours too: lose the keys and nobody can restore them. Several settle each purchase from a smart account, so the card works like a spending permission on your own wallet rather than a prepaid balance.

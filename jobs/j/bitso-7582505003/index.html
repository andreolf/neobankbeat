Working At Bitso We are a diverse team that takes pride in understanding the perspectives of others. We fully embrace working remotely and we are eager to act, improve and accelerate progress inside and outside of our organization. To drive revolutionary changes in society and make crypto useful, we delight our customers with world-class products, deep care, and intentional empathy. Your Purpose We are looking for an experienced technical lead to join the Crypto Revolution. In this crucial role, you will manage engineers and other leads, helping them grow while we build scalable, secure, and high-quality products with our users as the center.

You will work closely with product, design, and business stakeholders to define the strategy, set priorities, and align on roadmaps. Your ultimate goal will be to deliver excellent products to our customers, prioritizing security, compliance, user experience, and reliability in a high-volume trading environment. This role requires deep technical and architectural understanding, with a solid previous background as a developer. While day-to-day coding is not expected, you will need to provide hands-on technical guidance and support your teams in complex situations.

Who You Are

  • Passionate about the crypto ecosystem.
  • 6+ years of experience managing engineering teams, with at least 2+ years managing managers.
  • Strong background in trading or investment platforms.
  • Proven ability to scale engineering teams,optimize delivery processes, and handle high-throughput, mission-critical systems. .
  • Deep technical expertise in software engineering, architecture, and team building.
  • Excellent oral and written communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively across functions, geographies, and remote/distributed teams..
  • Familiarity with observability and monitoring tools (e.g., Splunk, Datadog).
  • Experience working with Git and automated CI/CD tools.
  • Strong ability to coordinate sizeable multidisciplinary product engineering squads.
  • Knows how to motivate teams through practical challenges and foster psychological safety..
  • Experience building and launching large-scale user-facing products to market.
  • Comfortable with modern delivery practices, including DevOps principles.

  • Crypto and trading knowledge
  • You know your way around Kubernetes, GRPC,REST, microservices, and cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, GCP)

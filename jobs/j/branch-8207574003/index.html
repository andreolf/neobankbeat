the role

Branch's fraud and disputes customers now reach us around the clock. Our BPO partner handles inbound phone and chat 24/7 for fraud claims, dispute intake, account unlocks, and account takeover (ATO) contacts, backed by a seven-day back office. That front office is where most customers first tell us something went wrong, and where a bad actor is most likely to try to talk their way into an account. As Risk and Recovery Contact Operations Manager, you own the inbound phone and chat operation for Risk Recovery. You know its health hour by hour, you are accountable for its performance, and when someone asks how the 24/7 operation is running, you are the answer. The BPO executes the contacts. You own the procedures, the quality bar, the coverage, and the outcomes, regardless of who picks up the phone. Enterprise Systems owns the BPO contract, renewals, and contractual SLAs; you own the day-to-day operation.

This is a hands-on individual contributor role with real ownership. It is a focused version of a full BPO support manager role: one vendor, Risk Recovery lines of business only, no internal team to manage. It suits someone with BPO contact center experience who also understands fraud, disputes, or risk, and who wants to own an operation rather than a queue.

Responsibilities

Run the 24/7 Office

  • Own the daily health of R R inbound phone and chat: volume, speed to answer, abandonment, handle time, first-contact resolution, transfers, and misroutes
  • Lead a daily stand-up with BPO operations leads to review prior-day performance and set the day's priorities
  • Watch intraday coverage and act on gaps before they become a backlog or a missed SLA
  • Drive resolution on the call. The initial target is resolving 50% of fraud contacts on the phone, without a hand-off to the back office

Staffing/Coverage

  • Supply the R R inputs the staffing model depends on: contact volume by line of business, work mix, handle-time assumptions, and arrival patterns
  • Partner hands-on with Support's BPO Manager on forecasting and scheduling in Zendesk WFM; you own whether R R coverage is right, they own the WFM tooling and the CX relationship
  • Hold the BPO to its staffing commitments, including the overnight floor and billable FTE minimums; confirm delivered hours against schedules before invoices go to the Director for approval

Contact Procedures Ownership

  • Own the BPO-facing phone and chat procedures for R R: call flows, scripts, and macros. Build the identity verification controls set by the Manager of Risk Controls into those call flows, and report where they break down in practice
  • Build them on the domain SOPs owned by the Managers of Risk and Recovery Program Operations; domain SOPs stay with their owners
  • Translate daily friction on the phones into specific, solvable problems for the right Manager of Risk and Recovery Program Operations, Product, or automation partner

Training Quality

  • Own R R new-hire and refresher training for BPO front-office agents, built on current procedures
  • Run the day-to-day QA operation for BPO calls and chats, including scoring, sampling and calibration sessions, against the QA criteria set by the R R QA owners and approved by the Operations Manager
  • Own the QA outcome: make sure feedback is coached, applied and visible in live contacts, hold agents accountable, and issue corrective action plans to the BPO, tracked to closure. Share what QA and the floor are showing with the R R QA owners, who own trend analysis and the process changes that follow

Operational Review Reporting

  • Serve as the day-to-day point of contact for the BPO on R R contact operations
  • Run weekly operational reviews with BPO leadership and provide the front-office performance input for monthly and quarterly business reviews
  • Be the first escalation point for front-office performance; resolve at your level or escalate with a recommendation attached
  • Give Enterprise Systems the performance data it needs for contractual SLA oversight, service credits, and renewals. Route contractual and non-user-facing vendor issues to Enterprise Systems; own everything that affects users day to day
  • Lead the operational side of transitioning new contact work to the BPO, with procedures, training, and escalation paths in place before go-live
  • Publish a weekly front-office scorecard: coverage, speed to answer, abandonment, handle time, resolution on the call, quality, and escalations
  • Surface trends and risks before they appear in a management report

What We're Looking For

Required

  • 4–6 years in contact center operations, including at least 2 years working directly with a BPO or offshore partner
  • 2+ years in fraud, disputes, chargebacks, payments, or risk operations at a financial institution, fintech, or payments processor, or similar work experience
  • Hands-on experience with workforce management: forecasting inputs, scheduling, intraday management, and adherence
  • Experience building QA programs, calibrations, and agent training
  • Strong analytical skills; comfortable living in contact center data
  • Strong writing and communication skills; able to hold a vendor accountable and keep the relationship productive
  • Comfort operating with accountability but without formal authority
  • Because the operation runs 24/7, you may occasionally need to attend meetings outside standard hours to align with BPO shift leaders. The role may require periodic travel to BPO sites.

Nice to Have

  • Experience with Zendesk, Zendesk WFM, and Aircall, or a comparable contact center stack
  • Experience running a 24/7 or multi-site operation
  • Working knowledge of Reg E error resolution and identity verification practices
  • Experience transitioning work from an internal team to a BPO
  • Experience in a high-growth fintech environment
  • Multi-lingual (Spanish) is a plus

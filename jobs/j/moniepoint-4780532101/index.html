Team Lead, System Administrator

Lagos, NigeriaPeople & LegalHybridposted 2d ago

Who we are Moniepoint is an all-in-one financial services platform for emerging markets and the second-fastest-growing company in Africa. Since 2019, Moniepoint’s technology has powered over 3 million people, offering personal and business banking, payment, credit and business management tools to help them succeed. Moniepoint processed $182 billion in 2023, and currently processes the majority of the POS transactions in Nigeria. Curious about what makes Moniepoint an incredible place to work? Check out posts on how we cultivate a culture of innovation, teamwork, and growth.

About the Role

The Team Lead, Systems Administration, is a senior individual contributor who also leads the IT infrastructure team tactically. This role is responsible for both the hands-on delivery of complex systems work and the orchestration of team priorities, standards, and output quality. The ideal candidate combines deep technical expertise in networking, cloud infrastructure, security, and automation with the leadership capability to coach a team, enforce SOPs, and drive measurable operational excellence, including leveraging AI tools to optimise how the team works.

This role sits at the Team Lead level in Moniepoint's Talent Architecture. Success is defined not only by personal technical output, but by the team's consistent performance and delivery against infrastructure-wide targets. Scope Decision Horizon Leads the IT infrastructure team tactically. Success is the orchestration of people and priorities to ensure the team's immediate output aligns with the broader roadmap. Principal Duties and Responsibilities Team Leadership Operations

  • Lead, coordinate, and coach a team of Systems Administrators, ensuring consistent output quality and adherence to SOPs.
  • Manage team workload, prioritise tasks across competing demands, and ensure SLA compliance across all infrastructure functions.
  • Conduct regular 1:1s, deliver direct and specific performance feedback, and actively develop team members' technical skills.
  • Design and enforce team workflows and escalation paths, ensuring quality is consistent regardless of who executes the task.
  • Own team-level metrics: system uptime (99.9%+), ticket SLAs, incident response times, and end-user satisfaction.
  • Identify gaps in team capability or process and proactively close them through training, tooling, or workflow redesign.

Infrastructure Management

  • Design, implement, and maintain a robust IT infrastructure, including servers and virtualisation platforms (Proxmox).
  • Administer and optimise Windows Server, Linux systems, and hybrid cloud infrastructures.
  • Maintain comprehensive system documentation, network diagrams, and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
  • Plan and execute system upgrades with minimal downtime; optimise for performance, reliability, and cost efficiency.

Cloud Network Infrastructure

  • Design, deploy, and manage cloud network environments across GCP, OCI, and Azure, Outpost, including VPCs, subnets, peering, firewall rules, and private connectivity.
  • Administer and optimise BGP routing policies, virtual routers (vRTF), and F5 load balancers across the enterprise network and cloud environments.
  • Configure, manage, and troubleshoot enterprise networking infrastructure (routers, switches, firewalls, wireless access points).
  • Implement and maintain network security protocols, VLANs, VPNs, and network segmentation across on-premise and cloud environments.
  • Manage DNS, DHCP, Directory as a Service, and other core network services.
  • Monitor network performance, analyse bandwidth utilisation, and implement QoS policies.

AI Tools Workflow Automation

  • Manage, maintain, and evaluate AI tools used across the IT and infrastructure function, ensuring reliability, access control, and appropriate use.
  • Build and maintain automation workflows using n8n and similar platforms to optimise repetitive operational processes.
  • Design and implement AI-assisted workflows for network deployment, incident triage, and infrastructure monitoring.
  • Coach team members on responsible AI integration, when to use AI tools, how to validate outputs, and where human review is required.
  • Identify opportunities to reduce manual toil through intelligent automation and continuously improve the team's automation capabilities.

Telephony Unified Communications

  • Administer and maintain the xCALLY PBX platform, including call routing, IVR configuration, queue management, and agent provisioning.
  • Troubleshoot telephony incidents end-to-end: SIP trunks, PBX dial plans, voice quality, and carrier integration.
  • Coordinate with ISPs and telecom carriers to ensure uptime and quality of voice services across all sites.

Automation Scripting

  • Develop and maintain automation scripts (Python, PowerShell, Bash) to eliminate repetitive tasks and improve operational efficiency.
  • Utilise Infrastructure as Code tools (Terraform, Ansible) for consistent and scalable infrastructure deployment.
  • Build and maintain monitoring and alerting solutions using Prometheus, Grafana, and Nagios.
  • Document automation and tooling clearly enough for an Associate-level team member to execute independently.

Security Compliance

  • Implement and enforce IT security policies, access controls, and data protection measures across on-premise and cloud environments.
  • Manage endpoint security solutions, patch management, and privileged access management.
  • Manage user authentication systems, including SSO, MFA, and identity governance.
  • Lead incident response: manage security events, conduct Root Cause Analyses (RCA), and implement permanent fixes.

User Endpoint Technical Support

  • Oversee onboarding and offboarding procedures, ensuring seamless first-day experiences and complete account lifecycle management.
  • Administer Google Workspace, including user provisioning, group management, Drive policies, and app integrations.
  • Manage MDM solutions for corporate devices across Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android.
  • Provide or oversee expert-level L2/L3 support, ensuring timely resolution and minimal user disruption.
  • Manage IT asset inventory, lifecycle, and procurement coordination.

Qualifications, Competencies Skills Education Experience

  • Bachelor's degree or diploma in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field.
  • 5+ years of proven experience in systems administration, infrastructure, or network engineering roles.
  • At least 2 years of formal or informal team leadership, including coaching, delegation, and performance feedback.
  • Relevant industry certifications (CompTIA, Cisco, Microsoft, GCP/Azure/OCI cloud, Linux) are highly desirable, but not compulsory.

Technical Skills

  • Advanced proficiency in Active Directory / Directory as a Service (e.g., JumpCloud); Linux/Unix administration (RHEL, CentOS, Ubuntu, Debian).
  • Cloud networking expertise across GCP, OCI, and Azure: VPCs, subnets, load balancers, private service connect, VPN gateways, and cloud firewall management.
  • Deep understanding of TCP/IP, BGP routing protocols, switching, VLANs, and enterprise network architecture across data centre and WAN environments.
  • Hands-on experience with virtual routers (vRTF), F5 load balancers, and enterprise networking equipment (Cisco, Ubiquiti).
  • Virtualisation experience with Proxmox; data centre operations including rack and stack, power, cooling, and structured cabling.
  • Experience administering xCALLY PBX or equivalent enterprise telephony/unified communications platforms.
  • Proficiency in scripting: Python, PowerShell, Bash; experience with Ansible and Terraform.
  • Hands-on experience with n8n or equivalent workflow automation platforms for building and managing operational automations.
  • Experience managing AI tools within an IT environment, including access control, integration, and responsible use governance.
  • Experience with monitoring tools (Grafana, Prometheus, Nagios, PRTG) and endpoint/MDM management platforms.
  • Proficiency in Google Workspace administration: user and group management, Drive, Gmail, Meet, and third-party app integrations.
  • Advanced infosec tooling experience (e.g., CrowdStrike); knowledge of Disaster Recovery protocols and DR drills.
  • Experience with Jira Service Management; understanding of SLIs, SLOs, and SLAs.

Leadership Soft Skills

  • Proven ability to lead a technical team: delegate effectively, deliver feedback, and develop others.
  • Exceptional analytical and systematic troubleshooting approach; moves fast without compromising rigour.
  • Strong documentation discipline — produces SOPs and runbooks clear enough for junior team members to execute.
  • Candid communicator: surfaces risks early, documents dissent, and challenges direction constructively.
  • Customer-obsessed: identifies and permanently resolves recurring pain points, not just individual tickets.

Working Conditions

  • This role may require occasional weekend or after-hours work to perform system maintenance, upgrades, and respond to critical incidents.
  • Physical requirements may include lifting equipment up to 50 lbs.

What we can offer you

  • Culture: We put our people first and prioritize the well-being of every team member. We’ve built a company where all opinions carry weight and where all voices are heard. We value and respect each other and always look out for one another. Above all, we are human.
  • Learning: We have a learning and development-focused environment with an emphasis on knowledge sharing, training, and regular internal technical talks.
  • Compensation: You’ll receive an attractive salary, pension, and health insurance.

What to expect in the hiring process

  • A technical interview with a member of the Infrastructure team.
  • A take-home assessment
  • A behavioral and technical interview with a member of the executive team.

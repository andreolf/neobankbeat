Senior Legal Counsel, Privacy, AI & Consumer Protection

MiamiData & AIHybrid

Apply on Nubank →

ABOUT NU Nu serves more than 143 million customers, guided by a mission to fight complexity and empower people. The company has been leading an industry transformation through innovative products and human-centered services. Proprietary technology and data at scale power Nu’s digital platform, built to promote financial access, advancement, and transparency. Its business model thrives on customer love and lower costs, feeding a flywheel of growth and profitability. Visit our Institutional Page https://www.nu.com/2026-en

ABOUT THE ROLE

Nu is building financial products for customers in the United States as part of a global digital banking platform. We are looking for a U.S.-based legal partner to help shape products and programs at the intersection of privacy, AI, and consumer protection. You will work closely with Product, Engineering, Data, Risk, Compliance, and Legal teams from early design through launch and ongoing operation. You will provide clear, practical advice, help strengthen privacy and AI governance across markets, and make complex legal requirements actionable—protecting customers while enabling responsible innovation.

This is a senior individual-contributor role with meaningful ownership and regular interaction with senior stakeholders. We welcome candidates with depth in one or more of the areas below and the curiosity to build breadth across the rest. What you'll be responsible for

  • Advise on products, privacy, and AI. Counsel product and technology teams on U.S. and global privacy, data use, and AI issues across the product lifecycle, including AI-enabled and agentic features, automated decisions, credit and servicing use cases, and payments. Turn legal requirements into clear product guidance, disclosures, and practical safeguards.
  • Lead privacy governance and program management. Work with the Global DPO to deliver the global privacy roadmap and own or co-own U.S. and global program pillars, including data mapping and RoPA, privacy risk controls and DPIAs/PIAs, data-subject-rights processes, training, and metrics. Simplify and automate workflows; support U.S. privacy-rights handling; improve privacy notices and in-product choices; assess third parties and cross-border data sharing and recommend proportionate safeguards; and coordinate privacy incident response, remediation, documentation, and regulatory readiness. Partner with Risk, Security, and Compliance on governance, providing legal interpretation and advice.
  • Counsel on consumer protection. Advise on U.S. consumer financial laws and customer outcomes across digital products and journeys. Help teams address fair lending, consumer information and reporting, customer disclosures and communications, adverse-action explanations, and automated customer interactions, including requirements such as ECOA/Regulation B, FCRA, UDAAP, and EFTA/Regulation E, as applicable.
  • Support new U.S. initiatives. Serve as an early legal partner for new products and business initiatives: spot issues, develop an initial legal position, and coordinate with relevant legal and control partners as the work evolves.
  • Work across teams and jurisdictions. Translate changing U.S. federal and state requirements into actionable advice and collaborate with global teams to support consistent governance across markets.

What we’re looking for

  • A law degree (J.D. or equivalent) and an active license to practice law in at least one U.S. jurisdiction, in the state where you practice.
  • Familiarity with U.S. and cross-border privacy and AI regulations, including state privacy laws, GDPR, and the EU AI Act, as well as consumer financial regulations such as ECOA/Regulation B, FCRA, UDAAP, EFTA/Regulation E, or TILA/Regulation Z.
  • Legal experience advising on one or more of the following: privacy and data protection, technology or AI-enabled products, or U.S. consumer financial services. This may come from an in-house team, a law firm serving technology or financial-services clients, a regulator, or a comparable setting.
  • Experience in fintech, banking, consumer technology, or a law firm advising companies in those sectors.
  • Exposure to AI governance, automated decision-making, agentic products, payments, or financial-services product counseling.
  • The ability to understand how a product or customer journey works, identify the material legal issues, and give clear, balanced recommendations to both legal and non-legal partners.
  • Sound judgment in ambiguous situations, strong written and verbal communication, and the ability to take ownership while influencing cross-functional teams.
  • Interest in building practical governance and processes, not only providing advice on individual questions.
  • Comfort in a lean, global and fast-paced environment, with ambiguity and with balancing lunch needs against long-term regulatory strategy.
  • Comfort using AI tools to draft, analyze and document, and with collaboration tools such as Jira, Confluence, Slack and Google Workspace.
  • Fluent written and spoken English, and the ability to explain complex legal concepts to non-lawyers in a clear, practical way.

Nice to have

  • Experience working across countries or with global teams;
  • Portuguese or Spanish
  • Relevant privacy certifications (e.g., CIPP/US, CIPP/E, CIPM, EXIN DPO) as a plus.

Intrinsic Motivation (Why)

  • Motivation to shape the frontier of digital regulation (privacy, AI, data, cyber) in a way that is both responsible and innovation‑friendly.
  • Willingness to work in a high‑ownership, high‑trust environment, taking accountability for outcomes and proactively surfacing risks, trade‑offs and opportunities.

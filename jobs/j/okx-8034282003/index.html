Head of Corporate Investigations

New York, United StatesOtherOn-site$240–361Kposted 13d ago

Who We Are At OKX, we believe that the future will be reshaped by crypto, and ultimately contribute to every individual's freedom. OKX is a leading crypto exchange, and the developer of OKX Wallet, giving millions access to crypto trading and decentralized crypto applications (dApps). OKX is also a trusted brand by hundreds of large institutions seeking access to crypto markets. We are safe and reliable, backed by our Proof of Reserves. Across our multiple offices globally, we are united by our core principles: We Before Me, Do the Right Thing, and Get Things Done. These shared values drive our culture, shape our processes, and foster a friendly, rewarding, and diverse environment for every OK-er.

About the Team

OKX's Legal team provides legal advice and guidance to the company across all coverage areas and geographic locations, and supports business growth and development by working with all teams within the company to help them achieve their goals. We are a high-functioning, collaborative and fast-paced group of lawyers who enjoy the cutting-edge nature of our work and the digital assets industry. Sitting in the Legal department, the Corporate Investigations team investigates fraud, misconduct and abuse committed against OKX. The team includes experienced investigators, among them former federal agents, and partners closely with the Compliance, Risk, Finance, and HR departments, colleagues in the Legal department, and business executives across OKX's global footprint.

About the Opportunity This is a senior, high-impact role for a former federal prosecutor who wants to build and lead a global investigations function. The Head of Corporate Investigations will be the single accountable owner for investigating wrongdoing against OKX and developing these matters for appropriate recovery and civil or criminal enforcement as warranted. As OKX's most senior investigations leader, you will set the standard for how the company uncovers and responds to wrongdoing, leading investigations that reflect the highest standards and best practices in the field. This is an opportunity to build a world-class investigations program, one whose rigor, independence and integrity protect OKX's people, assets and reputation

Where an investigation warrants it, you will be responsible for bringing matters to the appropriate authorities in the US, EMEA and APAC, ensuring each case is built to a prosecutorial standard and supported through to any civil or criminal resolution. We offer a competitive base salary and bonus structure as well as excellent benefits and an opportunity to work in a cutting-edge industry that will be the future of financial services. Extensive international travel on short notice expected in this role.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Lead the function and the team. Set the strategy, standards and methodology for internal investigations globally. Own intake, triage and prioritization. Manage and develop a team of investigators that includes former agents.
  • Run investigations. Direct investigations from allegations through findings, including interview strategy, subject and witness interviews, digital forensics and financial tracing. Personally lead the most complex and sensitive matters.
  • Build cases to a prosecutorial standard. Own OKX's framework for deciding which matters warrant escalation to authorities and in which jurisdiction. Build strong cases to the evidentiary and procedural standards of that jurisdiction, and partner with the Litigation team to pursue recovery of losses.
  • Manage engagement with authorities. Where matters are brought to the attention of authorities, oversee OKX's engagement through to resolution, including providing evidence, making witnesses available and responding to legal process, as appropriate.
  • Own law enforcement relationships. Build and maintain relationships and networks with law enforcement and prosecutors worldwide, including federal, state and local authorities in the US, EMEA and APAC.
  • Partner across the company. Work with Compliance, Risk, Finance, HR, colleagues in the Legal department, and business executives to investigate and resolve matters effectively.
  • Strengthen controls and report to leadership. Translate investigative findings into control improvements that prevent recurrence. Report significant matters and trends to senior leadership.

What We Look For In You

  • Prior service as an Assistant United States Attorney is required. Experience as a trial attorney in the DOJ Criminal Division, such as the Fraud Section, will also be considered.
  • A substantial record of investigating, charging and trying federal white-collar and fraud cases, including leading grand jury investigations and working closely with federal agents.
  • A deep understanding of what makes a case prosecutable and what prosecutors and police need before they will accept one.
  • In-house or law firm experience leading investigations required.
  • A JD and membership in a US state bar.
  • 15+ years of legal experience.
  • Experience leading and developing investigative teams, including former law enforcement professionals.
  • Sound, proportionate judgment, with a commitment to fairness and due process for the individuals under investigation.
  • Exceptional written and oral communication skills, with the ability to deliver clear findings to senior leadership and credible presentations to law enforcement.
  • Discretion, integrity and composure in sensitive, high-stakes matters.
  • A self-starter who is comfortable working independently in a fast-paced, growth-stage, multicultural environment and across global time zones.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively across functions and earn the trust of stakeholders at every level.

Nice to Haves

  • Experience in financial services or digital assets.
  • Exposure to cases involving cryptocurrency or blockchain tracing.
  • Experience engaging with foreign law enforcement and prosecutors, or with international cooperation mechanisms such as MLATs, particularly in Asia-Pacific.
  • Supervisory experience within a US Attorney's Office, such as a unit chief or deputy chief.
  • Global investigations experience.
  • Language capabilities: Mandarin

Perks Benefits

  • Competitive total compensation package
  • L D programs and Education subsidy for employees' growth and development
  • Various team building programs and company events
  • Wellness and meal allowances
  • Comprehensive healthcare schemes for employees and dependants
  • More that we love to tell you along the process!

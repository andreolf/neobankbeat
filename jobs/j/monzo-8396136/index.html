Staff Brand Designer

LondonDesignOn-site£98–114Kposted today

🚀 We’re on a mission to make money work for everyone. We’re waving goodbye to the complicated and confusing ways of traditional banking. After starting as a prepaid card, our product offering has grown a lot in the last 10 years in the UK. As well as personal and business bank accounts, we offer joint accounts, accounts for 16-17 year olds, a free kids account and credit cards in the UK, with more exciting things to come beyond. Our UK customers can also save, invest and combine their pensions with us.

With our hot coral cards and get-paid-early feature, combined with financial education on social media and our award winning customer service, we have a long history of creating magical moments for our customers! We’re not about selling products - we want to solve problems and change lives through Monzo ❤️ 📍 London | 💰 £98,000 - £114,000 + Incentive Awards tied to your performance | Benefits | Hear from the team ✨ ⭐ Our Design team We’re a team of 115+ designers, researchers and brand experts led by Chief Design Officer Vuokko Aro. We’re on a mission to make money work for everyone.

Our brand helps make Monzo recognisable and human, whether someone is opening the app, visiting our website or seeing a campaign for the first time. As we grow, there’s a big opportunity to evolve how our brand shows up and make those experiences feel connected. We’re looking for a Staff Brand Designer to help shape the future of Monzo’s visual identity, lead and develop designers, and set a high bar for creative work across the company. You’ll join our digital-first internal creative studio and work closely with our Global Creative Director, designers and writers. You’ll also partner with teams across Product Design and Marketing, as well as external agencies, to bring ambitious ideas to life.

This is for a designer who loves getting into the detail as much as shaping the bigger picture and helping others grow. You’ll bring strong conceptual thinking, art direction and typography to your work, and support a team of designers to do their best work too. 🔑 You’ll play a key role by…

  • Shaping our creative direction. Work with the Global Creative Director and partners across Monzo to evolve our visual identity and set a clear strategic direction for how the brand shows up globally.
  • Leading ambitious work. You’ll lead projects from idea to delivery and oversee work led by others, helping the team solve problems and deliver their best work. You’ll spot opportunities, create briefs and bring the right people together to make ideas happen. You’ll thrive working across multiple projects and teams at once.
  • Creating standout design. You’ll lead by example, setting a high bar for craft, concept and execution across the team. You’ll bring ideas to life across campaigns, marketing, product and other brand touch points, inspiring others through the quality of your work.
  • Managing and developing designers. Lead and support a small team of designers, helping them grow through regular feedback, coaching and career development conversations. Use Monzo’s levelling frameworks to support their progression and set clear expectations.
  • Raising the bar across the team. Lead critiques, share thoughtful feedback and mentor designers beyond your direct reports. Help create the conditions for people to do their best work.
  • Connecting brand and product. Partner with Product Design teams to bring Monzo’s brand to life in the app, including developing visual concepts, art direction and motion storyboards for customer experiences.
  • Evolving our brand systems. Develop guidelines, design principles and reusable assets that help teams create consistent, distinctive work as Monzo grows.
  • Influencing decisions across teams. Bring people together around a creative vision, explain the thinking behind your work and navigate trade-offs between customer needs, creative ambition and business goals.
  • Working with creative partners. Collaborate with external agencies and specialists, providing clear direction and feedback to make sure their work feels right for Monzo.
  • Improving how we work. Spot opportunities to make our creative process more effective, whether that’s through new tools, better ways of working or clearer guidance for teams.

🤩 We’d love to hear from you if…

  • You have a strong portfolio of brand design and campaign work, showing how you’ve developed distinctive creative ideas and brought them to life across different channels.
  • You’re an expert in your craft, with particular strength in typography, visual identity and art direction. You care about the details and can show how they make the overall work stronger.
  • You have experience managing and developing designers, including giving feedback, supporting career growth and helping people do their best work.
  • You’ve led complex creative projects from brief to delivery, making decisions, bringing people with you and maintaining a high standard throughout.
  • You can think beyond a single project: you’ve helped shape a brand’s direction, developed design systems or guidelines, or improved how creative work happens across a team or organisation.
  • You’re comfortable working with ambiguity. You can define a direction, navigate competing priorities and make thoughtful trade-offs without losing sight of the bigger picture.
  • You’re proactive, creating your own briefs, bringing the right people together to accomplish your goals. You’re not just a doer, but a problem solver and a self-starter.
  • You enjoy working in a fast-paced environment, working across multiple teams and projects at the same time.
  • You’re a confident collaborator who can explain and advocate for your ideas with designers, cross-functional partners and senior stakeholders.
  • You enjoy raising the quality of design beyond your own work, through mentoring, critique and sharing what you know.
  • You’re highly proficient in Figma and comfortable using the tools you need to develop and deliver your ideas. You have a clear interest in AI tools and are keen to explore and use them to evolve how we work and what we create across design, marketing and product.
  • You have experience working in a digital-first brand, creative agency or in-house team, and can show how your work has supported customer or business goals.
  • You can develop ideas for motion and communicate them through storyboards or creative direction. You don’t need to be a motion specialist.

We’d also love to see experience across areas such as web design, campaigns, print, film, illustration or 3D. You don’t need to be an expert in all of them. 🙌 What’s in it for you 💰 £98,000 - £114,000 + Incentive Awards tied to your performance. ⏰ We offer flexible working hours and trust you to work enough hours to do your job well, and at times that suit you and your team. 📚 £1,000 learning budget each year to use on books, training courses and conferences. 🏡 We’ll set you up to work from home. All employees are given a MacBook.

➕ Plus lots more! Take a look at our benefits. 🌈 The application journey

  • 30-minute call with our Recruiter
  • 1-hour craft interview where you’ll present a single case study to 2 members of our Brand Design team.
  • 1-hour interview covering values ways of working.

To be considered for this role you will need to include your CV and a portfolio showcasing your work. This will allow us to better understand your experience. We’d particularly love to see projects you’ve led from start to finish, including the thinking behind the work, the impact it had and your individual contribution.

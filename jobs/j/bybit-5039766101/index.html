Role Purpose Lead the establishment and ongoing oversight of the Pakistan entity’s independent Compliance function and Anti-Money Laundering, Counter-Terrorist Financing and Counter-Proliferation Financing (AML/CFT/CPF) framework. During the pre-licensing phase, the role holder will lead Compliance-owned licensing deliverables, develop the local control framework and assess operational readiness. Following licensing, the role holder will discharge the entity’s CO/MLRO responsibilities, advise the Board and senior management, and maintain effective engagement with PVARA, the Financial Monitoring Unit (FMU) and other relevant authorities.

The role combines CO and MLRO responsibilities, subject to applicable regulatory requirements and any required regulatory acceptance or approval. The suitability of the combined arrangement will be reviewed periodically, considering independence, conflicts, capacity, resources and the entity’s nature, scale and complexity. Key Responsibilities

  • Licensing and Operational Readiness
  • Lead Compliance-owned components of the PVARA licensing application and coordinate relevant inputs from Legal, Business, Risk, Finance, Operations and Technology.
  • Develop and maintain a regulatory obligations register, licensing deliverables tracker and local implementation roadmap.
  • Assess gaps between group policies, systems and controls and applicable Pakistan requirements; oversee localisation and track remediation.
  • Establish local Compliance and AML/CFT/CPF policies, governance arrangements, escalation procedures and regulatory reporting processes.
  • Define local control requirements for customer onboarding, transaction monitoring, sanctions screening, Travel Rule compliance and suspicious transaction reporting.
  • Assess the suitability and readiness of local, group-provided and outsourced controls, including relevant pre-launch testing.
  • Provide a documented Compliance and AML readiness assessment to the appropriate governance forum, escalating unresolved material gaps before launch.
  • Compliance Oversight
  • Establish, administer and maintain a documented, risk-based Compliance Management System (CMS) proportionate to the entity’s activities and risk profile.
  • Advise the Board, relevant committees and senior management on applicable legal and regulatory requirements, licence conditions and regulatory developments.
  • Develop and execute a risk-based Compliance Monitoring and Testing Plan; document findings, agree remediation ownership and deadlines with accountable functions, independently validate closure, and escalate overdue or inadequate remediation.
  • Oversee compliance with applicable conduct, customer protection, complaints-handling, client-asset and market-integrity requirements, according to the entity’s licensed activities.
  • Review proposed products, services and material business changes from a local compliance perspective, identifying requirements, restrictions and necessary controls.
  • Ensure material non-compliance is investigated, promptly escalated and remediated, and notified to PVARA where required.
  • Maintain regulatory submission and commitment trackers, overseeing timely and accurate Compliance-owned responses and escalating dependency risks.
  • Act as the primary Compliance liaison with PVARA for supervisory engagement, inspections and information requests.
  • Develop and oversee Compliance training for employees, senior management and relevant key individuals.
  • AML/CFT/CPF and Financial Crime
  • Establish, implement and maintain the entity’s AML/CFT/CPF framework, aligned with applicable Pakistan legislation, regulatory requirements and relevant directions or guidance.
  • Lead the enterprise-wide financial crime risk assessment and oversee risk-based mitigation measures.
  • Oversee customer due diligence, beneficial ownership identification, customer risk assessment, enhanced due diligence and ongoing monitoring.
  • Oversee transaction monitoring, sanctions screening and targeted financial sanctions controls, including escalation and action on relevant alerts.
  • Oversee virtual asset-specific controls, including blockchain analytics, risks associated with unhosted wallets and Travel Rule compliance where applicable.
  • Receive and assess internal suspicious activity referrals, determine whether external reporting is required, and submit suspicious transaction reports to the FMU within applicable deadlines.
  • Exercise independent judgement on suspicious transaction reporting without requiring business approval, while maintaining confidentiality and preventing tipping-off.
  • Manage the entity’s FMU/goAML registration, access arrangements and reporting processes, as applicable.
  • Ensure required customer, transaction and investigation records are retained for applicable periods and remain accessible and retrievable.
  • Liaise with the FMU and other competent authorities on financial crime matters and oversee relevant information requests.
  • Oversee AML/CFT/CPF training and awareness, tailored to employees’ responsibilities and exposure to risk.
  • Governance, Reporting and Control Assurance
  • Provide periodic reports to the Board or designated committee on Compliance and AML/CFT/CPF effectiveness, material risks, incidents, findings and remediation progress.
  • Escalate urgent or material matters promptly rather than waiting for scheduled reporting.
  • Attend relevant Board and committee meetings and provide independent advice and challenge.
  • Assess and oversee group-provided and outsourced Compliance/AML services, including local regulatory suitability, service standards, data access, management information and escalation arrangements.
  • Oversee delegated Compliance and AML/CFT/CPF activities and remain accountable for discharging the CO/MLRO responsibilities, without displacing the responsibilities of the entity, Board, senior management or operational control owners.
  • Coordinate with Risk and Internal Audit, while preserving the distinction between Compliance oversight and independent audit assurance.
  • Support periodic review of the role holder’s continuing fitness, propriety and suitability, including the required review of the MLRO appointment.
  • Maintain effective handover and suitably qualified cover arrangements for absences, consistent with applicable regulatory requirements.

Qualifications and Experience Essential

  • A degree in law, finance, business or a related discipline, or an equivalent relevant professional qualification.
  • At least eight years’ relevant Compliance and/or AML/CFT experience in financial services, including at least three years in a senior Compliance, MLRO or equivalent leadership role.
  • Strong knowledge of Pakistan’s financial regulatory and AML/CFT framework, including the Virtual Assets Act, applicable PVARA regulations, the Anti-Money Laundering Act and FMU reporting requirements.
  • Demonstrable understanding of virtual asset business models and their financial crime, conduct and operational risks.
  • Practical experience with customer due diligence, transaction monitoring, sanctions screening, investigations and suspicious transaction reporting.
  • Experience developing risk assessments, policies, monitoring programmes and Board-level reporting.
  • Ability to translate regulatory requirements into workable business, operational and technology controls.
  • Be resident in Pakistan by the date of the formal CO/MLRO appointment and maintain residency as required under applicable regulations.
  • Fluency in English and Urdu.

Desirable

  • Professional certification such as CAMS, an ICA qualification or an equivalent Compliance/AML credential.
  • Experience in virtual assets, exchanges, fintech or digital financial services.
  • Experience supporting a regulatory licence application or establishing a local Compliance function.
  • Experience engaging with PVARA, FMU, SECP, SBP or other relevant Pakistani authorities.
  • Familiarity with blockchain analytics tools and Travel Rule solutions.
  • Experience overseeing centralised group services and outsourced control arrangements.

Key Competencies

  • Independence, integrity and sound judgement.
  • Confidence to challenge senior management constructively.
  • Clear communication with regulators, the Board and cross-functional teams.
  • Strong analytical, investigative and problem-solving skills.
  • Effective prioritisation in a developing regulatory environment.
  • Ability to build a local control framework while working within a global organisation.

Authority and Independence

  • Direct access to the local Board or designated committee, including the ability to escalate without prior management clearance.
  • Timely and unrestricted access to relevant records, systems, personnel and management information necessary to discharge the role, subject to applicable confidentiality and data-handling requirements.
  • Adequate resources and suitably qualified support proportionate to the entity’s activities and risk profile.
  • No client-facing, sales or other responsibilities that compromise independence, create material conflicts or impair CO/MLRO duties.
  • Remuneration arrangements that do not link the role holder’s performance to sales or revenue targets.
  • Group reporting arrangements that do not restrict local regulatory accountability, Board access or independent MLRO decision-making.
  • Board oversight of appointment, replacement and removal, with regulatory approvals or notifications completed as applicable.

Regulatory Appointment Conditions The role holder must satisfy applicable fitness and propriety, residency, employment, competence and regulatory appointment requirements at appointment and on an ongoing basis. Any required PVARA approval, acceptance or notification must be completed in accordance with the applicable framework.

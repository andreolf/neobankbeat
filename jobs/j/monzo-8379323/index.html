Senior Treasury Capital Manager

DublinFinance & TreasuryOn-site€100–130Kposted 2d ago

🚀 We’re on a mission to make money work for everyone. We’re waving goodbye to the complicated and confusing ways of traditional banking. After starting as a prepaid card, our product offering has grown a lot in the last 10 years in the UK. As well as personal and business bank accounts, we offer joint accounts, accounts for 16-17 year olds, a free kids account and credit cards in the UK, with more exciting things to come beyond. Our UK customers can also save, invest and combine their pensions with us.

With our hot coral cards and get-paid-early feature, combined with financial education on social media and our award winning customer service, we have a long history of creating magical moments for our customers! We’re not about selling products - we want to solve problems and change lives through Monzo ❤️ 📍Dublin, Ireland | 💰€100,000 - €130,000, Incentive Awards tied to your performance➕ benefits ⭐Our Finance team We are looking for a dynamic Treasury Capital Manager in Dublin to become a key part of the team building the new Monzo European entity. Reporting into the Director of Treasury and Capital, you will shape, develop and implement the Capital management process and infrastructure in tandem with supporting the growth and strategic development of the business. As well as skills and experience in Treasury and Capital Management, the ability to adapt, be creative and work in a fast paced environment with wider Group colleagues will be essential talents as the business grows.

As part of a new and growing team, the Treasury Capital Manager will be responsible for ensuring that Monzo effectively manages its capital and ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements in Europe. This is an opportunity to be part of something from the start and make a real difference. 🔑You’ll play a key role by…

  • Lead the planning and production of Monzo Europe’s ICAAP in line with regulatory capital requirements.
  • Develop capital plans and forecasts, including stress testing and scenario analysis.
  • Lead the planning and implementation work in line with Monzo’s recovery and resolution requirements including the Recovery Plan and Solvent Wind Down Plan.
  • Identify the impact of prudential regulatory change and supporting relevant teams to assess and adapt to the challenges.
  • Optimise the capital structure and allocation to support growth and strategic objectives.
  • Prepare and present regular financial reports and forecasts to senior management.
  • Building strong relationships with internal teams such as the business/product collectives to optimize the bank's capital structure and funding strategies.
  • Provide operational coverage for the Director of Treasury and Capital during holidays.

🤩We’d love to hear from you if you have…

  • A bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Accounting, or a related field. A Master’s degree or professional qualification (e.g., CFA, ACA, ACCA) is preferred.
  • A minimum of 5 years of experience in Treasury Capital management, within the banking sector, a consultancy, or regulatory body.
  • Have detailed knowledge/experience of the European regulatory requirements, and the ability to interpret what that means for the relatively unique circumstances of our bank.
  • Have the ability to coordinate, participate and lead projects to produce regulatory deliverables and maintain the Bank’s set of prudential documentation and supporting materials.
  • Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills.
  • Strong leadership abilities and experience in working across dispersed teams.
  • Exceptional communication and interpersonal skills.

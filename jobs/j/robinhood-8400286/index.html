Events Marketing Manager

Menlo Park, CA; New York, NYMarketing & GrowthOn-siteposted 3d ago

Apply on Robinhood →

Join us in building the future of finance. Our mission is to democratize finance for all. An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades. The largest transfer of wealth in human history. If you’re ready to be at the epicenter of this historic cultural and financial shift, keep reading. About the team + role We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world’s biggest financial problems. We’re looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn’t a place for complacency, it’s where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We’re a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

Robinhood is hiring an Events Marketing Manager to own the cohesive marketing plans for its growing roster of events. This is an amplification role. You'll take the approved event strategy and build the marketing plan and messaging for each workstream (RSVP, livestream, and post-event) to get people to register or buy tickets, tune in to the livestream, and keep them talking after the event ends. Your plans will cover all the major channels (paid and organic social, email and lifecycle marketing, PR), and you’ll also shape how the brand shows up on-site. This is not an event production or logistics role. Venue, travel, F B, and on-site operations sit with other members of the Events team. We are looking for someone who thinks in funnels, channels, audiences, and reach, and who is just as comfortable owning a brief as running a daily performance check-in.

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA or New York, NY offices, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week. At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

What you’ll do

Event Marketing Strategy

  • Build the event marketing and amplification plan across 3 core workstreams (RSVP/ticket sales, livestream, and post-event), including goals, audience targeting, messaging hierarchy, asset needs, and channel mix.
  • Drive cross-functional alignment on the amplification plan with Channel Leads (Social, Lifecycle Marketing, Comms) and Marketing Leadership); own briefing to Creative team for assets.
  • Align with Product Managers on event-related product announcements and promotions, and incorporate into the event marketing plan and keynote.
  • Partner with Senior Brand Manager on ticketing/RSVP and pricing approach, and manage the registration types, flows and nurture strategy.
  • Collaborate with the Sponsorship Lead to define and execute the sponsor marketing strategy and deliverables included in each sponsor package (social, email, comms, etc).
  • Support Program Manager on Legal Compliance review of marketing assets.

Promotional Plan Execution Optimization

  • Execute the approved marketing plan across all channels with one promotional calendar that covers the full event arc (3 workstreams); ensures all assets go live on schedule and are on message.
  • Manage paid social planning and optimization, including budget tracking, platform partner relationships, and in-flight optimization against RSVP, ticket, and livestream tune-in goals.
  • Partner with the organic social team and Creative on teaser sequencing, social-first creative, and posting cadence to build momentum and reach.

On-Site Branding Marketing

  • Own the on-site branding strategy along the attendee journey (e.g., signage, branded environments, swag); includes brief creation.
  • Build marketing plans for cross-functional on-site activations, such as CX and Research, in partnership with their teams.

Content Capture Post-Event Promotion

  • Build the content pipeline to drive post-event momentum and reach: determine the post-event promotion plan that turns keynotes, sessions, and on-site moments into clips, highlights, and recap content that extends the moment and drives reach after the event ends.
  • Partner with the content capture and social teams for the on-site content capture, reviews, and go-live process.

Reporting Measurement

  • Set goals for each workstream and channel (RSVPs and attendance, livestream views and CPV, engagement), benchmarked against prior events.
  • Track daily registration counts, cohort conversion, and channel performance to forecast attendance and optimize in flight.
  • Own the post-event program report: consolidate channel results against goals and prior events, with key learnings per work stream and attendee survey results (in partnership with Research).
  • Lead the marketing retro with channel partners and turn the learnings into recommendations for the next event.

What you bring

  • 5+ years of experience in marketing (events, brand, social, or content marketing) within a consumer-facing company; financial services a plus.
  • Proven track record of building and executing integrated marketing plans across paid, owned, and earned channels (e.g., paid social, email and lifecycle, PR), with strong organic social expertise and a record of optimizing against performance goals.
  • Hands-on experience driving ticket sales, registration, or attendance for events, including promotions, audience targeting, and conversion optimization.
  • Proven track record driving awareness and large-scale reach for events or campaigns (e.g., livestream viewership, social and earned reach), with metrics to back it up.
  • Experience driving on-site branding and brand activations at events a plus.
  • Comfort using data (e.g., registration, campaign performance, and audience insights) to forecast, optimize, and report on results.
  • Clear, confident communicator with experience writing briefs and managing creative and agency partners through execution.
  • Experience leading cross-functional initiatives, aligning channel leads and stakeholders around a shared plan, including Legal Compliance review processes (ideally in a regulated industry).
  • Highly organized and detail-oriented, with strong project management skills and the ability to manage complex workflows in a fast-paced environment.
  • Self-starter and problem-solver who thrives in ambiguity and adapts quickly as priorities evolve.
  • Based in the U.S. (NYC or SF preferred), with travel required for key events throughout the year.

What we offer

  • Challenging, high-impact work to grow your career.
  • Performance driven compensation with multipliers for outsized impact, bonus programs, equity ownership, and 401(k) matching.
  • Top Tier benefits to fuel your work, including 100% paid health insurance for employees with 90% coverage for dependents.
  • Access to the Robinhood Employee Fund that gives eligible US employees the opportunity to invest in a private employee fund that provides exposure to Robinhood Ventures funds.
  • Access to the best AI tools on the market and continuous AI skill-building for every employee, technical or not.
  • Lifestyle wallet - a highly flexible benefits spending account for wellness, learning, and more.
  • Employer-paid life disability insurance, fertility benefits, and mental health benefits.
  • Time off to recharge including company holidays, paid time off, sick time, parental leave, and more!
  • Exceptional office experience with catered meals, events, and comfortable workspaces.

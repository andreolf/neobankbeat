Director of Member Engagement Marketing

India, Delhi NCRMarketing & GrowthOn-siteposted today

ABOUT TIDE At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting. Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, India, Germany and France. Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love.

Tide facts

  • Tide is available for UK, Indian, German and French SMEs
  • Over 2 million members: 900,000 UK and 1,100,000 in India and growing rapidly
  • Over $300 million raised in funding
  • Over 2,800 Tideans globally
  • Recognised with Great Place to Work certification three years in a row, and among India’s Top 50 Best Workplaces in Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance in 2026
  • We have offices in Central London, with a member support and technology centre in Sofia, Bulgaria, technology centres in Serbia, Romania, Lithuania and Hyderabad and offices in Gurugram, New Delhi, Berlin, Paris and Luxembourg

ABOUT THE ROLE

As the Director – Member Engagement Marketing (India), you will lead the strategy and execution of lifecycle, CRM, and engagement marketing initiatives that directly drive revenue and product adoption for Tide's India Business. You will work at the intersection of product, data, growth, and customer success to ensure every member interaction is personalised, impactful, and revenue-generating — while building and leading a high-performing, on-the-ground India team. You'll bring a test-and-learn, data-driven approach to member marketing, partnering closely with the SVP of Member Engagement Marketing, Product, and Data Analytics teams to build deep relationships with existing members that increase usage, cross-sell, and grow lifetime value.

Core responsibilities will include

Strategic Ownership

  • Own the member engagement marketing strategy across the entire lifecycle — from onboarding and activation to retention and reactivation.
  • Own the retention marketing strategy to grow revenue from the existing member base.
  • Build cross-sell and upsell campaigns that deepen product usage and maximise member lifetime value.
  • Identify high-potential member segments and create tailored journeys that boost engagement and reduce churn.
  • Lead CRM marketing programmes across email, push, in-app, SMS, and WhatsApp focused on driving behavioural outcomes and monetisation.
  • Lead nurture journeys that drive reactivation, repeat usage, and deeper financial engagement.

Analytics Data-Led Decision-Making

  • Partner closely with Data Analytics to deeply understand the India member base and define KPIs tied to conversion, churn reduction, and engagement.
  • Bring a test-and-learn approach: run A/B and MVT testing across CRM channels to continually improve targeting, content, and offers.
  • Build a segmentation and targeting strategy based on lifecycle stage, member value, product uptake, and behavioural signals.
  • Own dashboarding and reporting of all member marketing metrics and attribution, sharing results broadly with stakeholders.
  • Apply AI/ML tools to accelerate marketing execution — e.g. testing more variants, sharpening targeting, and speeding up insight-to-action — with a track record of measurable gains in team speed or campaign performance.

Platform Tooling Leadership

  • Hands-on experience setting up a team on a CRM platform. Salesforce Marketing Cloud experience is ideal; depth of hands on experience with CRM tooling, including ability to support key platform changes and team training.
  • Ensure the India CRM tech stack is set up to support scalable, personalised, multi-channel campaigns post-migration.

Team Building Leadership

  • Build and lead a high-performing team of CRM/lifecycle marketers, campaign managers, and content strategists in India — this is a team you will be building out, so hiring, structuring, and developing the right talent will be critical to success in this role.
  • Motivate, coach, and grow your team while embedding a test-and-learn, data-driven culture.
  • Collaborate cross-functionally with Product, Growth, Sales, Risk, and Customer Success teams, building strong networks across the business to deliver as 'one team'.
  • Contribute to quarterly planning around revenue growth via member marketing.

India Market Leadership

  • Represent Member Engagement Marketing within the India Business leadership team, acting as the senior on-the-ground owner for engagement marketing strategy and execution.
  • Adapt global MEM strategy and playbooks to the India context — including regional language personalisation, and WhatsApp/SMS as primary engagement channels alongside email and push.
  • Ensure marketing communications comply with India's regulatory requirements (e.g. TRAI/DLT registration and consent management for SMS and outbound calls).
  • Identify cross-sell opportunities specific to India SME members (e.g. GST-linked invoicing triggers, credit and collections products) and build journeys that reflect local product adoption patterns.
  • Partner effectively with UK-based Marketing, Product, and P E stakeholders across time zones, while owning delivery for the India market.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

  • 12+ years of experience in B2C or B2B2C marketing, preferably in fintech, lending, banking, or consumer tech, with meaningful experience in the Indian market.
  • Proven expertise in lifecycle, retention, and CRM marketing with demonstrable commercial impact (e.g. improved conversion, reduced churn, incremental revenue).
  • Track record of building and leading high-performing marketing/CRM teams — ideally having built a team from the ground up or through a significant scale-up phase.
  • Direct experience leading or contributing to a marketing platform/CDP migration, including local rollout and change management; hands-on experience with Salesforce Marketing Cloud specifically will be a strong advantage given the migration this role will lead (comparable platforms: MoEngage, Adobe Campaign, or similar).
  • Hands-on campaign experience across the full engagement channel mix this role covers — email, push, in-app messaging, SMS, and WhatsApp — rather than one or two channels in isolation.
  • Deep understanding of cross-sell/upsell mechanics, revenue contribution tracking, and member behaviour insights.
  • Strong working knowledge of marketing automation tools and customer data platforms (CDPs); highly analytical, comfortable using data to make decisions and build ROI-driven programmes.
  • Experience with A/B/MVT testing and optimising offers/incentives to drive the best commercial return.
  • Awareness of India-specific regulatory considerations for marketing communications (TRAI/DLT, consent management).
  • Exceptional leadership and stakeholder management capabilities, with the ability to operate confidently across India and UK stakeholders and time zones.
  • A strong, clear communicator able to explain complex information simply and build trust across functions.

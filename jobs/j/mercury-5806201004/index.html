Revenue Strategy Associate - AI Solutions Consulting

San Francisco, CA, New York, NY, Portland, OR, or Remote within Canada or United StatesData & AIRemote onlyposted 2d ago

Apply on Mercury →

Mercury* is building a complete financial stack for ambitious businesses. Since we launched in 2019, our customer base has grown to over 200,000 startups and small businesses from all around the world. As AI changes how finance teams run, we are adding a new kind of customer-facing role: a consultant who shows prospects and customers how much of their finance work Mercury and AI can run for them. As an Revenue Strategy Associate - AI Solutions Consulting, you'll work alongside Account Executives on our highest-value opportunities. You’ll take a deep dive into how money moves through their business, identify time-consuming processes and potential risks, and show CFOs and controllers how to build a more efficient finance stack using Mercury products like Mercury Command and Mercury MCP, integrated with the AI tools they already use. Rather than simply recommending solutions, you’ll build and implement workflows that deliver immediate value

This is a new role with the opportunity to shape how we approach these engagements moving forward. The first engagements will be hands-on and intensive. Your job is to turn them into repeatable playbooks that any engagement can run in a handful of hours. *Mercury is a fintech company, not an FDIC-insured bank. Banking services provided through Choice Financial Group and Column N.A., Members FDIC.

What you’ll do

  • Run finance-operations diagnostics for high-value prospects alongside Account Executives: map workflows, the tool stack, where the hours go, and where the inefficiency sits
  • Lead discovery with CFOs, controllers, and founders, speaking their language and earning trust in the first call
  • Grade a prospect's finance stack for AI readiness (which tools expose an MCP, where the manual hours sit) and recommend the two or three workflows with the biggest unlock
  • Build and run a library of repeatable playbooks
  • Set up those workflows with the customer, train their team, and hand off cleanly so they run without you
  • Feed what you learn about how customers run finance back to the Revenue organization and Mercury more broadly

What we’re looking for

  • 3+ years of hands-on finance

experience

FP A, controllership, finance operations, private equity or investment analysis, or being the finance lead at an early-stage company

  • Firsthand experience wiring AI into finance work: you have built agents or workflows, stood up an MCP server, or run forecasts and close processes through LLM tools, not just experimented with chat
  • Fluency in the stack a growing company runs on: accounting software and ERPs (QuickBooks, NetSuite), payroll, spend management, spreadsheets, and the exports that connect them
  • Comfort in a sales environment: you can run discovery, read a room, and work beside an Account Executive without taking over the deal
  • High EQ and proactive: you build trust with a skeptical controller in one call and figure things out when there is no playbook yet
  • A builder's instinct for repeatability: you turn a one-off fix into a pattern others can run
  • Clear written and verbal communication: you can explain a workflow to a non-technical founder and document it so they can run it later
  • Comfort with ambiguity: this role did not exist yesterday, and you will shape what it becomes

Total Rewards The total rewards package at Mercury includes base salary, equity (stock options/RSUs), and benefits. Our salary and equity ranges are highly competitive within the SaaS and fintech industry and are updated regularly using the most reliable compensation survey data for our industry. New hire offers are made based on a candidate’s experience, expertise, geographic location, and internal pay equity relative to peers. US employees in New York City, Los Angeles, Seattle, or the San Francisco Bay Area: $124,600 $140,100 USD US employees outside of New York City, Los Angeles, Seattle, or the San Francisco Bay Area: $112,100 $126,100 USD

About the Role

We are seeking a versatile and people-oriented HR professional to join our Americas Remote HR Operations team, based in Brazil. The team supports our operations across North and South Americas and assigned remote employee populations. You will serve as the primary local HR operations contact for Brazil while supporting employee services and projects across assigned countries, entities, and remote locations. You will also contribute to new-country setup, entity transfer projects, and the standardization and automation of regional HR processes. The role combines hands-on execution, local regulatory expertise, and cross-border collaboration to deliver accurate, efficient, and consistent HR services across office-based and remote teams.

Key Responsibilities

  • Employee Lifecycle Regional HR Operations
  • Manage onboarding for Brazil and assigned countries and remote employee populations, including employment documentation, vendor registration, and coordination with relevant internal teams.
  • Coordinate offboarding activities, including documentation, statutory notifications where applicable, benefits cessation, and coordination with the teams responsible for access removal and asset returns.
  • Provide accurate employee information and supporting records to the designated payroll team or vendor for offboarding. Final payment calculation is outside this role’s scope.
  • Coordinate with local HR service providers, professional employer organizations, and employers of record, where applicable, to support employee lifecycle processes.
  • HRIS, Employee Data Automation
  • Maintain accurate and up-to-date employee records in HRIS and related systems, ensuring data integrity, confidentiality, and timely updates.
  • Process employee data changes for assigned countries and entities, ensuring appropriate approvals and supporting documentation.
  • Conduct regular data checks and reconciliations to identify and resolve discrepancies.
  • Prepare HR operational reports and maintain audit-ready records.
  • Support the standardization and automation of regional employee data-change workflows.
  • Employee Queries Documentation
  • Serve as a point of contact for employee queries relating to onboarding, employment documentation, benefits, immigration, offboarding, and other HR operational matters.
  • Prepare employment verification letters, reference letters, visa-support letters, and other employee documentation using approved templates and in accordance with local requirements.
  • Track employee queries through resolution and escalate complex or sensitive matters to the appropriate HR partner or regional HR Lead.
  • Support employee relations matters in Brazil, including grievances, disciplinary procedures, and performance improvement plans, in coordination with the relevant HR partner and Legal.
  • Provide responsive support to remote employees across different locations and time zones.
  • Visa Immigration Coordination
  • Coordinate visa, work permit, and residency processes for Brazil and assigned locations, working with external immigration providers and relevant internal stakeholders.
  • Track application progress, renewal dates, and required documentation.
  • Provide employees with clear guidance on immigration-related requirements and timelines.
  • Escalate immigration risks or delays that may affect commencement dates, transfers, or ongoing employment.
  • New-Country Setup Entity Transfer Projects
  • Support the HR workstream for new-country and new-entity setup, including local HR vendor coordination, employment documentation, benefits arrangements, HRIS readiness, and operational processes.
  • Support entity transfer projects through coordination of employee documentation, communications, HRIS updates, and relevant service providers.
  • Maintain project trackers, monitor dependencies, and escalate risks to support timely implementation.
  • Establish scalable HR processes for new or growing locations and remote employee populations in partnership with regional stakeholders and local advisers.
  • Benefits Administration
  • Administer employee benefits enrollment, changes, renewals, and cessation for assigned countries, entities, and remote employee populations.
  • Coordinate with benefits providers, brokers, and internal stakeholders to resolve employee queries and service issues.
  • Support benefits reviews and improvements in line with company policy, local requirements, and regional priorities.
  • Occupational Health Safety Coordination
  • Coordinate pre-employment medical examinations with the appointed OHS vendor, ensuring completion and receipt of the required occupational fitness certificate before employees commence work.
  • Coordinate other required occupational medical examinations and track appointments, deadlines, and completion.
  • Collaborate with the OHS vendor on employee-related compliance activities, required training, documentation, and reporting.
  • Maintain relevant HR compliance records confidentially and coordinate workplace-related matters with the Admin team.
  • HR Compliance Audits
  • Ensure Brazil HR operations align with applicable labor and social security requirements.
  • Support HR compliance across assigned countries and remote employment arrangements in partnership with Legal and local service providers.
  • Monitor relevant regulatory changes and support updates to employment documentation, policies, and operational procedures.
  • Support internal and external HR audits by preparing records, responding to queries, and tracking corrective actions.
  • Maintain employee records and handle personal information in accordance with applicable data protection requirements, including Brazil’s LGPD.
  • SOPs Continuous Improvement
  • Develop, maintain, and improve daily HR operations SOPs, checklists, templates, and service workflows.
  • Standardize regional processes while accommodating country-specific legal and operational requirements.
  • Identify process gaps, recurring employee issues, and opportunities to reduce manual work through automation.
  • Share best practices and support consistent service delivery across the Americas Remote HR Operations team.

Other Responsibilities

  • Support regional HR projects and initiatives as directed by the regional HR Lead.
  • Contribute to local recruitment coordination and employer branding activities as needed.
  • Monitor relevant local talent-market developments, including hiring trends and compensation benchmarks, to support regional HR planning.

Required Qualifications

  • Minimum five years of progressive HR experience in HR operations, a generalist role, or HR management.
  • Comprehensive knowledge of Brazilian labor law and local employment administration.
  • Demonstrated experience managing employee lifecycle processes, HRIS records, benefits administration, and employee queries.
  • Ability to coordinate effectively with regional teams, internal stakeholders, and external vendors across multiple countries and time zones.
  • Fluency in Portuguese and English, both written and spoken.
  • Comfortable building HR processes, policies, and systems from the ground up in a new or scaling environment.
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage competing priorities.
  • Proactive and self-driven, with a track record of identifying gaps and implementing practical solutions.
  • A process-improvement mindset and an interest in HR standardization and automation.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience supporting HR operations across multiple countries in the Americas, including the United States.
  • Experience supporting geographically distributed or remote employee populations.
  • Experience working with PEOs, EORs, or outsourced HR and payroll providers.
  • Exposure to new-country setup, entity transfers, or cross-border employee mobility.
  • Experience coordinating visa and work permit processes.
  • Experience coordinating occupational medical examinations and OHS compliance with external vendors in Brazil.
  • Prior involvement in HR compliance reviews, audits, or SOP development.
  • Experience with HRIS implementation, employee data workflows, or HR process automation.
  • Experience in the technology, fintech, or crypto/Web3 sector.
  • Spanish language proficiency.

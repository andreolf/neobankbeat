ABOUT US At Meow, we provide startups with business accounts, corporate cards, and the clean financial reporting they need to operate and raise capital. Our customers are complex and global, from crypto protocols to multi-entity international businesses. We combine financial infrastructure with hands-on support so founders can operate efficiently and scale with confidence. We’re hiring a Software Engineer who wants real ownership in a fast-moving startup environment.

What You’ll Do

  • Build full-stack web features (front-end, back-end) from zero to one.
  • Comfortable with building against 3rd-party APIs and have a good mind for state machines. The financial system is one giant state machine.
  • Own your work from inception to launch and beyond. We expect engineers to hop in customer channels and iterate with our customers post product-launch.
  • Hold yourself accountable to high standards of quality.

REQUIREMENTS

  • 1-4 years of experience in full-stack web development, preferably with exposure to smaller companies where flexibility and versatility are key
  • Full-stack

experience

Comfortable working across both front-end and back-end technologies (our tech stack: TypeScript, React, Python (FastAPI), Postgres, AWS)

  • Side projects: You've built something because you wanted to or because you're ambitious or just for the sake of building.
  • Must be authorized to work in the U.S. without sponsorship. U.S. citizenship or permanent residency is required.

Compensation Total compensation may vary and could include equity and additional benefits depending on the position offered and location. This is a high-autonomy role where you’ll work directly with innovative, early-stage startups, gaining exposure to complex business models and global operations.

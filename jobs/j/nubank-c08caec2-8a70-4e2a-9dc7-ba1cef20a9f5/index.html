Lead Security Engineer - On-Premises and CloudNetwork

São Paulo · Campinas · Belo Horizonte · Rio de JaneiroEngineeringHybrid

Apply on Nubank →

ABOUT NU Nu serves more than 143 million customers, guided by a mission to fight complexity and empower people. The company has been leading an industry transformation through innovative products and human-centered services. Proprietary technology and data at scale power Nu’s digital platform, built to promote financial access, advancement, and transparency. Its business model thrives on customer love and lower costs, feeding a flywheel of growth and profitability. Visit our Institutional Page https://www.nu.com/2026-en

ABOUT THE ROLE

As Lead Security Engineer, you will own the security of Nu's endpoints and networks across on-premises and cloud infrastructure. Why it matters at Nu: endpoints and networks are the front line for protecting customer data and keeping our digital services available in every country where we operate. Your designs are used by many teams, so they need to scale through automation. Impact on our business: you reduce security exposure before it becomes an incident, shorten the time to detect and respond when one happens, and make security a smooth experience for engineers and customers instead of a blocker.

Goals you will own

  • Secure, automated, multi-country configuration and management of endpoints, network systems and security applications.
  • Lower operational toil in network security (alert enrichment, rule cleanup, change review), measured by time to triage and false positive rate.
  • Guardrails for safe AI adoption across the company: egress controls, authentication, logging and least-privilege access for agents.
  • Technical leadership on large initiatives, with a voice in annual planning for budgets, projects and long-term security investments.

YOU'LL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR

  • Securing endpoints (Windows, macOS, Linux), networks and cloud environments consistently across multiple countries.
  • Anticipating technical issues at the enterprise and platform level and preventing them through architecture and design decisions.
  • Working on small or undefined security problems, where requirements and approaches are not yet established.
  • Securing the use of AI in the enterprise while also using AI to move faster.
  • Design, implement and support multi-country security solutions for on-premises and cloud infrastructure (endpoint and network), including automation that enables large-scale deployment and operation.
  • Ensure secure configuration and management of endpoints, network systems and security applications, using the right security platforms, tools and operating-system-specific controls.
  • Support the response to cyber threats, incidents and anomalies affecting endpoints and networks.
  • Use AI to reduce operational toil in network security (alert enrichment, rule cleanup, change review) and measure the impact with metrics such as time to triage and false positive rate.
  • Design and implement guardrails for safe AI adoption across the company: egress controls, authentication, logging and least-privilege access for agents.
  • Evaluate build-vs-buy alternatives, articulating trade-offs between technical, analytical and platform needs, efficiency vs. thoroughness, and the impact on technical debt.
  • Develop and maintain security policies and documentation for new endpoint and network technologies: high-level material for non-engineers and detailed technical documentation for engineering teams.
  • Support risk assessments for endpoints, networks and information protection, helping identify and mitigate security exposures.
  • Support security reviews and audits, assisting internal audit teams with endpoint and network security issues and compliance activities, and taking an active part in the discussions.
  • Take part in core team processes (planning, on-call rotations, incident response, consulting meetings) and recommend and implement improvements.
  • Prioritize projects and demands using technical reasoning and data analysis, adapting communication for technical and managerial audiences.
  • Collaborate with vendors, industry groups and cross-functional teams to develop new security capabilities, company-wide initiatives, strategies and roadmaps.
  • Advise on secure solutions for systems, networks and cloud environments, contributing to annual planning for budgets, projects and long-term security investments.
  • Lead or support projects of varying sizes: area leader on large initiatives and project leader on small to medium efforts.
  • Mentor others, share expertise across the functional area and keep learning through research, work assignments and technical or user groups.
  • Obsess over the customer experience and drive operational excellence.

WE ARE LOOKING FOR A PERSON WHO HAS

  • Strong understanding of operating systems (Windows, macOS, Linux) and core security protocols, including authentication, authorization and cryptographic principles.
  • Knowledge of network technologies: routing, switching, network security protocols and cloud networking (AWS, GCP, Azure).
  • Technical expertise with cloud and on-premises infrastructure, including secure architecture, deployment and operations.
  • Knowledge of application-layer technologies, business continuity, disaster recovery (DRP) and secure system design.
  • Experience with identity and access management, security operations and security analytics, plus research, analysis and data-visualization tools.
  • Experience in major incident management and security incident response across endpoints, networks and cloud environments.
  • Ability to conduct in-depth analysis of computer networks and systems to identify vulnerabilities, misconfigurations and security gaps.
  • Awareness of the latest attack techniques, threat actor behaviors and methods used to compromise systems and networks.
  • Experience with automation and programming languages (e.g., Python and Go), including developing scripts, tools and system integrations that enable large-scale deployment and operation.
  • Experience using AI/LLM tools to accelerate security engineering work, such as log and alert triage, firewall rule review, configuration analysis and documentation, with critical validation of the outputs.
  • Understanding of security risks specific to AI systems, such as prompt injection, data leakage, insecure tool/agent permissions and model supply chain risks (e.g., OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications).
  • Experience securing the use of AI in the enterprise: network controls for AI traffic, data classification, DLP, and access control for LLM and agent integrations (e.g., MCP servers).
  • Advanced written and verbal English, used on a daily basis.

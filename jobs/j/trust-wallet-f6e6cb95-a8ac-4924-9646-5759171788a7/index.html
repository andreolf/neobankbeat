Global KOL Manager

Remote - GlobalMarketing & GrowthRemote only

Apply on Trust Wallet →

About the company Trust Wallet is the secure, self-custody Web3 wallet that empowers people to own and control their digital assets. Whether you're new to crypto or an experienced user, Trust Wallet makes it easier, safer, and more convenient to explore Web3, securely access dApps, manage your crypto, and buy and sell digital assets, all in one place.

About the Role

Trust Wallet is looking for a Global KOL Manager to lead and scale our global influencer, creator, and community ecosystem. In this role, you will identify, build, and manage relationships with influential KOLs, content creators, and affiliate partners across key crypto markets, while also helping grow and engage Trust Wallet's global communities. You will work closely with our Growth, Marketing, Community, and Product teams to develop impactful creator partnerships and community programs that drive brand awareness, user acquisition, engagement, retention, and measurable business growth.

We're looking for someone who is deeply connected to the crypto ecosystem, understands how crypto communities and influencers operate, and can turn strong KOL relationships into scalable growth opportunities. Key Responsibilities

  • Global KOL Strategy & Network Development
  • Develop and execute KOL growth strategies across key global markets.
  • Identify, recruit, and maintain strong relationships with high-impact crypto influencers, creators, and community leaders across X, YouTube, Telegram, TikTok, and other relevant platforms.
  • Build and manage a diversified KOL network across different tiers, audiences, regions, and content verticals.
  • Continuously discover emerging influencers, market trends, and new creator partnership opportunities.
  • KOL Partnerships & Affiliate Growth
  • Negotiate and manage KOL collaborations, including paid sponsorships, affiliate partnerships, ambassador programs, and long-term strategic relationships.
  • Develop incentive structures that align KOL performance with Trust Wallet's growth objectives.
  • Drive measurable results through KOL partnerships, including user acquisition, product adoption, trading activity, and revenue contribution.
  • Campaign Execution & Cross-functional Collaboration
  • Work closely with Growth, Marketing, Social, and Product teams to execute KOL activations for product launches, marketing campaigns, and ecosystem initiatives.
  • Coordinate KOL participation in livestreams, giveaways, product reviews, tutorials, community activations, and other creative formats.
  • Ensure clear campaign briefs, messaging, timelines, deliverables, and quality standards.
  • Manage KOL communication and execution from initial outreach to campaign completion.
  • Global Community Engagement
  • Serve as an on-camera and on-mic host for Trust Wallet, leading podcasts, AMAs, X Spaces, livestreams and video content with KOLs, partners and the community.
  • Develop and execute a global community growth and engagement strategy, tailored to key regions, languages, and user segments.
  • Connect KOL partnerships with community growth by turning creator audiences into engaged Trust Wallet users and community members.
  • Performance Tracking & Optimization
  • Set and monitor KPIs across KOL partnerships and communities, including reach, engagement, active community participation, community growth and retention, conversion, user acquisition, trading volume, and ROI.
  • Track campaign performance and continuously optimize KOL selection, budget allocation, and collaboration models.
  • Maintain an organized KOL database and partnership pipeline, including commercial terms, campaign history, and performance records.
  • Provide regular performance reports, market insights, and actionable recommendations.

Requirements

  • 3+ years of experience in KOL Marketing, Influencer Partnerships, Affiliate Marketing, Business Development, or Growth, preferably within Web3, crypto, fintech, or related industries.
  • Strong existing network of crypto KOLs, influencers, and community leaders across multiple markets.
  • Deep understanding of the crypto ecosystem, including wallets, DeFi, trading, and emerging Web3 trends.
  • Proven experience managing influencer campaigns and driving measurable acquisition, conversion, or revenue results.
  • Hands-on experience building or managing online communities, ideally across multiple regions or languages, with a strong understanding of community engagement, moderation, and ambassador programs.
  • Strong relationship-building, negotiation, and partnership management skills.
  • Data-driven mindset with the ability to evaluate KOL performance and optimize campaign ROI.
  • Excellent English communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively with international teams.
  • Highly proactive, organized, and comfortable working in a fast-paced, remote environment.

Nice to Have

  • Experience working with crypto wallets, exchanges, trading platforms, or major Web3 protocols.
  • Existing relationships with top-tier crypto influencers and KOL agencies.
  • Experience building affiliate or ambassador programs at scale.
  • Familiarity with on-chain analytics, referral attribution, and influencer performance tracking tools.
  • Additional language skills and strong regional crypto networks.
  • Experience managing livestreams, creator-led product launches, or community-driven growth initiatives.

What Success Looks Like

  • Build a strong, diversified global KOL network with high-quality, long-term relationships.
  • Consistently deliver successful KOL activations for Trust Wallet's key product and growth initiatives.
  • Improve KOL campaign efficiency, conversion performance, and marketing ROI.
  • Establish scalable processes for KOL discovery, onboarding, management, and performance tracking.
  • Grow healthy, active global communities that support product education, user engagement, retention, and a consistent feedback loop with internal teams.
  • Turn influencer relationships into a sustainable channel for user acquisition and business growth.

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION This is a global and fully remote role. You must have the right to work for the country you are based. WHY WORK AT TRUST WALLET?

  • Be a part of the world’s leading blockchain ecosystem that continues to grow.
  • Excellent learning and career development opportunities.
  • Work alongside diverse, world-class talent, in an environment where learning and growth opportunities are endless.
  • Tackle fast-paced, challenging and unique projects.
  • Work in a truly global organization, with international teams and a flat organizational structure.
  • Work fully remotely with flexible working hours.
  • Enjoy competitive salary and benefits.

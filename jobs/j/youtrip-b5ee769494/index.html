Product Manager

Singapore, Singapore, SingaporeProductOn-siteposted Jan 8

We are looking for a Product Manager to join YouTrip's Product team, working on our marketing and operations platforms that power growth, engagement, and day-to-day operational capabilities. This role is ideal for a Product Manager with 1–4 years of experience who enjoys working across marketing, growth, and operational systems and wants to understand how a fintech product operates end-to-end. You will work on one of our core problem spaces at a time, owning meaningful product areas while developing a strong understanding of how our marketing, operations, and product teams work together. The scope spans user-facing growth experiences, marketing infrastructure, and internal operational tools that must be effective, reliable, and scalable.

This role reports into the Director of Product Management and works closely with marketing, growth, operations, customer support, engineering, and data teams.

What you'll do

  • Own and drive product initiatives within marketing and operations, from problem definition through delivery and iteration
  • Translate marketing, growth, and operational needs into clear product requirements and workflows
  • Work closely with engineers on marketing infrastructure, campaign tooling, and operational systems
  • Improve marketing effectiveness, campaign performance, and operational efficiency across user acquisition, engagement, and retention flows
  • Partner with internal teams to reduce manual work and improve turnaround time on operational and marketing tasks
  • Contribute to roadmap planning, backlog prioritisation, and sequencing with awareness of dependencies and trade-offs
  • Define and track product and operational metrics such as conversion rates, campaign performance, engagement rates, and operational SLAs
  • Produce clear documentation including product requirements, workflows, and campaign specifications

Requirements

  • 1–4 years of experience in product management, growth, business analysis, or a closely related role
  • Comfortable working across marketing, growth, and operational products
  • Strong ability to understand workflows that cut across marketing, operations, engineering, and data
  • Curious, fast learner who is not intimidated by ambiguity, cross-functional complexity, or fast-moving priorities
  • Able to work closely with marketers, engineers, and operations teams and ask thoughtful questions about user behaviour, funnel performance, and process design
  • Structured thinker who can break down messy problems into practical, executable solutions
  • Clear written communicator who values good documentation and clarity
  • Collaborative, low-ego team player who takes ownership and follows through

Bonus (not required)

  • Exposure to fintech, consumer marketing, growth, or lifecycle marketing
  • Experience with CRM platforms, marketing automation tools, or campaign management systems
  • Familiarity with analytics, attribution, or growth experimentation
  • Technical background or strong comfort working with marketing and data infrastructure

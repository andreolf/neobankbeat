Resident Internal Control Officer (RICO)

Rivers, NigeriaOtherOn-siteposted 1d ago

Apply on Moniepoint →

About Us

Ranked in 2024 by the Financial Times, Moniepoint is Africa’s fastest growing fintech, trusted by over 10 million business and individual accounts, processing billions of Naira’s in transactions monthly. Our mission is to enable financial happiness for every African, everywhere.

About this role

The Resident Internal Control Officer is responsible for establishing a permanent, independent, on-site control presence at the assigned Moniepoint MfB branch. The primary objective is to monitor daily transaction integrity across all branch activities, mitigate operational risks, prevent and detect insider and outsider fraud, and ensure adherence to internal bank policies and procedures. Scope note: Loan origination, disbursement, recovery and portfolio review, cheque operations, and compliance activities (including KYC/AML reviews and regulatory reporting) are outside the scope of this role. All other products, services, and activities conducted at the branch fall within the RICO's remit. Where a RICO notices a suspected compliance or AML concern while carrying out control reviews, it is referred to the Compliance function without delay.

Responsibilities

  • Daily On-Site Operational Controls
  • Cash and vault controls: Perform daily vault, teller till, ATM/cash-recycler (where applicable), and petty cash counts, validating physical balances against the Core Banking Application (CBA) records and cash-in-transit documentation.
  • Dual control and key custody: Enforce dual-control principles for vault access, cash movement, high-value transactions, and custody of keys, combinations, tokens, and sensitive stationery.
  • Teller and counter operations: Review cash deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and teller end-of-day balancing for accuracy, correct authorisation, and adherence to transaction limits.
  • Transaction and exception review: Review daily transaction logs, exception reports, reversals, backdated entries, and system overrides to detect unauthorised activity or breaches of approval limits.
  • Reconciliation and general ledger: Verify timely reconciliation of settlement, nostro/correspondent, and inter-branch accounts; review suspense, sundry, and other control accounts for aged or unexplained items; confirm proper clearance.
  • Deposit products: Test the accuracy of interest computation and booking on savings and fixed/term deposit accounts; verify early-liquidation approvals and rollover instructions.
  • Account servicing: Review account maintenance activity, including mandate and signatory changes, BVN/NIN updates, account reactivation, dormant account handling, account closures, liens, and Post-No-Debit (PND) restrictions, to ensure each is properly authorised and supported.
  • Electronic and agent channels: Monitor branch-linked electronic channels and services (POS, ATM, mobile/internet banking, USSD, agent banking) for irregular activity; control the issuance, activation, and custody of cards, PINs, and tokens.
  • Staff and user access: Review system user profiles, access rights, segregation of duties, and staff account activity; flag dormant, shared, or excessive access.
  • Assets and expenses: Verify physical asset registers and monitor branch operating expenses and procurement against approved budget limits.
  • Physical security: Confirm that CCTV, alarms, strong-room integrity, cash-handling procedures, and the visitor register are functioning and in use.
  • Inspections, Investigations Reporting
  • Investigations: Conduct targeted investigations into cash shortages and overages, operational errors, customer complaints, and suspected staff collusion or fraud, preserving evidence and escalating in line with policy.
  • Surprise checks: Carry out unannounced cash, vault, asset, and process checks, and risk-based sample testing across branch activities.
  • Remediation tracking: Track and follow up on remediation plans issued by Head Office Internal Audit or external examiners, where they relate to branch controls, until closure.
  • Examination support: Serve as the branch point of contact during internal audits and external examinations, coordinating document requests and responses.
  • Incident escalation: Escalate material incidents, fraud events, and control breaches to the Regional Lead and the Head, Distribution Network Risk Control immediately, in line with the incident escalation timelines. Findings and escalations are not subject to approval by branch management.
  • Reporting: Compile and submit daily, weekly, and monthly control reports detailing branch risk trends, process gaps, control exceptions, and corrective actions taken.

Experience Background

  • Education: Bachelor's degree or Higher National Diploma (HND) in Accounting, Banking Finance, Economics, or a related business discipline.
  • Experience:

Minimum of 3 years' experience in internal control or internal audit within a Microfinance Bank, Commercial Bank, or Fintech company in Nigeria, with exposure to branch operations.

  • Professional certification (any one of the following):
  • CBN Microfinance Certification Programme (MCP) administered by the Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria (CIBN).
  • Associate of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (ACA).
  • Associate of the Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria (ACIB).
  • ACCA or CFE (Certified Fraud Examiner) will be an added advantage.

Skills Competencies

  • Technical knowledge:
  • Working knowledge of CBN guidelines for Microfinance Banks as they relate to branch operations and internal controls.
  • Understanding of branch banking operations: cash management, deposit products, account maintenance, electronic channels, reconciliations, and general ledger controls.
  • Hands-on experience with a modern Core Banking Application (e.g., Orbit, Finacle, T24, BankOne) and advanced Microsoft Excel; ability to analyse transaction data and exception reports.
  • High moral integrity: Uncompromising ethical standards with zero tolerance for irregularities.
  • Analytical mindset: Exceptional attention to detail and the ability to spot subtle patterns of fraud or process manipulation.
  • Firm communication: Ability to stand one's ground objectively against peer pressure or branch management overrides, while communicating issues clearly to executive leadership.
  • Independence and discretion: Maintains objectivity and confidentiality, and does not take part in the operational processes being controlled.
  • Sound judgement and resilience: Works effectively under pressure and escalates promptly and appropriately.

What Success Looks Like in This Role

  • Zero unmitigated cash discrepancies, dual-control breaches, or unauthorized system overrides across daily branch operations.
  • Early detection and swift escalation of potential insider or outsider fraud, protecting bank assets and customer trust.
  • Timely, accurate submission of daily, weekly, and monthly control reports that provide clear visibility into branch risk trends.
  • 100% closure of internal audit and external examination remediation items within agreed timelines.
  • Strong, independent control governance maintained objectively alongside branch operations.

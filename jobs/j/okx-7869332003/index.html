Legal Counsel - Commercial, Marketing & IP - EMEA (Remote)

EMEASales & PartnershipsOn-siteposted 3d ago

Who We Are At OKX, we believe that the future will be reshaped by technology. Founded in 2017, we are revolutionising world systems through our cutting-edge digital asset exchange, Web3 portal and blockchain ecosystems. We reshape the financial ecosystem by offering some of the most diverse and sophisticated products, solutions, and trading tools on the market. Trusted by more than 50 million users in 180 countries globally, OKX empowers every individual to explore the world of Web3. With our extensive range of products and services, and unwavering commitment to security and reliability, we provide an essential trading platform for the world's investors and institutions.

We are safe and reliable, backed by our Proof of Reserves. As strong believers in the value of blockchain technology, we are committed to building a more inclusive and open financial future — and we know that starts with the people who build it. About the Opportunity We're looking for a Legal Counsel to own two things: protecting the OKX brand globally, and enabling our marketing and commercial engine. Roughly half your time will be spent on IP and marketing legal work - managing our global trademark portfolio through external counsel, advising marketing teams on campaigns, sponsorships and advertising, and coordinating market-entry questions with our regulatory teams and outside firms. The other half is commercial contracts across sponsorship, advertising, vendor and revenue agreements.

You'll work closely with Marketing, Product and Regulatory teams, and act as the bridge to external counsel in the markets where we're expanding. What You'll Be Doing

  • Managing OKX's global IP portfolio - trademarks, branding, graphics and creative assets - instructing and overseeing external counsel on registrations and enforcement
  • Advising marketing teams on campaigns, content, sponsorships and advertising across multiple jurisdictions
  • Instructing external counsel on market-entry questions (e.g. advertising rules, reverse solicitation, sponsorship structures in new markets) and translating their advice into clear internal guidance
  • Drafting, reviewing and negotiating commercial contracts - sponsorship, advertising, media, vendor and revenue agreements
  • Partnering with Regulatory, Product and internal stakeholders to spot issues early and route them to the right specialist
  • Building and maintaining internal playbooks, templates and guidance so marketing teams can self-serve on routine questions
  • Supporting brand-protection actions against infringement and misuse of OKX marks

What We Look For In You

  • Qualified lawyer with 7-10+ years' post-qualification experience in-house or in private practice
  • Experience advising on marketing, advertising or brand matters - from tech, fintech, payments, media, an advertising agency, or a consumer brand
  • Hands-on IP portfolio

experience

trademark registrations, brand protection, and managing external counsel across jurisdictions

  • Strong commercial contracts background - drafting and negotiating complex commercial agreements including but not limited to sponsorship / advertising agreements, vendor agreements, revenue partnerships, digital assets framework agreements, subscription/distribution agreements, partnership agreements and building required stakeholder relationships
  • Sound judgment on when to handle an issue directly and when to escalate to regulatory specialists or external counsel
  • Comfortable working with data and marketing performance context, not just legal text
  • Clear, practical communicator who gives usable answers to non-lawyers

Nice to Haves

  • Background in crypto, tech, fintech, traditional marketing and advertising, or sports partnerships.
  • Experience supporting market entry or expansion into new jurisdictions, contentious experience.
  • Experience in a global or matrix organisation working across time zones.
  • Familiarity with data protection issues, material commercial clauses, in marketing (consent, targeting, analytics)

Perks Benefits

  • Competitive total compensation package
  • L D programs and education subsidy for employees' growth and development
  • Various team-building programs and company events
  • Wellness and meal allowances
  • Comprehensive healthcare schemes for employees and dependants
  • More that we love to tell you about during the process!

Our Commitment to Diversity OKX is committed to equal employment opportunities and building a diverse, inclusive team. We welcome applications from people of all backgrounds, identities and experiences, and we encourage you to apply even if you don't meet every requirement listed above.

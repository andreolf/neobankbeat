About the Organization/Team・組織/チームについて PayPay Bank is responsible for developing the financial infrastructure that supports the PayPay Group's financial services. Our team drives the entire process, from core system cloud infrastructure design and construction to operations and standardization, led by our own engineers. Beyond simply building and maintaining infrastructure, our role encompasses:

  • Evolve our infrastructure while maintaining high reliability
  • Design cloud architectures for future scalability and high-volume transactions
  • Build development organizations and cloud deployment processes themselves

As this is a newly established organization, you will have the opportunity to proactively engage in promoting standardization and building culture. ※ After joining the company, you are expected to be seconded to PayPay Bank as a member of the above project. PayPay銀行は、PayPayグループの金融サービスを支える金融基盤の開発を担っています。 私たちのチームは、コアシステムのクラウドインフラ設計・構築から運用・標準化までを自社エンジニアが主体となって推進しています。 単にインフラを構築・維持するだけではなく、

  • 開発組織やクラウドデプロイプロセスそのものをつくること

までが私たちの役割です。 新しい組織だからこそ、標準化推進や文化づくりにも主体的に関わることができます。 ※入社後に上記プロジェクトメンバーとしてPayPay銀行への出向を予定しています。 Background of Recruitment・募集の背景 At PayPay Group, we are accelerating the evolution of financial services across payment, banking, card, and securities. With the rapid expansion of our infrastructure's importance and scale accompanying business growth, we are looking for Cloud Infrastructure Engineers to support mission-critical systems and improve development efficiency and stability.   • Architect cloud infrastructure that achieves high reliability as a financial platform
  • Architect and build multi-zone infrastructure with AWS regional level failure tolerance
  • Maintain and evolve an infrastructure base where patching and upgrades can be easily implemented without service downtime

Promote Security and Automation

  • Provision secure infrastructure in collaboration with Security Engineers

Continuous Improvement of Development Environment and Deployment Processes

  • AWS deployment automation to ensure fast and secure delivery of cloud services to developers
  • Provide means for developers to easily deploy AWS resources through automation
  • Improve observability, monitoring, alerting, and failure analysis

  • パッチ適用やアップグレードを、サービス停止なしで容易に実施できるインフラ基盤の維持・進化

Ability to Design the Entire System Involved in overall infrastructure design including container platforms (EKS/ECS), networks, storage, and CI/CD pipelines.

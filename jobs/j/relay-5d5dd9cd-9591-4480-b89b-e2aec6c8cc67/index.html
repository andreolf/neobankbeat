compliance & risk · Relay

Risk Strategist

Toronto, ONCompliance & RiskHybrid

Relay is a digital banking platform that gives self-made business owners the tools and know-how to be great with money—bringing clarity, confidence, and control to every dollar earned, so they can turn hard work into lasting success. We do this by replacing financial guesswork with real visibility, transforming cash flow from a constant source of stress into a clear signal owners can use to run stronger, more resilient businesses. Our mission is to increase the success rate of small businesses. Traditional banking has been a growth limiter rather than a growth enabler for business owners, and we’re changing that. Relay is the all-in-one, collaborative money management platform. We’re building for employer SMBs and their finance function, internal and external, and are focused on delivering a human-centric customer experience. Ultimately, we help SMBs be ‘on the money'.

Relay is scaling rapidly, and we’re expanding our Risk Strategy team to help safeguard our platform and the millions of small businesses that rely on it. We’re looking for a data-driven, customer-obsessed problem solver who is excited to define risk strategy and drive meaningful impact – not just maintain existing processes. As a Risk Strategist, you’ll own projects end-to-end, analyzing fraud trends, backtesting and optimizing fraud rules, and collaborating closely with Risk Operations, Customer Experience, Product, Engineering, and Data to strengthen our defenses. You’ll turn insights into rules, processes, and systems that protect our customers and directly influence how we detect and prevent fraud while ensuring a seamless, trusted experience.

At Relay, your work has real reach – helping millions of SMBs operate safely and confidently. If you’re passionate about using data to solve meaningful problems, shaping risk strategy, and making a tangible impact, this is the role for you. What You'll Be Doing

  • Risk Strategy: Own the design and optimization of rules and strategies that balance fraud prevention, operational efficiency, and a seamless customer experience
  • Data-Driven: Analyze onboarding, account, and transaction data to uncover emerging fraud patterns, vulnerabilities, and growth opportunities
  • Design & Optimization: Develop, test, and tune fraud rules across customer touchpoints (onboarding, payments, account usage) to protect customers and reduce false positives
  • Impact Measurement: Backtest rule changes to assess effects on fraud rates, customer experience, and operational workload
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Collaborate with Product and Engineering to embed fraud controls in new features and products
  • Risk Operations Partnership: Work closely with Risk Ops to ensure risk decisions are actionable, efficient, and scalable
  • Tooling & Vendor Management: Implement automation, fraud tools, and external data sources to strengthen the risk engine
  • Continuous Improvement: Monitor performance post-deployment, iterating rules and strategies to drive ongoing optimization
  • Stakeholder Communication: Translate insights into clear recommendations for technical and non-technical stakeholders, aligning risk decisions with business objectives

Who You Are

  • You have 2+ years of experience in fraud, risk strategy, or data analysis, preferably in fintech, banking, or payments
  • You’re proficient in SQL (joins, aggregations, window functions, subqueries) and can write efficient queries with minimal supervision
  • You’re analytical and curious, digging into trends to understand the “why” and using data to shape smarter fraud rules
  • You own projects from start to finish, defining the problem, diving into data, and driving solutions to completion
  • You’re a strong communicator, translating complex insights into clear, actionable recommendations for technical and non-technical stakeholders
  • You’re collaborative and know how to navigate cross-functional work with Risk Operations, Customer Experience, Product, Engineering, and Data
  • You’re developing risk intuition, understanding not just what works, but why each decision drives downstream impact
  • You thrive in fast-paced environments, tackling challenges, optimizing processes, and raising the bar across the team
  • You're actively using AI tools in how you work today and you're excited to push what's possible with them

Bonus Points

  • You have credit risk experience – comfortable with portfolio analysis, decisioning logic, and/or underwriting models
  • You’re proficient in Python for advanced analysis or automation
  • You have worked in a startup or scale-up environment

The Interview Process

  • Stage 1: A 45-minute Google Meet video call with a member of the Talent team
  • Stage 2: A 45-minute Google Meet video call with the Manager, Risk Strategy
  • Stage 3: A take-home case study followed by a 60-minute Google Meet video call with our team
  • Stage 4: A 45-minute in-person interview with a member of the Leadership team

Our Compensation Approach We believe Relayers should feel rewarded for the impact they have on our mission and growth. Compensation follows impact. As impact increases, compensation grows, and we do not limit compensation changes to a once-a-year review cycle. The annual salary range for this role is $112,500 CAD to $137,500 CAD For candidates who demonstrate full readiness for the defined scope of the role, the typical starting salary is $125,000 CAD. Offers below this point reflect candidates we believe can grow into the full scope of the role with support and development. Offers above this point reflect impact that meaningfully exceeds the role’s defined expectations or an expanded scope from day one.

We encourage you to have a conversation with your recruiter and ask questions about compensation throughout the hiring process.

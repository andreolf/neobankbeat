About PhonePe Limited

Headquartered in India, its flagship product, the PhonePe digital payments app, was launched in Aug

  • As of April 2025, PhonePe has over 60 Crore (600 Million) registered users and a digital payments acceptance network spread across over 4 Crore (40+ million) merchants. PhonePe also processes over 33 Crore (330+ Million) transactions daily with an Annualized Total Payment Value (TPV) of over INR 150 lakh crore.

PhonePe’s portfolio of businesses includes the distribution of financial products (Insurance, Lending, and Wealth) as well as new consumer tech businesses (Pincode - hyperlocal e-commerce and Indus AppStore Localized App Store for the Android ecosystem) in India, which are aligned with the company’s vision to offer every Indian an equal opportunity to accelerate their progress by unlocking the flow of money and access to services.

Why PhonePe

At PhonePe our vision is to provide financial inclusion to a billion Indians. Today we have made significant progress on this vision by making digital payments far simpler and ubiquitous than it has ever been. However we wish to make it so easy, safe, and universally accepted that people never feel the need to carry cash or cards again. And not just in Urban India. At PhonePe, our aim to transform the entire Indian landscape so that every Indian, in every corner of the country, makes significant economic progress. Onwards and upwards! That is our goal.

Why PhonePe Design

If you are sold on the vision of the company, you may want to consider the design team as a place to make this a reality. The design team at PhonePe is a bunch of creative trailblazers who are leading the effort to make a dent in the fintech universe. The design team consists of Product Designers, Visual Designers, Graphic Motion Designers, and Illustrators who are all engaged in blending art and science into a promising value proposition for our users. About Share.Market Share.Market is how PhonePe is rebuilding trading and investing for India. It's a full-stack broking platform — stocks, F O, ETFs, mutual funds, IPOs, and WealthBaskets — with a research and intelligence layer (factor analysis on every stock, expert-curated baskets) sitting on top. It's a separate app from the main PhonePe one, and it's backed by PhonePe's reach, trust, and distribution.

This is a hard, interesting design problem. You're working with regulated financial products, real money, and a spectrum of users that runs from a first-time investor buying their first stock to an active trader running F O strategies. The job is to make something genuinely powerful feel calm and trustworthy — not to bolt more features onto a busy screen.

The role

You'll own design for Share.Market end to end. Today that means you, the craft, and a close partnership with product and engineering — this is a hands-on leadership role, not a manage-from-a-distance one. As the product grows, you'll build and lead the team around it. You'll set the design direction, ship the work yourself when it matters, and be the person accountable for the quality of the experience. You'll report to the Head of Design (PhonePe Group) and work alongside the broader PhonePe design org, but Share.Market's design is yours to own.

What you'll do

  • Own the experience. Set the bar for what good looks like on Share.Market, and hold it. Strategy, flows, visual craft, microcopy — you're accountable for all of it.
  • Ship, don't just direct. Get into the file. Design the hard screens yourself. Prototype to find the answer rather than arguing about it in a doc.
  • Make complex things simple. Turn dense financial data and regulated flows into something a first-time investor trusts and a serious trader respects. This is the core craft of the job.
  • Partner closely. Work shoulder to shoulder with product and engineering as an equal. Bring the user's point of view to roadmap and tradeoff conversations early, not at the end.
  • Build the team. As Share.Market grows, manage and grow the designers around you, and set the standards they'll work to.

What we're looking for

  • 12+ years in product design, with real ownership of consumer products at scale. Some of that as a lead, whether managing people or anchoring quality for a product.
  • Proven craft. A portfolio that shows depth, not just breadth — interaction design, visual polish, and clear thinking about why each decision was made.
  • Domain knowledge is a strong advantage. This is a genuinely complex space — broking, F O, market structure, the way regulation shapes every flow. If you've worked in trading, broking, or wealth, or you invest seriously yourself and understand it from the inside, that's a real head start. Not a hard requirement, but it counts for a lot.
  • Comfort with complexity. You've designed for data-heavy or regulated products — fintech, trading, health, anything where the details are hard and the stakes are real. Or you can show us you'll get there fast.
  • Systems thinking. You design with the whole product in mind, not one screen at a time. You're fluent with design systems and know when to extend one versus break from it.
  • Strong written and verbal communication. You can write clear microcopy, make a tight argument for a direction, and give direct, useful feedback.
  • Player-coach instinct. You're senior enough to lead and still want your hands on the work.

What we care about We hire and grow people around three things: craft, context, and community.

  • Craft — the quality of the work and the attention to detail behind it.
  • Context — understanding the bigger picture: the user, the business, the system you're part of.
  • Community — building the people and the relationships around the work, not just the work itself.

We believe in subtractive design: the best version of a financial product usually has less in it, not more. We'd rather remove the right thing than add another feature. If that resonates, you'll like it here. Why this matters Most Indians have never owned a stock. The ones who do often find investing intimidating, opaque, or built for someone more sophisticated than they feel. Getting this right means more people participating in the country's growth, with confidence and without getting burned. That's the work.

CRM Business Designer

London, GBDesignHybridposted 2d ago

We're looking for a Visual Designer to join our CRM team in London. You'll be helping our 60+ strong global CRM team run as smoothly as possible, to get millions of our customers excited about what Wise has to offer. As a Visual Designer, you’ll be a key member of the CRM team, working collaboratively with our in-team designers and copywriters to deliver campaigns of the highest quality. You will be responsible for designing emails and comms that help Wise reach millions of customers around the world.

You have a passion for crafting visually compelling email campaigns for business customers, and a deep understanding of how integral design is to CRM performance. You’re a curious A/B tester, as well as an efficiency driver to spot opportunities to scale CRM Design to support our growing team.

  • As one of three designers in our team, you’ll be our go-to for Business customer (mostly sole traders or SMEs) emails, while also working on comms for personal customers.
  • Create visually appealing email templates that champion our brand guidelines and are laser-focused on our marketing objectives.
  • Present your work in a clear, compelling way that shows creative and thoughtful responses to brief
  • You’ll propose ideas for A/B Testing for understanding design’s impact on core CRM metrics.
  • You’ll contribute to our CRM Design System, and be responsible for scaling our design function in line with our growing CRM team.
  • Design and implement responsive layouts, animations and incorporative multimedia elements that provide optimal viewing experiences across various devices and email clients.

This role will give you the opportunity to

  • Have Strategic Impact - your work with define how millions of Wise customers around the globe interact with the Wise brand, allowing you to shape a core pillar of the customer experience
  • Work with Autonomy - you’ll have the freedom to define processes and strategies for improving creative quality and team efficiency.
  • Be Analytical and Creative - you’ll get to balance data and creativity to measure the effectiveness of your team’s work

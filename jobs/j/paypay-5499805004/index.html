We are looking for a UX Writer to join our team and work closely with designers to create products that put the user experience first.

Job description

As a member of the UX Writing Team, you will create, proofread, and propose clear, user-friendly English UX copy for the PayPay app. You will collaborate closely with engineers, product managers, designers, and other stakeholders to improve the overall user experience. You will work alongside other UX writers, with support from the UX Writing Team Lead and Manager. Main Responsibilities ・Write clear and concise English microcopy that follows our voice and tone and writing guidelines

・Collaborate with designers, product managers, stakeholders, and other UX writers to improve UI copy and user flows ・Maintain consistency across products by contributing to and improving our writing guidelines ・Identify opportunities to improve UX writing processes and workflows, including through the use of AI The Experiences You Will Gain ・Work on a diverse range of features and develop a uniquely comprehensive knowledge of the product and its many experiences ・Be a pioneer of UX writing in Japan leading in the industry

・Work with a team of UX writers Required Skills ・Native-level English proficiency, with the ability to write clear and natural English copy ・Strong Japanese communication skills, both written and spoken, with sufficient proficiency to discuss detailed and nuanced topics with team members ・Either A or B below: A: Japanese to English translation (3 years minimum), or Japanese to English localization (2 years minimum) B: UX or content writing experience in English (2 years minimum)

Preferred Skills ・Basic knowledge about websites and mobile apps (iOS/Android) ・Basic user flow and UI design knowledge PayPay 5 senses

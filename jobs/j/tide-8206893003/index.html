ABOUT TIDE At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting. Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, Germany and France. Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love.

Tide facts

  • Tide is available for UK, German and French SMEs
  • Over 2 million members globally and rapidly growing
  • Over $300 million raised in funding
  • 3,000 Tideans globally
  • We have offices in Central London, with a member support and technology centre in Sofia, Bulgaria, technology centres in Serbia, Romania, Lithuania and Hyderabad and offices in Gurugram, New Delhi, Berlin, Paris and Luxembourg

ABOUT THE TEAM

We are a close-knit team of around 20 lawyers based in the UK, Germany, France, India and Bulgaria. We are one of the most cross-functional teams in the company, helping Tideans accomplish their goals, grow the company and stay compliant. We place a lot of emphasis on team collaboration, honesty and support. We seek to make all new joiners feel part of Team Legal from day one. We're looking for an individual who'll not only fit into that team, but will bring new ideas, challenge us and roll up their sleeves when needed. We value business-minded lawyers who can provide pragmatic, actionable advice.

ABOUT

THE ROLE

We are looking for a 5+ PQE UK-qualified or EU-qualified counsel who will join as a critical part of our team to help meet growing business needs and support our ambitious expansion plans. As Employment Legal Counsel, you will have the opportunity to lead a wide range of legal initiatives in the employment area, providing strategic advice in a cross-jurisdictional context. You will be able to lend your skills to a number of business areas across Tide with regular exposure to senior business stakeholders.

As Employment Legal Counsel, you’ll be:

  • Strategic HR Partnering: Serving as primary legal advisor to the People/HR team, Talent Acquisition, and executive leadership on all employment law matters.
  • Cross-Border Workforce Expansion: Advising on international expansion, establishing local employment frameworks, Employer of Record (EoR) setups, contractor vs. employee classification assessments, and global mobility policies.
  • Employment Litigation Disputes: Managing potential and active employment tribunal claims, grievances, disciplinaries and high-risk separations, including pre-action strategy and ACAS early conciliation. Supporting terminations across all of Tide’s jurisdictions, negotiating and drafting settlement agreements, and instructing and supervising external counsel.
  • Incentives Executive Pay: Partnering on executive hires, restrictive covenants, retention strategies, and equity/incentive plan rules.
  • Workplace Policies Compliance: Drafting and updating global employment contracts, handbooks and D I policies, and keeping Tide compliant with evolving employment laws across our operating hubs. This includes preparing for the phased reforms under the UK Employment Rights Act 2025, implementing pay transparency requirements, and managing working time rules across jurisdictions.
  • M A / HR Due Diligence: Providing employment law input on corporate transactions, TUPE / business transfers, and post-acquisition HR integration.
  • External Counsel Management: Managing local counsel across foreign jurisdictions efficiently and cost-effectively.
  • Scale and Enablement: Further developing the collaboration between the Legal and People teams, building out processes, playbooks, escalation matrices and template banks. Designing and delivering training for managers, HR and the wider business on key employment law topics, and keeping stakeholders informed of legislative changes and their impact on Tide.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

You’ll have good working knowledge of technical areas of employment law in the UK and EU, particularly:

  • Employment disputes (particularly in managing UK employment tribunal claims)
  • Workforce restructuring (contractors to employees, temp workers to employees, remote to hybrid to on-site and vice versa, etc)
  • HR compliance (employment laws, policies, frameworks)
  • Discrimination, gender equality, pay transparency
  • TUPE
  • Whistleblowing
  • Incentive plans, bonus schemes
  • Working time arrangements, including on-call, shift work and averaged or aggregated working hours
  • Settlement agreements
  • Enforcement of restrictive covenants
  • External counsel management
  • You'll be strategically and commercially aware with an appreciation of the business
  • Ability to build strong relationships with senior executives and stakeholders across the business

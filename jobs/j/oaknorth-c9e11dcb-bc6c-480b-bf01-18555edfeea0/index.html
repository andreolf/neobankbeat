Director, Portfolio Monitoring - Trading

LondonOtherHybrid

Apply on OakNorth →

ABOUT US At OakNorth, we're on a mission to empower ambitious businesses and the communities they serve. Since 2015, we've lent over $21 billion across the UK and US, helped create more than 58,000 new homes and 36,000 new jobs, and supported hundreds of thousands of personal savers — all while driving economic growth in the markets we serve.

ABOUT THE TEAM

The Portfolio Monitoring team is responsible for the ongoing management and surveillance of a complex, high-value lending book. We track performance, identify emerging risk and make sure the Bank has a clear, current view of its exposure. It's a hands-on team — close to the data and the clients, and expected to surface issues early and act on them. We work across leveraged finance, real estate and corporate lending. Our approach combines rigorous credit analysis with commercial judgement and direct engagement with borrowers and other stakeholders. We work closely with Credit, Workout & Restructuring, Legal and Operations, and increasingly use technology and AI to sharpen how we monitor and report.

ABOUT THE ROLE

We are looking for a commercially minded credit professional with experience in corporate or leveraged lending to join as a Director. You will take ownership of a portfolio of complex trading and leveraged finance credits, maintaining a forward-looking view of performance and acting early when risk emerges. You will understand the borrower, its business plan, capital structure and key stakeholders; challenge financial performance and forecasts; and determine what changes mean for the Bank's position. You will lead borrower discussions, assess requests for waivers and amendments, and present clear, evidence-based recommendations to senior management and Credit Committee.

This is not a reporting role. It calls for hands-on ownership, sound credit judgement and the confidence to challenge assumptions and stand behind recommendations. You will work closely with Credit, Workout & Restructuring, Legal and Operations, including on the timely escalation and handover of deteriorating credits where appropriate.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

Own and actively manage the portfolio

  • Take ownership of a portfolio of complex trading and leveraged finance credits, maintaining a clear view of each borrower's performance, financial position and emerging risks
  • Lead annual credit reviews, assessing business performance, management forecasts, risk grade and appetite, and recommending any action required
  • Challenge performance against the original underwriting assumptions and understand the borrower, its management team, sponsor, capital structure and potential exit or refinancing routes
  • Manage watchlist and early warning processes, identify deterioration promptly and agree appropriate monitoring, actions and escalation
  • Work with Workout & Restructuring and other colleagues when a credit deteriorates, ensuring a clear and timely handover where appropriate

Lead borrower engagement and credit decisions

  • Lead direct discussions with borrowers, management teams and sponsors, building relationships while confidently challenging performance and protecting the Bank's position
  • Own waiver, consent and amendment requests, assess their credit implications, negotiate terms and present clear recommendations for approval
  • Engage with co-lenders, agent banks, advisers and Legal as needed, understanding stakeholder positions and the Bank's rights under the lending documentation
  • Prepare and present well-structured credit papers and portfolio updates to senior management and Credit Committee, setting out the key facts, risks, options, recommendation and rationale
  • Follow through on agreed actions, decisions and escalation, ensuring issues are addressed rather than simply reported

Apply rigorous financial and portfolio analysis

  • Analyse and challenge financial statements, trading performance, cash flow and liquidity forecasts, leverage, debt service and covenant headroom
  • Run stress tests and downside scenarios to assess the impact of changes in performance, market conditions or macroeconomic events on individual credits and the portfolio
  • Assess risk ratings, sector and borrower concentrations, maturity pressures and refinancing risk, translating analysis into timely, practical recommendations
  • Understand lending documentation, security packages and capital structures, including relevant co-lender and intercreditor arrangements, and assess how they affect the Bank's position

Governance, controls and team leadership

  • Maintain accurate, current portfolio information and ensure appropriate governance, documentation, risk escalation and policy compliance
  • Oversee the effectiveness of lender controls, including covenant testing, information undertakings and consent rights, without owning routine loan administration
  • Monitor co-lender and agent bank performance against contractual reporting and consent obligations; identify control gaps and drive remediation with the relevant owners
  • Help develop portfolio monitoring frameworks, processes and scalable controls with Credit, Risk and Operations, using data and technology where they improve outcomes
  • Support and challenge junior colleagues, sharing credit expertise and setting high standards for analysis, judgement and follow-through

YOU'LL BE A GREAT FIT IF YOU HAVE…

  • A track record in portfolio monitoring, credit risk or leveraged finance within a bank or financial institution, with at least five years of relevant experience and personal accountability for complex credits
  • Strong technical credit and financial skills, including financial modelling, cash flow and liquidity analysis, covenant assessment and downside scenarios
  • Experience with corporate and leveraged lending documentation and capital structures, including the ability to understand the Bank's rights, security and position alongside other lenders
  • Sound commercial judgement: you can identify emerging risks, challenge assumptions, weigh options and make practical recommendations that balance credit discipline with commercial outcomes
  • Direct borrower or client engagement experience, with the confidence to challenge management, negotiate and hold a position in senior conversations
  • Strong communication and influencing skills, including experience writing credit papers and presenting risk assessments and recommendations to senior leadership or credit committees
  • Personal accountability and resilience: you're comfortable managing competing priorities, acting early on concerns and following decisions through
  • The seniority and judgement to operate at Director level, whether you're stepping into the title or already hold it

NICE TO HAVE

  • Exposure to AI or data tooling applied to credit monitoring or risk analytics
  • Experience across multiple asset classes or sectors within a leveraged or corporate lending context
  • Experience working with sponsor-backed borrowers, co-lenders or agent banks
  • Experience supporting and developing junior credit professionals

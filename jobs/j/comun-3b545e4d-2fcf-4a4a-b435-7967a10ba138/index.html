About Común Comun’s mission is to help Hispanic immigrants turn their hard work into upward mobility — starting with financial services that support their transition from a cash-based system to the digital economy. We offer checking accounts that can be opened using 100+ types of Latin American IDs, access to over 90K locations nationwide to deposit cash, international transfers to 17 countries at market-leading rates, and 24/7 bilingual customer support with <5 min SLA across email, chat, and phone.

We currently process over $1.5B in annual transaction volume and surpassed $100M in annualized international transfers just six months after launch. Comun has raised +$50M from top investors including Redpoint, Costanoa Ventures, and South Park Commons. Our team brings experience from leading fintech companies like Brex, Nubank, and Mercury. We believe immigrants are the ultimate entrepreneurs — driven by relentless ambition and a vision for a better future for their families. Comun exists to serve them throughout their journey.

About the Role

At Común, brand is trust. We serve a community that has historically been underserved by financial institutions — and for many of our users, Común will be their first experience with a brand that actually speaks to them. This role owns that first impression. We are looking for a Content Manager who will be the face of Común. You will create content that is clear, warm, and credible — appearing on camera, behind the keyboard, and across every channel where our community shows up. You are equally comfortable writing a punchy Instagram caption, scripting and filming a short-form video, and planning the production calendar that delivers assets on time.

Reporting to the Brand & Content Lead, you will translate our brand narrative into high-quality, high-volume content across formats. Rather than just experiment with AI, you treat these tools as essential to your craft — using them to move faster, maintain quality, and stay ahead of the content calendar. You are organized, resourceful, and deeply connected to the communities we serve. This role is based in New York City. Written and spoken fluency in both English and Spanish required

What You'll Do

  • Be the face of Común across social and video channels — scripting, filming, presenting, and publishing content that represents our brand with authenticity and warmth
  • Execute content across paid marketing and OOH formats – owning creation and delivery of assets varying across short-form and long-form video, static social posts, landing page copy, radio scripts, and OOH large format stills, among others
  • Own and manage the content production cycles — working with the Brand & Content Lead, prioritize creation learnings and own the production calendar to meet needs across product launches, seasonal moments, and paid media campaign iteration
  • Scale your output with AI – Use AI tools throughout your workflow to increase output, accelerate drafts, and maintain quality across a high volume of deliverables
  • Incorporate content data into your priorities — use insights across organic, paid media, and in-app channels to translate data signals into new content ideas
  • Triage content production – coordinate production needs across micro-influencers, designers, photographers, and agencies as required to ensure required assets are produce on-time, at high quality

You Are

  • On-Camera Confident. You are comfortable being the face of a brand on video — not just passable, but genuinely compelling. You know how to connect with an audience through a screen.
  • Video forward, but Multi-Format Fluent. Your creative and editing base is in video. You are at home editing, adding after effects, and polishing videos in programs like CapCut, but can adapt quickly between producing a TikTok script, a landing page headline, and a YouTube thumbnail brief without missing a beat. Format doesn't constrain you — it energizes you.
  • AI-Native. You actively use AI tools as a core part of your creative process. You see them as a multiplier, not a shortcut, and you know how to get great output from them.
  • Organized Under Pressure. You can manage a content calendar across multiple channels, juggle competing priorities, and hit deadlines in a fast-moving startup environment — without sacrificing quality.
  • Community-Connected. You have a deep connection to the Latin American immigrant experience in the US, which helps you base your content and tone in respect, care, credibility, and humor.
  • Data-Curious. You pay attention to what performs and why. You're comfortable pulling basic analytics, forming hypotheses, and adjusting your approach based on what the numbers tell you.
  • Collaborative by Default. You work closely with brand, product, growth, and operations teammates, and you raise the bar by giving and receiving direct, thoughtful feedback.
  • Owner. You show passion and care for your work and the company's trajectory. You take initiative, move with urgency, and hold yourself accountable to outcomes — not just outputs.

About the role and the job scope Based in our Vienna, Austria headquarters, the MiFID Compliance Officer is a critical regulatory role within our expanding European operations. You will act as the primary legal and compliance expert for investment services, ensuring our platform adheres strictly to European and Austrian capital markets regulations.

Your efforts will directly shape and safeguard our regulatory framework as we expand our service offerings across the EEA. You will be responsible for translating complex regulatory requirements into actionable internal policies, advising leadership, and maintaining the highest standards of market integrity. Key

Responsibilities

  • Establish, manage, and continuously improve a robust compliance framework in accordance with MiFID II and the Austrian Securities Supervision Act 2018 (WAG 2018).
  • Draft, implement, and maintain internal policies, procedures, and controls relevant to the Compliance area.
  • Act as the subject matter expert on investment services and activities under MiFID II / WAG 2018, providing regulatory guidance to internal teams.
  • Prepare and deliver clear, actionable reports on compliance topics to senior management and the Board.
  • Oversee and advise on complaints handling processes, ensuring alignment with relevant regulatory requirements and internal standards.
  • Monitor regulatory developments in European capital markets and proactively adapt internal frameworks to ensure ongoing compliance.

HR Ops Intern

TorontoOperationsHybridposted 20d ago

The Core Responsibilities of the Job

HR Administration & Data Management

  • Assist with maintaining employee records and HR documentation.
  • Support updates and data entry in HR systems.
  • Assist with employee lifecycle activities, including onboarding, job changes, and offboarding.
  • Support data audits and reporting to ensure information is accurate and up to date.
  • Assist with maintaining HR process documentation and resources.

Employee & Stakeholder Support

  • Respond to routine employee and manager inquiries and direct requests to the appropriate HR team member.
  • Assist with monitoring and managing requests received through the HR shared mailbox.
  • Provide administrative support to the HR team.
  • Support the delivery of a positive employee experience through timely and professional service.

Onboarding & Offboarding

  • Assist with onboarding and offboarding activities and employee communications.
  • Support new hire setup, orientation, and documentation requirements.
  • Prepare and maintain onboarding and offboarding checklists and tracking tools.
  • Support employee lifecycle administration related to hires, transfers, and departures.

Payroll & Benefits Support

  • Assist with maintaining employee information that supports payroll and benefits administration.
  • Support payroll reporting, audits, and data validation activities.
  • Respond to routine payroll and benefits inquiries or escalate as appropriate.
  • Assist with employee lifecycle changes that impact payroll and benefits.
  • Support payroll documentation and year-end activities as required.

HR Projects & Continuous Improvement

  • Support HR projects and initiatives through research, coordination, and administrative assistance.
  • Assist with preparing reports, trackers, and HR metrics.
  • Support process improvement initiatives and documentation updates.
  • Participate in team meetings, training sessions, and learning opportunities to build HR knowledge and skills.

Let's Talk About You!

  • Currently enrolled in a diploma or degree program in Human Resources, Business Administration, Commerce, or a related field.
  • Strong interest in Human Resources, Payroll, and employee experience.
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite, including Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills.
  • Strong interpersonal and customer service skills.
  • Ability to handle confidential employee information with professionalism and discretion.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a team environment.
  • Demonstrates initiative, curiosity, and a willingness to learn.
  • Previous administrative, customer service, volunteer, or co-op experience is considered an asset.

About Común Comun’s mission is to help Hispanic immigrants turn their hard work into upward mobility — starting with financial services that support their transition from a cash-based system to the digital economy. We offer checking accounts that can be opened using 100+ types of Latin American IDs, access to over 90K locations nationwide to deposit cash, international transfers to 17 countries at market-leading rates, and 24/7 bilingual customer support with <5 min SLA across email, chat, and phone.

We currently process over $1.5B in annual transaction volume and surpassed $100M in annualized international transfers just six months after launch. Comun has raised +$50M from top investors including Redpoint, Costanoa Ventures, and South Park Commons. Our team brings experience from leading fintech companies like Brex, Nubank, and Mercury. We believe immigrants are the ultimate entrepreneurs — driven by relentless ambition and a vision for a better future for their families. Comun exists to serve them throughout their journey.

About the role

As a Product Manager at Comun you will be responsible for driving product development and ensuring that our products align with our business goals and customer needs. This role involves strategic planning, execution, and continuous improvement to create a seamless and impactful user experience.

What you’ll do

  • Product management: You’ll be the project owner of projects of different scope at Comun. You’ll be responsible for coordinating all relevant Comun team members and external vendors.
  • Prioritize and build the roadmap: You’ll be part of the prioritization conversations at Comun, helping build and bring clarity to our roadmap.
  • Conduct User interviews: You will be responsible for gathering and synthesizing customer insights, through surveys and interviews. These insights will be used to inform our roadmap and product execution.
  • Data analysis: Navigate our data tools to draw and communicate conclusions that are used as inputs in the product roadmap.

You are

  • Strategic Thinker: You can see the big picture and align product goals with company strategy. You make data-driven decisions that balance short-term wins with long-term vision.
  • Customer-Centric: You can build a deep understanding of our users and their needs. You use user feedback and market research to drive product decisions and create solutions that resonate with our target audience.
  • Collaborative Leader: You build strong relationships across teams, fostering collaboration and ensuring all voices are heard. You excel in managing project stakeholders and timelines through clear and effective communication.
  • Results-Oriented: You have a track record of successfully managing product launches and delivering projects on time and within budget. You set measurable success metrics and continuously seek ways to improve product performance.
  • Innovative Problem Solver: You thrive in ambiguity and can bring clarity to complex situations. You use creative thinking and relevant frameworks to develop innovative solutions to challenging problems.
  • Data-Driven: You independently navigate datasets to draw actionable insights. You use data to prioritize and advocate for product changes that drive business outcomes.
  • Adaptable and Resilient: You are flexible and can quickly adapt to changing priorities. You see setbacks as opportunities to learn and grow, displaying resilience in the face of challenges.
  • Owner: You show passion and care for your work and the company's trajectory. You set ambitious personal and professional goals, bringing energy and dedication to your role.
  • Resourceful: You can figure out solutions independently and leverage your network to employ best practices. You are constantly seeking ways to uplevel and raise the bar for yourself and the team.

Requirements

  • 3+ years of experience as a startup Product Manager
  • A critical thinker and problem solver with appetite to take ownership where needed
  • An interest in serving an underserved community :)

Team We are a team of 40 based out of New York, coming from industry-leading companies like Brex, Nubank, Cruise, and Verkada. Over half of us are immigrants, and have experienced the problems we’re solving first hand. We value customer focus, high ambition, principled decision-making, and deep trust.

marketing & growth · Qonto

Head of Video and Storytelling

ParisMarketing & GrowthHybridposted 5d ago

Our mission and customers: We are creating the freedom for SMEs to succeed by delivering Europe's leading finance workspace with banking at its core, augmented by financial tools. We are proud to be rated 4.8 on Trustpilot, based on 55,000+ reviews. Our culture puts customer satisfaction at the core of what we do, as proven by our Net Promoter Score of 75 (more about our culture here). Our journey: Founded in 2017 by Alexandre and Steve, Qonto has grown to 1,700 Qontoers serving over 750,000 customers across 8 European countries. We have been profitable since 2023, and we are just getting started.

Our beliefs: We hire for skills and potential. With 80+ nationalities, 45% women, of which 56% of women in our leadership team, diversity isn't a program; It's who we are. We've built a discrimination-free hiring process because the best teams are built on merit. AI at Qonto: AI is deeply embedded in how we work (here) - Every Qontoer gets unlimited access to the best AI tools. We want people who experiment without waiting for permission, push AI beyond the obvious, know when to trust it, and when to question it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Join us as Head of Video & Storytelling and build the video engine behind Qonto’s brand. You’ll report to Julie Regis, our Brand & Communications Director. Today, most of our videos start as a one-off request and end after one shoot. Your job is to change that. Every shoot should become five or more finished videos. Our best series should become recognisable formats that people look for. Everything we publish on YouTube and LinkedIn should be something people remember and something search engines and AI assistants can find and quote.

➡️ What you’ll do Decide what gets made. You’ll priorotise requests against the brand strategy, evaluate every month what we can output communicate limits to stakeholders. Turn one shoot into many videos. You’ll run the system that makes this routine: dedicated editing and motion design, and tools for reframing, clipping, subtitling and AI voice localization. You’ll also rework our workflows with AI-assisted production. Build series that belong to Qonto. Business Finance Academy, Product Drop and The Industry Numbers are the start. You’ll give each one a clear idea, a recognisable look and a fixed format, so our videos feel like Qonto in all six markets.

Raise the standard for scripts and copy. You’ll manage our copywriters, from scripts and hooks to captions and transcripts. You’ll decide when a script deserves real craft time and when a template does the job. Work with outside producers. You’ll set up a trusted bench of studios and freelancers, with clear briefs and quality checks. You’ll also give each market one contact for planning and prioritising. ➡️ What we’re looking for Real craft in video. You’ve made or shaped video work you’re proud of, and you can explain why it’s good. You keep that standard when time is short.

Experience managing creative people. You’ve led producers, editors or copywriters, ideally a team of around eight, and you can balance delivering work with building better tools and processes. A track record with recurring formats. You’ve built series, characters or sets that audiences came back to, and you’ve kept them consistent across markets or languages. A feel for both audiences. You know how to make a video people remember and how to make it easy for search engines and AI assistants to find and cite.

A clear head under pressure. You’re comfortable with numbers like lead times and capacity, and you can turn down a senior stakeholder with good reasons while keeping their trust. ➡️ What we can offer you A team that’s ready for a new direction. Eight producers and copywriters, with a model to move from one-off requests to formats that build on each other. Clear goals. Videos delivered within 30 days for time-sensitive launches, at least five finished videos per shoot, and a growing share of outside production. We’ll also track how often our flagship series are cited by AI assistants.

Two channels, six markets. YouTube in five markets, adapted locally, and LinkedIn run centrally. You’ll define how Qonto looks, sounds and feels in motion. A voice in the Creative Factory. You’ll work alongside Brand Design and the other creative teams, and bring video’s craft standards to the whole group. Direct access to decision-makers. When demand outgrows capacity, you’ll raise the trade-offs with senior leadership, with the data to back them up. At Qonto, we understand that true diversity isn’t just about ticking boxes on a hiring checklist. Apply regardless of the boxes you tick — who knows? You may have the missing piece of the puzzle we’ve been searching for all along.

By applying, you agree that Qonto processes your personal data to assess your application. Your data is kept for up to 2 years in our candidate pool.

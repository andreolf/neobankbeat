CHIEF OF STAFF TO THE CHRO

ABOUT THE ROLE

The Chief of Staff to the CHRO is the operational and strategic backbone of the People & Culture organization. Working closely with the CHRO and the P&C Leadership Team (PLT), this person owns the team's reporting and operating rhythm and leads selected strategic initiatives. They bring independent judgment to consequential people and organizational decisions and serve as a trusted partner to P&C leaders and senior stakeholders across Nubank. This is a broad role. It combines governance, strategic planning, and business analysis, and it is designed as an entry point to other roles in the organization. Candidates are evaluated both for the Chief of Staff role itself and for their potential to take on broader responsibilities over time.

This is an individual contributor role built on ownership and trust. We expect someone who anticipates needs, brings structure to ambiguous problems, and moves work forward with a high degree of autonomy. They build alignment across teams, challenge assumptions constructively, and know when to bring a trade-off or risk to the CHRO. The role requires sound judgment and discretion in handling sensitive people and organizational matters. YOU'LL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR

  • Own the P&C operating rhythm. Connect P&C strategic priorities to planning cycles, leadership forums, business reviews, and ensures a focused CHRO agenda, continuously reassessing what deserves her attention. Define what gets reported, to whom, and at what cadence.
  • Run the P&C management routines. Run the PLT forums and rituals with clear purposes, the right participants, and documented outcomes. Ensure agendas, pre-reads, and follow-ups are delivered on time. Continuously evolve how these forums work.
  • Shape priorities across the P&C portfolio. Maintain a clear view of major P&C initiatives across teams. Anticipate dependencies, identify overlaps and gaps, and bring leaders together to resolve trade-offs across capacity, risk, and business impact.
  • Bring independent judgment to CHRO and PLT decisions. Frame the decision, challenge assumptions, and identify the options and evidence leaders need to consider. Set standards for how data is validated and how recommendations are presented. Design the alignment process for complex decisions, surface disagreements early, and guide discussions toward resolution and clear ownership.
  • Drive follow-through on decisions and planning performance. Maintain a clear record of decisions, owners, and deadlines. Track P&C metrics against targets and budget, investigate material gaps, and recommend actions. Work with owners to resolve blockers and escalate risks early. Partner with Data, and People Analytics to keep P&C metrics reliable.
  • Build the Workforce Planning cycle. Help design the model, its inputs and governance, and run the planning in partnership with Finance, Talent Acquisition and the business units.
  • Leading short-cycle strategic projects. Pick up topics such as organizational design, AI initiatives, P&C operating model and ad-hoc leadership priorities. Get up to speed quickly, clarify the problem and deliver in weeks, not months;
  • Lead CHRO-sponsored initiatives. Drive a focused set of cross-functional initiatives where ownership is unclear. Define measurable outcomes, run the implementation, and track impact.
  • Partner with other areas of the organization. Build trust with other Chiefs of Staff and senior leaders, building trust and speaking their language. Work with Internal Communications to cascade P&C priorities and decisions.

WE ARE LOOKING FOR A PERSON WHO HAS PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE & EDUCATION

  • Previous experience in management consulting, strategy, business operations, people analytics or an executive-adjacent role in a large, complex organization.
  • Business analyst capabilities, with strong structured problem-solving and diagnostic skills.
  • Experience operating alongside and influencing senior leaders.
  • Track record of running reporting, planning or governance cycles independently.
  • Knowledge of Workforce Planning (headcount modeling, demand and supply forecasting, scenario analysis, budget linkage) is desirable.
  • Familiarity with People Analytics and HR data (e.g. Visier or similar tools) is a plus.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication, able to frame complex material for executive decision-making.
  • Advanced Excel / Google Sheets and PowerPoint / Slides (or equivalent); SQL or data visualization tools are an advantage.
  • Fluent English; Spanish or Portuguese is an advantage.

COMPETENCES & ATTRIBUTES

  • Exceptionally disciplined and rigorous: Operates with rigor in everything from analysis to meeting preparation, closes the loop on every commitment, and delivers on time.
  • Learning Agility: Learns fast, structures complex and ambiguous problems. Strong prioritization and autonomous execution across multiple concurrent workstreams;
  • Strong relationship builder: Builds trusting relationships quickly with senior leaders and handles demanding stakeholders with maturity.
  • High ownership and a high bar: Moves work forward without being pushed, and challenges the PLT respectfully when needed.
  • Discretion: Treats confidential information, especially on people, compensation and organizational topics, with absolute care.
  • Analytical mindset: Comfortable turning data into clear recommendations.

Director, Legal - Product & Regulatory

KOHO (CAN)Compliance & RiskRemote only$220–260K

ABOUT KOHO We’re on a mission to make financial services better for every Canadian. That means no hidden fees, no predatory interest rates - just financial products designed to help our users spend smart, save more, and build real wealth. We’re a performance organization with a strong heart: we care deeply about outcomes, and everything ties back to our mission - to financially empower a generation of Canadians. At KOHO, we’re not your average 9-5. We believe real impact comes from people who are trusted, empowered, and supported to do their best work - without sacrificing their lives to do it. We prioritize work-life integration, not just work-life balance. That means asynchronous collaboration, flexible hours, and a remote-first setup built around autonomy and high trust.

KOHO is entering its next chapter - leaner, smarter, more AI-integrated. We’re building for impact, not bureaucracy. If you thrive in environments that value clarity, ownership, and bold thinking, you’ll fit right in.

About The Role

As Director, Legal – Product and Regulatory, you will lead legal support for KOHO’s product development and regulatory strategy across both KOHO Tech and KOHO Bank (letters patent pending) You will work closely with Product, Technology, Marketing, Risk, and Compliance teams to provide practical legal advice that enables innovation while protecting customers and maintaining strong regulatory standards. This role is ideal for a lawyer who enjoys rolling up the sleeves and building new things, solving complex regulatory questions, and taking ownership of outcomes in a fast-moving, flat organization.

What You’ll Do

  • Partner across KOHO Tech and KOHO Bank (letters patent pending) to provide practical regulatory advice that enables growth while protecting customers.
  • Support the design and launch of new financial products and features, identifying regulatory risks early and helping teams build compliant solutions.
  • Play a key role in helping KOHO establish and operationalize a regulated bank, including supporting engagement with regulators such as OSFI, FCAC, and the Bank of Canada.
  • Support Marketing and Growth teams by ensuring campaigns are effective and compliant with financial services and consumer protection requirements.
  • Provide guidance to customer-facing teams on complaint handling, communications, and fairness considerations.
  • Help build scalable legal and regulatory processes to support KOHO’s long-term growth as a regulated financial institution.
  • Support the development of knowledge management for Legal.
  • Mentor other members of the Legal team and represent Legal in cross-functional and leadership forums.

What You’ll Bring

Experience

  • 10+ years of post-call experience as a lawyer practicing in Canada (in-house and/or at a law firm), with substantial in-house experience.
  • Direct experience advising on the launch of retail banking products, as well as related financial services regulation, banking law, and consumer protection frameworks.
  • Demonstrated ability to operate with significant autonomy and deliver practical, business-oriented legal advice to senior stakeholders.
  • A track record of leading complex legal or regulatory initiatives with minimal oversight.

Ways of Working

  • High-agency and entrepreneurial: You take ownership, figure things out, and move problems forward without waiting for perfect instructions or established processes.
  • Commercial and pragmatic: You understand that Legal’s job is to help the business achieve its goals while making smart, risk-calibrated decisions (not simply identify what could go wrong).
  • Curious and solutions-oriented: You enjoy getting into the weeds of how products and technology actually work, asking the right questions, and finding creative paths through ambiguity.
  • A builder: You don’t just solve the problem in front of you. You also look for ways to turn recurring problems into better processes, playbooks, tools, and systems.
  • Low-ego, high self-awareness: You’re confident in your judgment but genuinely open to being challenged, changing your mind, and learning from others.
  • Comfortable with ambiguity and speed: You can make progress with incomplete information, prioritize effectively, and operate calmly when the roadmap changes.
  • Collaborative and influential: You build strong relationships with Product, Compliance, Risk, and other teams and can bring people along without relying on hierarchy or formal authority.
  • Technically curious: You’re excited by technology and AI and look for ways to use them to make legal work and business processes more effective.

Nice to Have's

  • Experience working at both a large bank and a smaller tech startup or fintech, ideally through a bank charter or launch process.
  • Experience engaging directly with regulators.
  • Lobbying or government relations experience.
  • Familiarity with the RPAA and the Financial Consumer Protection Framework (FCPF).
  • AML advisory experience at a bank.
  • Experience implementing privacy laws.
  • Experience mentoring or managing junior legal counsel.
  • Fluent in French.

The budgeted salary range for this role is $220,000 - 260,000 CAD / year. At KOHO, we are dedicated to providing pay transparency to all candidates. Compensation at KOHO is determined through various factors including but not limited to: comparable salary market data within Canada, technical skill assessment, a holistic view of previous work history, and internal pay equity with other KOHO team members.

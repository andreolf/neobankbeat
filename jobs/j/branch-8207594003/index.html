the role

Every risk control at Branch, whether for fraud, financial crime, disputes or collections, answers two questions. The first is detection: should this user, login or transaction be flagged? The second is treatment: once it is flagged, what happens to the user, how quickly, and how does a legitimate user get back to normal? Our rules team owns the first question. This role owns the second. As Risk Controls Manager, you own the control paths users move through once a control fires: step-up verification, holds, locks, review queues, unlock and recovery, and the handoff to offboarding. You decide who goes down each path, when, and on what exit criteria, across onboarding, login and account takeover, money movement and card activity, for fraud, financial crime, disputes and collections alike, including how Branch treats scams and first-party fraud. You also build and run Branch's User Risk Score, the user-level view that decides which treatment each user gets. Then you make the changes happen.

The role exists to close the gap between a strategy decision and the frontline. You will take ideas from the Enablement Director and carry them through Product, Engineering, Operations and our BPO partner until they are live, measured and understood by the people working the queues. This is an individual contributor role with program ownership. You will not have direct reports.

Responsibilities

Own the Control Paths and User Risk Score

  • Map and document every control path Branch uses today: the trigger, the treatment, the owner, the service level and the exit criteria for each
  • Set the standard for which treatment applies to which risk, so similar risks get similar treatment across products and channels
  • Keep the map current as rules, products and vendors change, and make it the reference Operations, Support and Product work from
  • Document each control path, with its rationale, approvals and change history, to a standard our partner banks, internal audit and examiners can follow, and support partner bank reviews, audits and exams of the control environment, working with Compliance
  • Build and run Branch's User Risk Score: a lifetime view of each user that starts at enrollment and moves with behavior, money movement, payouts and account changes. You own the factors, weights, risk bands and the treatment each band receives; the Enablement Director owns the score's results and approves material changes
  • Combine existing signals (device and login intelligence, transaction monitoring, disbursement and payout models, organization risk classification, dispute and collections outcomes) into a single user-level view, working with the Manager of Risk Rules and Monitoring and Data
  • Measure and tune the score on capture rate, false positive rate and approval-rate cost, and feed confirmed fraud back into it, so every user removed changes how the next one is scored
  • Set the identity verification and step-up standards for phone and chat contacts, including unlocks and ATO recovery, and close the social engineering gaps the Manager of Risk and Recovery Contact Operations surfaces from live contacts

Change Management Operationalization

  • Use loss, friction and operational data to find the paths that cost the most in loss, user friction or manual work, and redesign them
  • Write the requirements for each change, sequence it with Product and Engineering, and run it from design through testing to live
  • Before a change goes live, map its upstream and downstream impacts on disputes, collections, Support and other control paths, so a fix in one place does not create loss, friction or volume in another
  • Measure each change after launch and report whether it did what it was meant to do
  • For every control change, update the operational side before it goes live: queue design, SOPs, BPO procedures and agent guidance, working with the Managers of Risk and Recovery Program Operations and the BPO partner
  • Write procedures that meet audit and partner bank requirements and that an agent can still follow in the middle of a live queue
  • Brief Operations on what changed and why, so the frontline understands the controls it works with
  • Consult on improvements that start inside Operations; those remain with the Managers of Risk and Recovery Program Operations

Protect Good Users

  • Own the friction legitimate users absorb from fraud controls: step-up pass and drop-off rates, time to unlock or resolve, and the volume of escalations controls create
  • Design recovery paths for account takeover victims and good users caught by a control, in partnership with Support
  • Track loss on users released from controls, so friction reductions are not paid for in fraud

Scams and First-Party Fraud

  • Design the treatment paths for scams: warnings and added friction on high-risk outbound payments (P2P, Branch Direct, instant transfer), protective holds when a user appears to be acting under a scammer's direction, and recovery paths for scam victims, in partnership with Support
  • Build first-party fraud factors into the User Risk Score, such as repeat or unsupported disputes, bust-out patterns, negative-balance abuse and payback failures. Set the treatment each band receives, from limits to dispute scrutiny to offboarding
  • Apply Branch's scam and first-party fraud definitions consistently across control paths. Set the standard, with Disputes Operations and Compliance, for separating authorized scam payments from unauthorized transfers at the handoff to disputes

Onboarding Guardrails

  • Bring Risk Recovery's control requirements to the cross-functional onboarding review led by the User Onboarding Product Manager, who owns the onboarding experience end to end
  • Model the risk and conversion tradeoff of proposed step-up and verification changes; Product makes the final call, and agreed tradeoffs are documented

What We're Looking For

Required

  • 4 to 6+ years in fraud strategy, fraud operations, account takeover or customer risk at a financial institution, fintech or payments company
  • Experience designing or changing fraud control flows: step-up authentication, holds, locks, review and recovery
  • Experience designing or owning a customer-level risk score or risk rating model: factor selection, weighting, banding and treatment design
  • A record of taking process and product changes from design to adoption across Product, Engineering and Operations
  • Able to size exposure and measure friction, false positives and loss from data; SQL and BI fluency
  • Working knowledge of Regulation E as it applies to unauthorized transfers, account takeover and scam claims, including the authorized versus unauthorized distinction
  • Clear writing; able to turn a control design into requirements an engineer can build and an SOP an agent can follow
  • Comfort operating with program accountability and no direct reports

Nice to Have

  • Experience with SEON, Persona, device intelligence or real-time card decisioning platforms
  • Experience in a BPO-supported operating model
  • Working knowledge of NACHA rules and Mastercard dispute and chargeback rules as they apply to ACH, debit card and push-to-card activity
  • Experience supporting partner bank oversight, internal audit or regulatory examinations of fraud controls
  • Exposure to earned wage access, gig or staffing platforms, or employer-verified onboarding
  • Experience designing protective treatment for scam and account takeover victims, or first-party fraud controls such as dispute abuse and bust-out
  • CFE or comparable industry certification

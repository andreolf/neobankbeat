Regulatory Compliance Manager

Sydney, AUCompliance & RiskHybridposted today

Apply on Wise →

Since 2011, we’ve had a clear mission: Money without Borders. Built by and for people who live global lives, we’re the fairest, easiest way to manage your money across borders. We’re just at the beginning of our story and we’re growing at an incredible pace. We won’t stop until anyone, anywhere can send, spend and receive money wherever they are, whatever they’re doing. There’s still heaps to do and we can’t do it alone. We’re looking for a Regulatory Compliance Manager to join our growing Compliance Team. Your primary role will be to help manage our Australian Financial Services Licences (AFSL) across our two Australian entities covering both payments and investments while supporting cross-border marketing compliance in New Zealand. You will also play a hands-on role in supporting new product launches, offering a huge scope to grow your remit and progress within the team.

You’ll be primarily responsible for

  • Maintaining our AFSL, including having primary oversight of procedures and processes to meet all our Australian Financial Services Licence (AFSL) obligations and the key contact for the Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC).
  • Providing Regulatory Compliance guidance specifically in respect to our AFSL and Corporations Act obligations to all functional areas of the Pacific tribe to empower the tribe to implement new products that meet our regulatory requirements.
  • Building upon our existing horizon scanning and regulatory change management program, ensuring effective communications of regulatory changes to the business as well as timely implementations in line with our mission.
  • Enhance our regulatory compliance control framework for growth in the Pacific, including enhancement of the regulatory risk (non financial crime) measurement and monitoring tools, preparing management information for reporting to the local Risk Committee and Board.
  • Regulatory training based on needs assessment with areas carrying concentrated regulatory risk, and created for functional teams.

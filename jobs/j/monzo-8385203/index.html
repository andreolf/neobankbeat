Interim Director of Financial Operations & Control

Cardiff, London or Remote (UK)OperationsRemote only£142–170Kposted 1d ago

🚀 We’re on a mission to make money work for everyone. We’re waving goodbye to the complicated and confusing ways of traditional banking. After starting as a prepaid card, our product offering has grown a lot in the last 10 years in the UK. As well as personal and business bank accounts, we offer joint accounts, accounts for 16-17 year olds, a free kids account and credit cards in the UK, with more exciting things to come beyond. Our UK customers can also save, invest and combine their pensions with us.

With our hot coral cards and get-paid-early feature, combined with financial education on social media and our award winning customer service, we have a long history of creating magical moments for our customers! We’re not about selling products - we want to solve problems and change lives through Monzo ❤️ 📍London / UK Remote | 💰£142,000 - £170,000 + Benefits ✨ Our Financial Control team ⭐ We are seeking an experienced finance operations leader for a 12-month fixed-term contract to lead our Global Financial Operations and Control team. The incumbent Director is stepping out of day-to-day operations to spearhead a strategic, multi-year finance automation and systems program.

Your mandate is to provide steady-state operational leadership across all financial operations, close cycles, and management reporting; and serve as the essential operational contact for the program. You will ensure BAU integrity while actively operationalising and absorbing newly automated processes. 🔑 You’ll play a key role by...

  • Leading all aspects of financial control and operations, including Accounts Payable, Treasury Operations/Product Control, Money Operations ensuring robust, well controlled, and timely transaction processing.
  • Leading the production and end-to-end delivery of monthly close and management reporting cycle, working across Finance to provide the Group Financial Controller, UK CFO and EU CFOs with actionable insights and variance analysis.
  • Maintaining a robust, control and governance framework focused on integrity of our data and processes.
  • Serving as the primary operational counterpart and sounding board to the Director of Financial Operations Control (incumbent) within the programme, providing operational feedback on target operating models and workflow designs.
  • Managing team capacity and workflows to free up key subject matter experts for involvement in the programme, including (not limited to) User Acceptance Testing, parallel runs, and systems testing.
  • Supporting the practical change management and operational rollout of new automation tools within the live environment, establishing new standard operating procedures without interrupting daily processing.
  • Working closely with the Director of Financial Regulatory Reporting to ensure core financial operations and control processes comply with overarching accounting policies and provide a seamless hand off into the consolidation.
  • Providing stable, empathetic, and accountable leadership to a distributed team during a period of change, maintaining high engagement, retention, and performance.
  • Building and maintaining effective working relationships with internal stakeholders, external auditors and advisors.
  • Driving the delivery of the Financial Operations and Control teams milestones and actions on existing key projects such as EU expansion, working with stakeholders to identify and resolve delivery risks.
  • Managing a structured, documented operational handover back to permanent leadership at the conclusion of the 12-month term.

Role Scope Clarification

  • Included: Global financial operations and control process ownership, including delivery of those services to subsidiaries, Treasury operations/Product control, Money Operations, Month-end Close and related management reporting.
  • Excluded: Group Consolidation and the preparation/filing of Statutory Financial Statements for the Group and subsidiaries; Regulatory Reporting.

🤩 We’d love to hear from you if…

  • You have a demonstrated track record stepping into high volume, multi-entity finance operations leadership roles and delivering immediate stability within regulated environments (Fintech, Banking, or Financial Services preferred).
  • You’re an empathetic, effective people leader with a strong track record of coaching individuals and fostering a positive, high-performing environment.
  • You have a deep understanding of internal control environments and the ability to build "best-in-class" processes.
  • You have experience operating within complex, multi-entity or cross-border structures where data consistency is paramount.
  • You have demonstrated experience operationalising new technology, and embedding change within Finance teams.
  • Have excellent stakeholder management skills, with the ability to explain operational complexities to key stakeholders and peers.
  • You are adaptability and resilience in fast-paced, tech-led, and agile environments.
  • You hold strong technical accounting knowledge (IFRS)

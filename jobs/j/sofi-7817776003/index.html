The role

We are looking for licensed insurance professionals to act as the frontline champions of our insurance agency. In this role, you will serve as the go-to guide for our users as they navigate their insurance protection journeys. You will own the entire post-purchase experience - troubleshooting complex billing issues, processing endorsements, adjusting coverage on the fly, spotting upsell opportunities, and saving accounts at risk of churning. If you are a naturally empathetic problem-solver who loves insurance and wants to help users get the most value out of our all-in-one platform, we want to hear from you.

What you’ll do

  • Engage potential and current members via phone, email, text, and chat to assist with their insurance needs
  • Respond quickly to customer needs helping to resolve inquires quickly and efficiently
  • Help collect post-bind documentation to ensure customer policies are handled properly
  • Navigate 20+ carrier portals to adjust coverages with high accuracy
  • Receive customer service inquiries from a variety of sources helping our agents focus on selling
  • Monitor the book of business to identify retention opportunities
  • Review renewals to ensure customers have the most up to date information
  • Identify cross-sell opportunities and help close any coverage gaps.

We are a small team looking for someone who wants to be collaborative, identify problems, and have a voice in making the business better.

What you’ll need

  • 2+ years of experience selling Property Casualty Insurance
  • Must have valid state active licenses in Property Casualty or Personal Lines insurance
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Proven interpersonal and sales skills with a track record of meeting or exceeding sales goals are highly preferred
  • Ability to multitask and stay organized to meet deadlines on time
  • Ability to analyze members' needs and provide a tailored insurance solution
  • Self-motivated, resourceful, and flexible with the ability to take an entrepreneurial approach to problem-solving

Nice to have

  • Independent agency experience working with broad range of insurance carriers
  • Life insurance experience

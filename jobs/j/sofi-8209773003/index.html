The role

We are seeking an experienced, technically proficient Lead Onboarding and Account Manager to own complex client implementations and serve as a trusted technical and functional advisor to business banking clients. This role combines strategic account leadership, complex implementation management, and operational expertise. The ideal candidate has experience supporting organizations with complex structures, multiple legal entities, high transaction values, and demanding technical or regulatory requirements. Serving as a trusted client partner, they will coordinate internal teams and mentor relationship managers and cash management implementation specialists. This position requires a strong blend of business banking experience, technical aptitude, and exceptional communication skills to translate complex banking requirements into scalable solutions, guide clients through integration capabilities, and oversee sophisticated, multi-stakeholder implementations.

As a lead within client success and account management, this role owns the end-to-end implementation lifecycle for strategic clients, and serves as account support for large and complex clients. Partnering closely with Product, Engineering, Operations, and Client Success, they deliver high-quality implementations on time, drive continuous feedback loops, and apply critical thinking to resolve complex client issues.

What you’ll do

Implementation Leadership

  • Lead end-to-end implementations for complex business banking clients, involving multiple accounts, legal entities, currencies, products, systems, and stakeholders.
  • Develop comprehensive implementation plans based on client’s complex needs, timelines, milestones, resource models, and risk mitigation strategies from discovery and requirements gathering, through configuration, integration, testing, launch, and post implementation stabilization.
  • Anticipate implementation risks, product and client requirement gaps, resolve blockers, manage dependencies, and escalate issues with clear recommendations and actionable solutions.
  • Manage multiple concurrent implementations with varying levels of technical complexity, stakeholder involvement, and business criticality.
  • Ensure implementations meet defined quality standards, business objectives, client expectations, and operational readiness requirements.

Business Banking Product Technical Expertise

  • Hold a deep understanding of business banking products, workflows, capabilities, and technical architecture to guide clients through ongoing account needs.
  • Explain product functionality, system behavior, technical dependencies, and integration requirements in clear, practical terms to both technical and nontechnical audiences.
  • Serve as a subject matter expert on business banking product capabilities, technical considerations, and best practices.
  • Manage difficult conversations, competing priorities, scope changes, and escalations with professionalism and a solutions-oriented mindset.
  • Ensure clients are equipped to operate, support, and maximize the value of implemented products after launch.
  • Establish strong post-implementation governance, including operating procedures, service expectations, issue management, and ongoing account needs.

Cross-Functional Leadership Continuous Improvement

  • Represent client needs internally and ensure product limitations, recurring implementation challenges, and enhancement opportunities are communicated effectively.
  • Act as a player/coach to team members on technical problem-solving, client communication, project execution, and implementation best practices.
  • Develop and improve implementation playbooks, technical documentation, requirements templates, testing frameworks, and escalation processes.
  • Identify opportunities to standardize workflows, reduce implementation timelines, improve quality, and increase delivery scalability.
  • Share lessons learned across implementations to strengthen organizational knowledge and improve the overall client experience.

What you’ll need

  • 8+ years of experience in cash management, treasury services, banking, payments, fintech, financial technology, or a related field.
  • 7+ years of experience in implementation management, technical project or program management, solutions delivery, or client-facing technology roles, preferably within business banking, commercial banking, payments, fintech, or financial services.
  • Experience with complex clients including stablecoin issuers, exchanges, infrastructure providers, institutional investors, software developers, etc.
  • Demonstrated experience leading complex, end-to-end implementations involving multiple systems, technical integrations, cross-functional teams, and external client stakeholders.
  • Strong understanding of the end-to-end lifecycle of business banking transactions and workflows, including account onboarding, payments, money movement, transaction processing, reporting, and reconciliation, as applicable to the platform.
  • Proven technical aptitude, including the ability to understand system architecture, APIs, data flows, integration specifications, and technical dependencies.
  • Ability to interpret technical documentation and partner effectively with software engineers, solution architects, and client technology teams.
  • Ability to lead with confidence, act autonomously while being an exceptional partner with transparency, and run after problems
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain complex technical concepts to both technical and business audiences.
  • Demonstrated ability to manage competing priorities, dependencies, risks, scope changes, and escalations across multiple concurrent engagements.
  • Strong executive presence and the ability to lead client meetings, facilitate decisions, and influence stakeholders without direct authority.
  • Experience coordinating integration testing, user acceptance testing, issue resolution, and production launches.
  • Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills, with a high degree of ownership and accountability.

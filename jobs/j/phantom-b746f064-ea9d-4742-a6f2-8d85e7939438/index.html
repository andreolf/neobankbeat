Release Management Engineer, Mobile

Remote-USEngineeringRemote only

Phantom https://phantom.com is the modern money app used by tens of millions around the world. Our product combines everything people need to manage, spend, and grow their money in one simple, intuitive experience. Phantom brings all the control and flexibility of crypto-powered finance, without unnecessary complexity, into mainstream consumer finance. By combining industry-leading security with thoughtfully designed tools, Phantom makes powerful financial tools feel approachable and easy to use. This focus on speed, control, and user experience has fueled rapid growth, with a community of 20M+ people, reaching #1 in the Google Play Store finance category, and consistently ranking as a Top 50 app across all categories, alongside some of the most recognizable consumer technology platforms in the world.

Phantom is used by millions of people to access crypto applications and financial services. We’re looking for a Release Management Engineer to own and continuously improve the release process for our iOS and Android applications. You will work closely with engineering, QA, product, and support teams to make releases predictable, efficient, and safe. This is a hands-on role for someone who understands mobile build and deployment systems and enjoys coordinating complex releases across teams.

This role is remote; however, we’re only open to candidates working in US time zones.

Responsibilities

  • Own the end-to-end release process for Phantom’s iOS and Android applications.
  • Manage release schedules, release branches, versioning, build promotion, and app store submissions.
  • Coordinate release readiness across engineering, QA, product, and other stakeholders.
  • Lead go/no-go decisions and ensure risks, blockers, and outstanding issues are clearly communicated.
  • Manage TestFlight, App Store Connect, Google Play Console, and staged or phased rollouts.
  • Coordinate hotfixes, emergency releases, and release rollback plans when needed.
  • Monitor release health, including crashes, regressions, rollout status, and user-impacting issues.
  • Troubleshoot build, signing, submission, and deployment failures.
  • Improve and automate repetitive release tasks using tools such as GitHub Actions, Fastlane, Bash, or Python.
  • Maintain clear release documentation, checklists, runbooks, and status reporting.
  • Ensure releases comply with Apple and Google policies and submission requirements.
  • Partner with engineering teams to improve the reliability and reproducibility of mobile builds.

Qualifications

  • 3+ years of experience in release engineering, mobile DevOps, build engineering, or a similar role.
  • Hands-on experience releasing production iOS and Android applications.
  • Strong knowledge of App Store Connect, TestFlight, Google Play Console, mobile signing, provisioning, and certificates.
  • Experience working with mobile CI/CD systems such as GitHub Actions, Bitrise, CircleCI, Buildkite, or similar tools.
  • Experience managing release branches, versioning, hotfixes, and staged rollouts.
  • Ability to troubleshoot mobile build and deployment issues.
  • Familiarity with scripting or automation
  • Strong organizational, communication, and cross-functional coordination skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and make clear decisions during time-sensitive releases or incidents.

Nice to Have

  • Experience with React Native applications.
  • Experience with Fastlane or other mobile release automation tools.
  • Experience supporting a high-scale consumer mobile application.
  • Familiarity with mobile QA, automated testing, crash monitoring, or release observability.
  • Previous experience in crypto, fintech, web3, or another highly regulated or security-sensitive product area.

We strongly encourage candidates of all backgrounds to apply. We believe that our work is stronger with a variety of perspectives, and we’re eager to further diversify our company. If you have a background that you feel would make an impact at Phantom, please consider applying.

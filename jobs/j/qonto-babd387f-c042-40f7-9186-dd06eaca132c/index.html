------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Join us as a Customer Care Onboarding Officer (KYCB) to help Qonto scale its French market operations from Barcelona. You'll handle company account opening and onboarding cases in a fast-growing team, supporting one of Qonto's core banking pillars as the company moves toward full banking license status. ➡️ What you'll do Validate and process KYC cases end-to-end: Handle production work across Full Company Creation and/or Capital Deposit Only flows — reviewing corporate structures, beneficial ownership, and business models to make compliant account-opening decisions — while keeping pace with a 40-45 resolutions/week target.

Support internal and external requests: Respond to requests from other teams and external partners needing help unblocking cases. Spot and escalate patterns: Flag systemic issues when you notice similar problems recurring across multiple tickets, rather than just closing them one by one. Support clients and manage social media claims: Handle cases requiring dedicated attention, including sensitive client relationships and public claims. Contribute to a compliance-first team: Work within KYC/banking regulation frameworks and help build the rigor Qonto needs as it moves toward banking license status.

➡️ What we're looking for You have between 1–2 years in KYC/KYB, customer care, compliance or customer operations. You've reviewed real documentation and know how to justify a decision clearly. You're comfortable reviewing complex legal and company documents, spotting inconsistencies, and making structured decisions under ambiguity — detail and precision are second nature to you. Strong communication in French and English: Fluent written and spoken French is essential; English is Qonto's internal language.

Proactive ownership mindset: You don't wait to be told what's broken. You flag bottlenecks, take initiative on pending cases, and escalate appropriately when something needs to move. Prioritisation under pressure: You can manage a queue of complex cases, balance lead-time targets against quality, and keep track of pending validations without dropping anything. ➡️ What we can offer you Meaningful, complex work from day one: This role isn't high-volume routine processing — every case is different. You'll build real KYC/KYB expertise applied to the french regulatory framework.

Autonomy to improve things: You'll have the chance to propose process improvements, and contribute to a faster, smoother account-opening experience. Cross-functional exposure: You'll work closely with Anti-Financial Crime, AML, Fraud, and frontline operations teams — building a network across Qonto's compliance and risk ecosystem from an early stage. A supportive, high-trust team: Management here favors autonomy over micromanagement, in a team known for strong cohesion. ➡️ Your future manager

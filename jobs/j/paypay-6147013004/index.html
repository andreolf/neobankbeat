Job Description

About the Organization/Team・組織/チームについて Our team builds products that transform banking operations through technology, making them more seamless, efficient, and user-friendly. These include bank account opening, identity verification (eKYC), various operation related procedures, fraud prevention, regulatory compliance, and customer support. ※ After joining the company, you are expected to be seconded to PayPay Bank as a member of the above project. 配属先組織は、口座開設や本人確認、各種手続き、不正対応、法令対応、カスタマーサポートなどの銀行業務を、テクノロジーによってより便利で効率的なものに変えていくプロダクト開発を行っています。

※入社後に上記プロジェクトメンバーとしてPayPay銀行への出向を予定しています。 Background of Recruitment・募集の背景 We're expanding our team to strengthen our ability to work closely with business stakeholders, identify operational challenges, and drive end-to-end product solutions that create real business impact. 業務部門の課題を整理し、プロダクトとして解決まで推進できる体制を強化するため新たなメンバーを募集します。 Job Responsibilities・具体的な業務内容 In this role, you will work closely with business stakeholders to identify operational challenges and respond to regulatory and policy changes. You will define the target state for business processes and products by balancing customer experience, operational efficiency, business value, and regulatory compliance, driving solutions from concept through implementation.

Define What to Solve

  • You will proactively gather insights from business stakeholders and analyze data to identify business challenges. Taking business impact and regulatory requirements into account, you will determine which problems should be solved through products and prioritize them accordingly.

Design Business Processes and Customer Experiences

  • You will design optimal end-to-end experiences for both users and the organization including account opening, identity verification (KYC), customer information updates, inquiries, and risk management.

Drive Delivery from Requirements to Launch

  • You will translate use cases into Product Requirements Documents (PRDs) and define functional requirements, including user interfaces, data models, and business rules. Working closely with designers and engineers, you will drive the entire product development lifecycle—from design and implementation to testing and release.

Measure Impact and Drive Continuous Improvement

  • You will define KPIs such as operational efficiency, error rates, customer inquiries, and costs to measure the impact of product releases. Based on data and feedback from business teams, you will continuously improve both products and business processes.

本ポジションは、業務部門が抱える課題や法令・制度改正への対応事項を整理し、ユーザーの利便性、業務効率、事業効果、法令遵守を総合的に考え、業務プロセスやプロダクトのあるべき姿を定義する役割です。 「何を解決するか」を決める

  • 業務部門へのヒアリングやデータ分析を通じ、課題を整理します。事業効果や法令上の要件を踏まえ、プロダクトで解決すべき課題と優先順位を決定します。

業務プロセスと顧客体験を設計する

  • 口座開設、本人確認、各種変更手続き、問い合わせ対応、リスク対応などについて、ユーザーと銀行双方にとって最適なフローを設計します。

要件定義からリリースまでを推進する

  • ユースケースをPRDに整理し、画面、データ、業務ルールなどの要件を定義します。デザイナーやエンジニアと実現方法を検討し、デザイン、開発、テスト、リリースまでを推進します。

リリース後の効果を検証する

  • 業務工数、不備率、問い合わせ件数、コストなどのKPIを設定し、リリース後の効果を検証します。得られたデータや現場からのフィードバックをもとに、継続的な改善を行います。

Attractiveness of This Position・本ポジションの魅力 Transform the Future of Banking Operations Rather than simply digitizing existing workflows, you'll have the opportunity to rethink how banking operations should work. By redesigning business processes and replacing manual, paper-based operations with seamless digital experiences, you can drive meaningful transformation across the bank. Make a Significant Impact to the Business You'll work across a broad range of banking domains, including account opening, identity verification (KYC), customer procedures, customer support, and the platforms that power banking services. Your work will not only improve the customer experience but also directly contribute to greater operational efficiency, lower costs, and reduced risk.

銀行業務を大きく変えることができる 既存の業務をそのままシステム化するのではなく、業務プロセスの見直し、手作業や紙を前提とした運用をデジタルな体験へ変えていくことができます。 幅広い領域への影響力 口座開設、本人確認、各種手続き、カスタマーサポート、銀行サービスを支える幅広い領域に関わります。ユーザーの利便性向上だけでなく、業務コストやリスクの削減にも直接貢献できます。 Required Experience/Skills・必要な経験／スキル We are looking for candidates with experience in web services, mobile applications, or enterprise systems, along with the following:

  • 6+ years of experience as a Product Manager or Systems Planning Manager.
  • Proven experience analyzing business processes and translating them into clear business and system requirements.
  • Strong technical ability to read and understand API specifications and technical documentation, and translate them into detailed product requirements.
  • Native or Business level Japanese proficiency
  • Willingness to communicate in English (using translation tools or generative AI is perfectly fine)

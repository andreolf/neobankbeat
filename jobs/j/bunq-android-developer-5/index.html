Android Developer

Amsterdam · Istanbul · SofiaEngineeringOn-site

At bunq, we're not just building a banking app; we're reshaping how people around the world experience financial freedom. As our Android Developer, your mission is to ensure our app feels smooth, responsive, and enjoyable every time a user opens it. You'll build new features our users will love, fix the bugs that get in the way, and constantly improve the app’s performance and stability so it never lets them down. Kick off your application by taking our assessment and find out if bunq is your perfect match! 🚀

As our Android Developer at bunq, you will take full ownership of the user experience and technical excellence of our app. You'll: Deliver a stable and highly reliable app by proactively identifying, analyzing, and fixing crashes, bugs, and performance bottlenecks. Own the end-to-end implementation of new features, delivering high-quality updates quickly and efficiently. Drive the architectural vision of our app by writing clean, testable, and maintainable code to simplify future updates and keep our codebase healthy.

You have a deep knowledge of Kotlin and write clean, idiomatic code, leveraging features like coroutines and flows to build scalable and performant applications. You have expert-level knowledge of modern Android architecture patterns (MVVM/MVI) and extensive experience with Jetpack libraries, especially Compose. You are a master of debugging, with a proven ability to use profiling tools to pinpoint and resolve complex issues related to crashes, memory leaks, and performance. You are proactive and have a keen eye for detail, taking full ownership of your work from the first line of code to the final user experience.

You are fluent in English - able to communicate effectively in a global team, ensuring collaboration and clarity across all project stages. All new hires are subject to Pre-employment Screening (PES), which includes checks conducted by our third-party partner, Zinc.

Who we are

Shape a brighter financial future with us. Together with our members, we’re changing the way people think about and interact with personal finance. We’re a next-generation financial services company and national bank using innovative, mobile-first technology to help our millions of members reach their goals. The industry is going through an unprecedented transformation, and we’re at the forefront. We’re proud to come to work every day knowing that what we do has a direct impact on people’s lives, with our core values guiding us every step of the way. Join us to invest in yourself, your career, and the financial world.

The role

SoFi is looking for a strategic, data-driven, and highly effective operator to join our Member Business Unit as Business Operations Director and Chief of Staff to the Member Executive Vice President. The Member team includes a diverse set of businesses and capabilities, including SoFi Plus, our premium membership offering; SoFi Coach, our AI-powered financial planning and insights product; SoFi Pay, our global remittance business; Growth, which supports our marketing enablement platforms; and more.

In this role, you will serve as a trusted partner to Member leadership, leading the business unit’s planning and operating cadence while helping teams translate strategy into measurable results. You will connect priorities across Member and the broader company, provide insights on business performance, drive cross-functional execution, and help ensure the Member team remains on track to achieve, and exceed, its objectives.

What you’ll do

  • Lead operations and operating cadence across Member’s diverse portfolio: Partner with leaders across SoFi Plus, SoFi Coach, SoFi Pay, Growth, and other Member businesses to establish effective meeting rhythms, maintain alignment on priorities, surface dependencies, and drive accountability across the Business Unit.
  • Own quarterly and annual planning: Lead the Member Business Unit’s planning process in partnership with Finance, Product, Engineering, Marketing, Data Science, and business leaders. Set timelines and milestones, clarify requirements and ownership, coordinate inputs, and ensure planning materials are complete, cohesive, and executive-ready.
  • Drive performance management and insights: Partner with Member teams to set meaningful OKRs, monitor progress against goals, and identify opportunities, risks, and performance trends. Build or oversee dashboards and analyses that explains what is happening, why, and what actions should follow.
  • Connect strategy across the organization: Gather cross-functional dependencies, surface tradeoffs, and help align roadmaps and priorities across Member and the broader company. Connect the dots across teams to improve decision-making and execution.
  • Serve as a strategic advisor to Member leadership: Maintain a deep understanding of the Member business, its objectives, and its role in SoFi’s broader strategy. Provide perspective on business health, organizational effectiveness, and opportunities to increase impact; represent the Member Business Unit on behalf of the Business Unit Leader as needed.
  • Strengthen culture and team effectiveness: Partner with the People Business Partner and Member leadership to translate engagement survey feedback into actionable improvements, and drive initiatives that strengthen team health, culture, and performance.
  • Lead executive communications: Develop clear, concise updates for Executive Staff, Member leadership, and other key stakeholders on business performance, strategic priorities, dependencies, risks, and progress against company and Member OKRs.
  • Improve how the business operates: Identify opportunities to simplify processes, strengthen accountability, and improve team effectiveness. Develop recommendations and drive solutions from idea through implementation.
  • Support high-impact strategic initiatives: Lead or contribute to special projects, business reviews, planning sessions, and offsites that advance Member’s objectives and SoFi’s mission.
  • Use AI to amplify team impact: Identify and apply AI tools to accelerate analysis, synthesize information, improve decision-making, automate repeatable processes, and increase the efficiency and effectiveness of Member’s operating rhythms. Evaluate opportunities to adopt AI thoughtfully and responsibly across the business.

What you’ll need

  • 12+ years of experience in business operations, strategy, management consulting, chief of staff, or related roles; MBA preferred
  • Experience in financial services or fintech strongly preferred
  • Demonstrated ability to operate as a trusted advisor to senior leadership (VP+ level)
  • Strong analytical skills (snowflake, spreadsheet modeling, Tableau, etc.) to independently pull and analyze data
  • Experience working with AI tools for analysis and process automation
  • Proven track record of leading cross-functional programs from ambiguity through execution
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to synthesize complex topics into concise executive-ready materials
  • Experience with OKR frameworks, strategic planning processes, and operating cadences at scale
  • Ability to context-switch rapidly and manage competing priorities in a fast-paced environment

What it takes to succeed

  • You’re a full-stack operator: You can move fluidly from shaping strategy and clarifying priorities to building the plan, coordinating stakeholders, and driving execution through completion.
  • You influence without authority: You build trust quickly and align senior leaders and cross-functional partners around shared objectives, even when priorities are competing or the path forward is not fully defined.
  • You’re a data-driven storyteller: You use data to get to the answer, understand the “why” behind performance, and distill complex information into clear recommendations and executive-ready communications.
  • You create structure from ambiguity: You bring clarity to open-ended problems, establish practical processes and operating rhythms, and help teams make progress when inputs, timelines, and ownership are evolving.
  • You have a bias toward action: You balance strategic thinking with pragmatism, identify the highest-impact next steps, and follow through to ensure decisions turn into results.
  • You can juggle multiple priorities and wear many hats: You’re comfortable shifting between planning, analysis, communications, problem-solving, and execution while staying focused on the work that matters most.
  • You’re Member-first: You connect business decisions to the end-to-end member experience and understand how improving outcomes for members drives long-term business performance.

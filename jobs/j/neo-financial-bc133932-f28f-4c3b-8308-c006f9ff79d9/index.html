THE ROLE

Financial software runs underneath the biggest decisions people make. When it's good, it saves real money over years but when it's not, those losses compound just as quietly. Most of it was built to capture value, not create it. Neo is building the alternative. Neo has been Canada's fastest-growing company for three years running. The platform behind that growth is built entirely in-house: 150 services in production, hundreds of pull requests merged every week, tens of millions of transactions processed every month. Engineers own their services from architecture through production. There is no QA team to hand work off to and no DevOps team to manage your deployments.

Neo if looking for a Staff engineer who remains hands-on. You write code, not just docs. You shape architecture and raise the technical ceiling across teams in a program, not only inside one squad. You are a sparring partner for Engineering Managers and Directors on hard technical calls, and you drive multi-team work that would otherwise stall or fragment. Our stack is GraphQL, Mongo, Node.js, TypeScript, React, and React Native on AWS. If you have spent time at a company where good ideas died in committee and you know you could be going faster, this is the environment those engineers tend to thrive in.

WHAT YOU'LL BE DOING

  • Own and ship critical services and shared libraries end to end: design, implementation, production delivery, and long-term health.
  • Set the architectural bar across multiple teams in your program: review designs, catch cross-team dependency risk early, and drive the target state when current systems will not scale.
  • Lead scoping and planning for large, complex projects that span teams; keep product and engineering goals aligned without waiting for perfect requirements.
  • Build and evolve tools, templates, and patterns that many teams use, so the whole program moves faster.
  • Raise and own the technical debt that blocks reliability, security, or scale; get the work onto the roadmap and see it through.
  • Keep production honest: observability, alerting, incident response, and blameless follow-through with actionable fixes.
  • Mentor Senior engineers and coach teams through pairing, design reviews, and deliberate knowledge sharing.
  • Advise Engineering Directors and Managers on tech debt, delivery risk, and when to ship an MVP versus invest in a durable system.
  • Treat AI as a force multiplier for yourself and the teams you support: standards, guardrails, and review expectations, not one-off prompting.

WHO WE ARE LOOKING FOR

  • You have roughly 10+ years building production systems, including serious time as a senior or lead IC, with impact that holds up at scale.
  • You have been the technical owner of systems that mattered: you shaped the architecture, not only implemented against someone else's plan.
  • You still write high-quality code and take pride in being a major contributor and code owner, not only a reviewer of others' work.
  • You think in systems: coupling, contracts, failure modes, cost, and second-order effects across services and teams.
  • You are opinionated about architecture, can defend a position, and change it when the argument is better.
  • You level up the people around you. Your reviews teach. Your mentoring sticks. Seniors and managers seek you out on hard problems.
  • You connect technical work to business outcomes and can sit in prioritization conversations with product and directors without losing the engineering bar.
  • You embrace agentic workflows as how you develop, not as a side experiment. You design and run multi-step agent loops for implementation, testing, and review, and you hold the quality bar when the agents get it wrong.
  • You shape how the program adopts this: skills, agents, playbooks, and guardrails that raise throughput across teams without creating review debt or unsafe shortcuts.
  • Must be a Canadian citizen, Permanent Resident or have a valid work permit.

This is not the right role if you want to leave coding behind, wait for fully scoped tickets, or optimize only for your own squad's backlog.

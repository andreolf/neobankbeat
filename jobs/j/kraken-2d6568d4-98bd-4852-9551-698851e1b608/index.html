SOX Director - ITGC

United States · CanadaFinance & TreasuryRemote only

Apply on Kraken →

BUILDING THE FUTURE OF OPEN FINANCE Payward - the parent company behind Kraken, NinjaTrader, Breakout, xStocks, Payward Services and CF Benchmarks - has spent the last 15 years building one of the most modern and globally accessible financial infrastructure platforms in the industry, built to advance an open, global financial system. THE TEAM Payward - the parent company behind Kraken, NinjaTrader, Breakout, xStocks, Payward Services and CF Benchmarks - has spent the last 15 years building one of the most modern and globally accessible financial infrastructure platforms in the industry, built to advance an open, global financial system.

This role sits within SOX Compliance and is the senior technical authority for IT controls in the SOX program. You will set the strategy for ITGCs, IT application controls, and the controls over private key management and digital asset custody. Internal Audit independently tests the controls you own. You will own the ITGC SOX program, building the control framework, testing approach, and evidence standards from the ground up. This is a hands-on role with real program ownership at a crypto exchange. The technology stack spans blockchain-native infrastructure, digital asset custody systems, and on-chain and off-chain processing, and engineering changes at a pace most companies never see. The systems are complex, the deployments are frequent, and the controls are consequential.

THE OPPORTUNITY

  • Strategy & program ownership
  • Own the enterprise IT SOX strategy and roadmap, covering ITGC, ITAC, and digital asset and key management controls across all in-scope entities and jurisdictions.
  • Define the scoping methodology and risk assessment for IT SOX, including in-scope applications, infrastructure, wallet and custody systems, and third-party and service-organization dependencies.
  • Set control design standards and own the control framework. Design controls that fit modern engineering practices (CI/CD, infrastructure as code, cloud) and don’t slow them down.
  • Set the standard for management testing and evidence. Direct co-sourced and matrixed resources, and review the quality of their work.
  • Use AI-enabled workflows, continuous controls monitoring, and data analytics to move IT SOX from periodic testing toward scalable, automated assurance.

Private key management & digital asset controls

  • Own SOX control design and coverage for the full private key lifecycle: generation, storage, access, signing and approval workflows, rotation, backup and recovery, and retirement.
  • Assess controls over key custody architectures, such as HSMs, MPC, multi-signature, and hot/warm/cold wallet structures, including segregation of duties, quorum and approval controls, and privileged access.
  • Evaluate how key management and custody controls support the existence, completeness, and safeguarding of customer and corporate digital assets and their financial reporting implications.
  • Assess controls over on-chain and off-chain transaction processing, reconciliation of wallets to ledgers, and reliance on blockchain data and third-party custodians or service organizations.
  • Be the SOX authority on emerging digital asset risks and decide how the control framework should respond as products, chains, and custody models evolve.

Deficiency evaluation & remediation

  • Lead evaluation of control deficiencies, including root cause analysis and severity and pervasiveness assessments, and recommend classification (control deficiency, significant deficiency, or material weakness).
  • Own remediation strategy for open ITGC and key management findings. Drive executive-level accountability with Engineering, Security, and Infrastructure leaders.
  • Escalate gaps and delays directly to senior leadership. Report status to the Audit Committee as required.
  • Coordinate with Internal Audit so remediated controls are ready for independent validation.

Executive & external stakeholder engagement

  • Be the senior SOX voice to the CFO, CISO, CTO, Controller, and the Audit Committee on IT control posture, risks, and remediation.
  • Lead the external auditor relationship on IT matters: audit strategy, reliance and scoping decisions, walkthroughs, and resolution of disagreements.
  • Serve as a trusted advisor to engineering and security leaders, translating audit and control requirements into practical engineering decisions.
  • Partner with Internal Audit, ERM, the business process SOX lead, and co-sourced providers to align coverage across the full assurance program.
  • Mentor SOX and risk team members and raise the technical bar across the function.

WHAT YOU BRING

  • 10+ years in SOX compliance, IT audit, internal audit, or external audit, with deep ITGC experience and a record of owning programs at enterprise scale.
  • Direct, hands-on experience with crypto and private key management controls, including designing, assessing, or auditing controls over key custody, wallet infrastructure, signing, and digital asset safeguarding. This is a must-have.
  • Experience in a crypto exchange, custodian, fintech, or payments company with complex, rapidly evolving infrastructure.
  • CISA and CPA certifications required. Candidates with one who are actively pursuing the other will be considered.
  • Expert knowledge of ITGC frameworks, SOX 404, COSO, COBIT, and PCAOB standards, with the judgment to make and defend scoping, reliance, and deficiency-classification decisions.
  • Strong technical fluency in how systems, databases, cloud infrastructure, and deployment pipelines work.
  • Proven ability to connect an ITGC or key management failure to its impact on business process controls and the financial statements.
  • Experience working with external auditors (Big 4 preferred) and presenting to Audit Committees and senior executives.
  • Experience across multi-entity structures or multiple jurisdictions.
  • Exceptional communicator who earns credibility with both engineers and finance executives.

NICE TO HAVES

  • Experience building or scaling an IT SOX program in a growth-stage or first-year SOX company.
  • Familiarity with CI/CD, GitLab or similar version control, and cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP).
  • Familiarity with SOC 1/SOC 2 reliance for custodians and service providers, and with proof-of-reserves or similar attestations.
  • Familiarity with audit and GRC platforms such as AuditBoard or Workiva.
  • Familiarity with AI-assisted compliance tools and willingness to adopt new technologies.

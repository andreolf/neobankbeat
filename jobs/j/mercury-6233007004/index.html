Senior Recruiter - GTM

San Francisco, CA, New York, NY, Portland, OR, or Remote within Canada or United StatesSales & PartnershipsRemote onlyposted 2d ago

Our Sales, Customer Success, Support, and Partnerships teams are often the first people a founder or business owner talks to at Mercury, and the people who help them as their company grows. Who we hire onto those teams shapes how customers experience Mercury. We're looking for a Senior Recruiter to help build and scale those teams. You'll own full-cycle searches across our go-to-market organization, from high-volume roles like Sales Development Representatives to specialized roles in Customer Success and Partnerships. You'll partner closely with GTM leaders and hiring managers, run searches end to end, and help improve how our recruiting team operates as we grow.

This role is a good fit for someone who is organized, proactive, and comfortable owning their work. You should enjoy learning a new business quickly, work well with autonomy, and care about giving every candidate a thoughtful experience.

What you'll do

  • Own full-cycle recruiting for Sales, Customer Success, Support, Partnerships, and Business Development, from sourcing to close
  • Run kickoffs and calibration sessions with hiring managers to define what a strong candidate looks like, and adjust the search as you learn
  • Identify and engage strong candidates through sourcing and inbound channels, using funnel data to decide where to spend your time
  • Communicate clearly and consistently with stakeholders on pipeline health, progress, blockers, and recruiting timeline
  • Represent Mercury to candidates, sharing our mission, culture, and what it's like to join a growing GTM organization
  • Contribute to recruiting processes, tools, and documentation across the team
  • Own offer strategy and closing for your searches

What you'll need

  • 4+ years of full-cycle recruiting experience, ideally including GTM roles such as sales, customer success, or account management, in-house or at an agency
  • Strong organization and attention to detail, with experience managing several searches at once, including high-volume and complex or high-priority roles
  • Strong sourcing skills and ability to write compelling outreach
  • Ability to work with recruiting data, such as pass-through rates and time to hire, to plan searches and explain trade-offs
  • Experience influencing hiring managers and senior leaders with data and clear communication
  • A track record of owning searches end to end with minimal oversight
  • Comfort operating in ambiguity and improving processes when they aren't working

Nice to have

  • Experience recruiting at a fintech, SaaS, or high-growth startup
  • Familiarity with Greenhouse, Gem, or similar recruiting tools

Sr. Director of Product, ExtraCash

United StatesOtherRemote only

Apply on Dave →

DAVE VS. GOLIATH. WE’RE DAVE. Dave is a financial app on a mission to build products that level the financial playing field. It is redefining the financial landscape by leveraging technology to create an affordable, transparent, and user-centric access to liquidity for millions of Americans. As a leading innovator in the U.S. financial services sector, Dave’s digital financial platform offers products designed to meet the credit needs of those underserved by traditional financial institutions. Dave’s offerings include its flagship ExtraCash product, providing members up to $500 within minutes. The company is on track to launch several new product offerings in 2026, including a Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) option.

Dave is focused on serving Americans who are financially vulnerable or living paycheck to paycheck. Dave is leading the charge in creating a new era of credit products that prioritizes speed, affordability, and accessibility, making it the go-to financial partner for those who need it most. We’re hiring a Sr. Director of Product to serve as the end-to-end product and business executive for Dave’s ExtraCash product line, a $600M+ business. Reporting directly to the VP of Product, you will have product ownership and P&L accountability and lead the overall vision, product roadmap, unit economics, and operational execution for our flagship business.

THE OPPORTUNITY This is an executive product role that will serve as the primary business and product leader for ExtraCash, driving sustainable revenue growth, optimizing credit performance, and deepening member engagement. You will set multi-year strategic direction while managing feature delivery and business performance, and directly lead a high-performing team of product leaders and squads. Partnering with cross-functional teams across Engineering, Data, Risk, Credit, Marketing, Finance, Legal, and external partners, you will exercise business-owner level authority to maximize long-term value for both Dave and our members.

WHAT YOU’LL BUILD AND OWN

  • Scale ExtraCash by conceiving, designing, and delivering product features that are innovative and differentiated
  • Own financial performance for the ExtraCash business, balancing top line revenue, member growth, and credit risk.
  • Direct multi-disciplinary product squads and cross-functional teams from discovery through launch and long-term scaling, maintaining complete accountability for business outcomes.
  • Define and steer core business metrics—including gross/net ARPU, ExtraCash Actives, conversion, credit losses, and lifetime value—regularly reporting performance and strategy to senior leadership.
  • Provide cross-functional leadership across Engineering, Credit, Risk, Marketing, Finance, Legal, and sponsor banks to align operational strategy with company OKRs.
  • Lead, mentor, and grow a team of high-perfoming Product Managers and Group PMs, establishing clear operating standards, context, and executive oversight.
  • Identify and validate new growth levers, credit capabilities, and strategic product expansions to reinforce ExtraCash as the market-leading liquidity product.

THE IMPACT You will directly drive the scale and strategic trajectory of ExtraCash - success is expanding profitability while growing the base of users for whom we provide clear and transparent short term credit solutions to.

WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR

  • Proven track record as a P&L or business owner with total accountability for driving revenue, unit economics, and product strategy at 9-figure revenue scale.
  • 10+ years of product management and business leadership experience in consumer fintech or financial services, including 4+ years leading other PMs or multi-disciplinary product organizations.
  • Deep financial acumen and business judgment, with experience connecting user experience, credit risk, loss performance, and net economics.
  • Strong cross-functional leadership skills and experience working with technical, operational, risk, financial, and external partners.
  • Clear executive communication skills, including the ability to simplify complex decisions and influence senior stakeholders.
  • Practical experience applying AI to product work, analysis, or team workflows in ways that improve decision-making and effectiveness.
  • Technical curiosity and fluency; a bachelor’s degree in a technical field is a plus.

WHAT TO EXPECT This role has significant autonomy but also significant accountability to stakeholders. Priorities can be fluid and evolve but you’ll have meaningful influence over how the strategy develops. Decisions are grounded in data, but judgment matters. You’ll be expected to create clarity from ambiguity and bring the right partners together to move the business forward. WHY JOIN

  • Meaningful scale: Run a $600M+ business that serves millions of members.
  • Real ownership: Own the full stack - product strategy through execution all the way to business outcomes.
  • Strong partnership: Work closely with leaders across Product, Engineering, Design, Data, Risk, Marketing, and Finance.
  • A growing team: Develop product talent today with the opportunity to expand the organization as the business grows.

Ready to build for the underdog? Reports to: VP, Product Management Don’t let imposter syndrome get in the way of an incredible opportunity. We’re looking for people who can help us achieve our mission and vision, not just check off the boxes. If you’re excited about this role, we encourage you to apply. You may just be the right candidate for this or other roles.

Why you’ll love working here

At Dave, our people are just as important as our product. Our culture reflects the values that guide who we are, how we work, and what we aspire to be. Daves are member-centric, helpful, transparent, persistent, and better together. We strive to create an environment where all Daves feel valued, heard, and empowered to do their best work. As a virtual-first company, team members can live and work anywhere in the United States, except Hawaii.

A few of our benefits & perks

💚 Opportunity to tackle tough challenges, learn and grow from fellow top talent, and help millions of people reach their personal financial goals 💻 Flexible hours and virtual-first work culture with a home office stipend 🏥 Premium Medical, Dental, and Vision Insurance plans 👶 Generous paid parental and caregiver leave 💰 401(k) savings plan with matching contributions 📈 Financial advisor and financial wellness support 🏖️ Flexible PTO and generous company holidays, including Juneteenth and Winter Break

🎉 All-company in-person events once or twice a year and virtual events throughout to connect with your team members and leadership team Dave Operating LLC is proud to be an Equal Employment Opportunity employer and is dedicated to cultivating a diverse and inclusive workplace. We will consider for employment all qualified applicants and do not discriminate on any basis protected by federal, state, or local law, including the City of Los Angeles’ Fair Chance Initiative for Hiring Ordinance relating to an applicant's criminal history.

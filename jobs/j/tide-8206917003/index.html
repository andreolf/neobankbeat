ABOUT

THE ROLE

We are looking for a highly analytical, commercially minded FP A Manager to own key parts of our planning, forecasting and performance reporting cycle. You will be a trusted finance partner to senior leaders, turning numbers into clear insights that shape decisions. This is a high-impact role: you will make sure our investments, including people, line up with our strategic and financial goals. You’ll need strong financial modelling skills, a rigorous approach to process and automation, and the confidence to challenge and influence stakeholders across the business. You thrive on ambiguity and take full ownership of a process or workflow from start to finish, building the structure and policies yourself without waiting for direction.

The successful candidate in this role will be focused on the following:

  • Own key parts of the annual budget, quarterly reforecast and long-range planning cycles, from setting assumptions to final sign-off.
  • Lead month-end performance reporting, including detailed variance analysis against budget and previous forecasts, with a clear explanation of what drove each variance and the outlook for the rest of the year.
  • Build, maintain and improve driver-based financial models in our planning system (Aleph) that link operational drivers to P L, EBITDA and cash outcomes. Act as a subject matter expert for the system.
  • Run scenario and sensitivity analysis and provide financial modelling and decision support on major initiatives, including organisational design, location strategy and large-scale investments, testing the impact of different growth, cost and investment options.
  • Act as a Finance Business Partner to budget holders. Analyse spend and assumptions, support investment cases, and help leaders make informed trade-offs.
  • Own the forecasting of people costs, including headcount, compensation, bonus and equity. Work with the People and Total Rewards teams so these costs are fully and accurately reflected in the plan.
  • Maintain financial control over headcount and hiring approvals so that open roles stay in line with approved budgets.
  • Flag risks and opportunities to the forecast early, using real-time operational data from source systems such as Workday and Greenhouse.
  • Prepare insight-led reporting and investor/board materials for senior leadership, leading with conclusions supported by data. Build and automate the reports and dashboards behind them to cut manual effort and speed up decision-making.
  • Track and analyse key business and cost-efficiency metrics, translating operational data into a financial story.
  • Design and launch new FP A processes and policies independently, from scoping requirements through to adoption.
  • Drive continuous improvement in FP A processes, data quality and tooling, including integrations between Aleph and our core finance and operational systems.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

  • 7+ years of progressive experience in FP A, corporate finance or a similar role, ideally in a fast-growth tech or scale-up business. Experience with headcount or people-cost planning is a plus.
  • Strong track record of owning core FP A processes from start to finish, including budgeting, re-forecasting, long-range planning and month-end reporting.
  • Proven ability to build and maintain driver-based financial models, including people-cost and compensation drivers, that connect operating activity to financial outcomes.
  • Demonstrated end-to-end ownership of an ambiguous process. This means taking a loosely defined problem, scoping the requirements yourself, designing the process, getting stakeholders to adopt it, and keeping it running over time with minimal direction.
  • Excellent business partnering skills with budget holders and senior leaders, with the confidence to challenge assumptions and influence decisions.
  • Advanced Excel/Google Sheets skills and hands-on experience with dedicated FP A planning tools (e.g. Aleph, Anaplan, Adaptive, Pigment).
  • Strong data skills, including hands-on SQL for pulling and analysing complex datasets.
  • A systems mindset, with experience managing data flows between the planning tool and source systems such as the ERP/GL, HRIS (e.g. Workday) or ATS (e.g. Greenhouse).
  • Exceptional ability to turn complex financial analysis into clear, actionable insight for both finance and non-finance audiences, including the Executive Team and the Board.

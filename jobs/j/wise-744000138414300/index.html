Payroll Specialist

São Paulo, BRFinance & TreasuryHybridposted 27d ago

Payroll Specialist - AMER SP FTC 12 Months We’re currently hiring for a 12 months fixed term Payroll Specialist to join our global Payroll Team based in São Paulo/SP! We’re looking for a payroll superstar, with a keen eye for accuracy, attention to detail and a desire to share your AMER payroll expertise and knowledge with our Global Payroll Team. Reporting to the Payroll Lead, you’ll have above-average understanding of payroll across the AMER region with focus on LATAM, its overall function, and how our team provides excellent service to others. You’ll be responsible for the accurate, effective, and timely processing of your payroll for each pay period.

Your mission This exciting role will join a team responsible for the overall global payroll process, including compliance, internal audit, and reconciliation across multiple countries for Wise. You’ll complete a variety of daily, weekly, monthly, annual, and ad-hoc tasks to ensure your payrolls are processed in accordance ahead of payroll deadlines. This role will include supporting the payroll application, Cloudpay, of which we facilitate our payrolls. In processing and supporting the payroll, you will strive for accuracy, reduce errors, and payroll more efficiently. You will develop robust & detailed processes to ensure the execution of payroll in an effective, efficient, and timely manner. Cross-training will be key!

This amazing role will also participate by providing a variety of deliverables, including global projects, pre-post payroll journals, internal audits, and more. Also, you will provide input regarding team goals, KPIs, efficiencies, process improvements, and future optimization of our payroll team - all while providing outstanding customer service. Always. Wise has already pioneered new ways for people to transfer money across borders and currencies. Our customers can also manage their hard-earned money with the world’s first platform to offer true multi-currency banking. We’re 8000+ Wisers strong, with over 90 nationalities working in 25+ locations around the globe! We’re looking for a Payroll Specialist who lives our values, will help grow our culture, and drive our mission forward! 🚀

The specifics

  • You’ll be working as a part of a wider Global Payroll Team, servicing 25+ countries using Workday (HRIS), while focusing specifically on our AMER & LATAM based Wisers
  • You’ll prepare, double-check, cross-check, review and analyze payroll inputs according to the established deadlines
  • You’ll be completely responsible to ensure the timely submission of your payroll inputs to our third party payroll provider
  • You’ll partner with your Lead to recommend new approaches, policy and procedure changes towards continuous improvement within the team
  • You’ll ensure your payrolls balance to your inputs - always
  • You’ll be responsible for all documentation within your historical payroll files
  • Continue to increase subject matter expertise on your allocated locations, on all payroll and legislative matters, providing support, as needed, to others
  • Work with the Payroll team and the Payroll Lead to provide additional processing/transaction support, as needed

Compliance

  • You’ll ensure compliance across local payroll tax laws, while meeting applicable timelines effectively and efficiently
  • Stay current with regulation changes affecting your payrolls to ensure accuracy and service to our customers
  • Partner to complete and respond to regulatory agency inquiries

Financials, Audits, and Reporting:

  • Responsible for outputs of your payrolls, such as payroll journals, GL files, and ad-hoc reporting
  • You’ll ensure all reporting and data is completed timely

Your challenges

  • We’re evolving our payroll model - you’ll have the chance to bring your brainpower and prior experience to help us build an outstanding payroll team as we continue to grow and scale
  • You’ll assist to create efficiencies in our current ways of working and help us develop new ways of payroll processing

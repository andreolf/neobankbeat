People Operations Specialist – 9 month FTC

LondonOperationsHybrid

Apply on OakNorth →

About us At OakNorth, we’re on a mission to empower ambitious businesses and the communities they serve. Since 2015, we’ve lent over $21 billion across the UK and US, helped create more than 58,000 new homes and 36,000 new jobs, and supported hundreds of thousands of personal savers — all while driving economic growth in the markets we serve.

About the team

Our People team supports employees across OakNorth, working with leaders and colleagues across the business to deliver a smooth employee experience. We’re evolving our systems and exploring how automation and AI can help us work more effectively.

About the role

We’re looking for a proactive, detail-oriented People Operations Specialist to join us on a nine-month fixed-term contract. Reporting to the People Operations Manager, you’ll coordinate day-to-day employee lifecycle processes for our UK and US teams, helping ensure accurate records, timely delivery and a positive experience for employees. This is a hands-on role with ownership from day one. You’ll manage your own priorities, follow through on actions and flag risks early, with direction and support from your manager and team. You’ll also have the opportunity to suggest improvements and help shape how we work.

What you’ll do

Employee lifecycle

  • Coordinate onboarding across the UK & US, from offer acceptance through to day one and beyond.
  • Manage employee changes and lifecycle processes, including promotions, transfers, salary changes, probation confirmations and offboarding.
  • Track probation reviews, ensuring they’re completed on time, outcomes are accurately recorded and documentation is maintained.
  • Manage background screening through Zinc, ensuring checks are completed and appropriately documented.
  • Partner with IT, leaders, People Partners and other stakeholders to coordinate employee changes and keep outstanding actions moving.

People operations and employee support

  • Maintain accurate, complete and up-to-date employee information in our People systems, and monitor key processes and workflows.
  • Support compliance and audit requirements by keeping records and documentation accurate and accessible.
  • Act as a first point of contact for UK People Operations queries, responding to day-to-day requests promptly and professionally.
  • Help employees with benefits enrolment and benefits-related queries.
  • Identify and escalate unfamiliar, sensitive or higher-risk issues to the appropriate person.

Process improvement

  • Look for ways to improve operational efficiency, employee experience and data accuracy.
  • Explore AI and automation opportunities to streamline administration and improve service delivery.

You’ll be a great fit if you have…

  • 1–2 years’ relevant experience in UK HR operations, with strong knowledge of contracts, onboarding, offboarding, background screening and employee lifecycle processes.
  • Experience working with modern HR systems and maintaining accurate employee information.
  • Exceptional attention to detail and a commitment to checking your own work.
  • Strong organisational skills, with the ability to manage competing priorities and follow through without reminders.
  • Clear communication skills and a customer-focused approach to supporting employees.
  • Sound judgement and discretion when handling confidential information, including knowing when to ask for guidance or escalate an issue.
  • A proactive, collaborative approach: you use the information available to solve problems, build relationships and share ideas for better ways of working.
  • An interest in using AI and technology to improve how work gets done.

Nice to have

Experience supporting People Operations across both the UK and US. If you have solid experience in one geography and are keen to learn the other, we’d still love to hear from you. What we offer

  • Ownership from day one — the trust to manage your work and contribute ideas.
  • Support from your team — context, direction and a collaborative People team to help you succeed.
  • The opportunity to improve how we work — help shape evolving systems and explore AI and automation.

If you enjoy getting the details right, supporting people and finding better ways to work, we’d love to hear from you.

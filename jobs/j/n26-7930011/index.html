Growth Program Manager

BerlinProductOn-sitevisa sponsorshipposted 5d ago

About the opportunity We are looking for a Growth Program Manager to join the Growth Operations team and drive the execution of N26’s most critical strategic initiatives. This role focuses on high-impact, cross-departmental projects – such as refining our value proposition or optimizing global consent frameworks – that fundamentally shape our business. You will be the "bridge" between strategy and reality, ensuring that complex projects with dozens of moving parts stay on track. If you are a high-energy operator who excels at navigating ambiguity, chasing stakeholders, and maintaining a bird’s-eye view of multiple workstreams, this is the role for you.

In this role, you will:

  • Orchestrate the Growth Roadmap: Own the overarching Growth roadmap, maintaining a clear overview of all active initiatives and ensuring the organization stays on track to deliver against its strategic priorities.
  • Drive Strategic Execution: Own the end-to-end delivery of high-priority, cross-departmental projects across the growth department (e.g., Value Proposition shifts, Consent Optimization) from initial kickoff to final implementation.
  • Orchestrate Multi-Project Timelines: Maintain a rigorous overview of deadlines, KPIs, and deliverables across several complex workstreams simultaneously, ensuring no detail is overlooked.
  • Proactively Manage Risk: Identify potential bottlenecks and project risks early on, taking the initiative to escalate issues and propose solutions to prevent delays in our strategic roadmap.
  • Influence Cross-Functional Stakeholders: Act as the central point of contact for internal teams (Product, Legal, Marketing, Markets, Finance), relentlessly chasing deliverables and ensuring every stakeholder meets their commitments on time.
  • Enable Executive Decision-Making: Keep the Senior Leadership team informed through clear, concise status reporting, highlighting progress against milestones and resource requirements.

What you need to be successful

  • Proven Track Record in Program Management: You have 5+ years of experience managing large-scale, strategic projects in a fast-paced environment (fintech or high-growth tech experience is a plus).
  • High Emotional Intelligence Stakeholder Management: You are a master at "chasing" without causing friction, possessing the emotional intelligence to navigate complex organizational dynamics and the interpersonal skills to hold senior stakeholders accountable while keeping teams motivated.
  • Operational Rigor: You have a systematic approach to project management, utilizing frameworks and tools to track complex dependencies and ensure 100% reliability in delivery.
  • Strategic Financial Acumen: You are comfortable forecasting resource needs and managing project-specific budgets to ensure strategic goals are met efficiently.
  • Clear Concise Communication: You have the ability to synthesize complex project updates into actionable insights for executive-level audiences, focusing on what matters most for the business.

What’s in it for you

  • Accelerate your career growth by joining one of Europe’s most talked about disruptors 🚀.
  • Impact at scale: You drive the most important Marketing Growth initiatives at N26.
  • Global footprint: You are responsible for international campaigns across all EU markets.
  • Dynamic environment: You thrive in a fast-paced culture.
  • Employee benefits that range from a competitive personal development budget, work from home budget, discounts to fitness wellness memberships, language apps and public transportation.
  • As an N26 employee you will have access to a Premium subscription on your personal N26 bank account. As well as subscriptions for friends and family members.
  • Additional day of annual leave for each year of service.
  • A high degree of autonomy and access to cutting edge technologies - all while working with a friendly team of peers of diverse nationalities, life experiences and family statuses.
  • A relocation package with visa support for those who need it.

Who we are N26 has reimagined banking for today’s digital world. Technology and design empower everything we do and it’s how we are building the global banking platform the world loves to use. We've eliminated physical branches, paperwork, and hidden fees for an elegant digital experience and supreme savings. Giving people the power to live and bank their way is what gets us out of bed in the morning and inspires the work that we do. We are headquartered in Berlin with offices in multiple cities across Europe, including Vienna and Barcelona, and a 1,500-strong team of more than 80 nationalities.

Sounds good? Apply now for this position.

Financial Assistance Head of Compliance & Insights Location: Cardiff & Manchester Reporting to: Director of Financial Assistance Closing date: Thursday, 26th November at 5pm About Starling At Starling, we are on a mission to provide people with a fairer, smarter, and more human alternative to traditional banks. We are building a leading digital bank, driven by fast technology, fair service, and honest values. Our vision is to create a banking industry that serves everyone, which we accomplish by developing best-in-class proprietary technology, offering an exceptional customer experience, and establishing a strong, sustainable business.

Our five core values – Listen, Keep It Simple, Do The Right Thing, Own It, and Aim for Greatness – are at the heart of everything we do, guiding our strategy and shaping our culture. We're a branchless, largely paperless bank, committed to responsible banking, sustainability, and fostering an inclusive environment where everyone can thrive. Hybrid working: All Starling colleagues spend a minimum of 10 days per month in the office with flexibility to work the rest from home. The Opportunity

As a hands-on technical 1st Line of Defence (1LoD) practitioner, this role oversees regulatory compliance assurance, local risk and control management, Standards authoring, and insight-led continuous improvement across all retail and business banking Financial Assistance operations. Operating directly in the technical detail, the role translates complex regulatory obligations (including FCA, PRA, Consumer Duty, and CONC) into clear local Standards and robust 1LoD controls aligned to Bank risk appetite. By combining frontline compliance monitoring with deep-dive root cause analysis, the role converts operational, quality, and customer findings into actionable intelligence that drives continuous improvement and informs strategic direction.

What You'll Do

  • Compliance Monitoring and Oversight: Directly perform and lead continuous 1LoD compliance monitoring, thematic reviews, and controls testing across all retail and business banking collections and recoveries operations to guarantee adherence to UK legal frameworks, regulatory guidelines, and internal governance standards. Detect compliance deficiencies or regulatory breaches and oversee effective remediation.
  • 1LoD Risk & Control Management: Own and maintain the Financial Assistance local Risk and Control framework and risk register. Direct the identification, design, testing, exception and control reporting, and remediation validation of local risks and controls, and coordinate cross-functional responses to regulatory shifts and emerging risks.
  • Policies and Standards: Author and maintain local Financial Assistance Standards, ensuring they align directly with the Bank's Customer in Financial Difficulties (CiFD) policy and legal and regulatory obligations. Manage cross-functional document consultation (across Legal, Compliance, Credit Risk, and Operations), secure formal policy approval, and provide ongoing first-line compliance assurance against Bank-wide policies and Standards.
  • Insights & Continuous Improvement Enablement: Synthesise qualitative and quantitative intelligence from compliance testing, quality outputs, complaints, and operational data to uncover systemic friction, vulnerable customer pain points, and emerging conduct trends. Direct the execution of structured deep-dives and thematic reviews, translating insights into measurable operational and journey improvements.
  • Conduct, Customer Outcomes & 2LoD Interface: Act as the primary 1LoD technical SME for regulatory matters across retail and SME arrears, translating obligations into actionable controls and strategies. Collaborate with second-line Compliance, Credit Risk, Legal, and Internal Audit to drive a unified compliance posture, managing assurance reviews and issue validation with authoritative detail.
  • Reporting & Specialist Coaching: Prepare and oversee authoritative compliance, risk, and insight reports for senior management, governance forums, and regulatory bodies. Provide hands-on technical direction, coaching, and quality assurance for a lean specialist team, balancing light-touch people management with personal technical delivery.

What You'll Bring

  • End-to-End CiFD & Portfolio Expertise: Deep subject matter expertise across the complete Customer in Financial Difficulties (CiFD) lifecycle, spanning pre-arrears, collections, forbearance, and recoveries, across both Retail Banking and SME/Business Banking portfolios.
  • UK Regulatory Mastery: Comprehensive operational command of the UK regulatory landscape governing retail and commercial debt (FCA CONC, MCOB, Consumer Duty, PRA expectations, Insolvency/Breathing Space legislation, and LSB Standards of Lending Practice).
  • 1LoD RCSA & Standards Authoring: Expert practitioner knowledge of 1LoD risk and control frameworks (RCSA), control testing methodologies, and drafting audit-ready operational Standards and policy dispensations.
  • Root-Cause Insights & Continuous Improvement: Strong analytical grasp of operational MI, quality and outcome testing, and structured continuous improvement methodologies to convert assurance findings into systemic operational fixes.

Behaviours & Competencies

  • Hands-On Technical Rigour & Precision: Thrives in the technical detail—personally authoring Standards, interrogating raw data, and testing controls with meticulous attention to quality rather than relying purely on delegation.
  • Analytical & Root-Cause Thinking: Connects dots across disparate compliance, quality, outcome testing, and customer data points to separate isolated human errors from systemic control or journey weaknesses.
  • Constructive Challenge & Integrity: Demonstrates professional courage and objectivity when surfacing compliance gaps or challenging operational and strategic proposals that risk poor customer outcomes.
  • Pragmatic Stakeholder Engagement & Collaboration: Translates dense regulatory rules into workable 1LoD controls, partnering seamlessly across Strategy, Operations, Legal, and 2LoD Risk/Compliance.
  • Thriving Under Pressure & Cultural Alignment: Balances competing regulatory and operational priorities in a fast-paced environment while actively embodying and promoting the Bank's core values.

Why Join Starling?

  • Make an Impact: Work on projects that directly shape the future of banking and improve the financial lives of our customers. Starling is technology-led, and you'll have the freedom to accomplish your goals in innovative ways.
  • Culture of Excellence: Be part of a collaborative, empowered, and forward-thinking team. We encourage experimentation, ownership, and a relentless focus on "doing the right thing.”
  • Growth and Development: We are committed to your professional growth, offering opportunities to learn new technologies, take on new challenges, and own interesting things from day one.
  • A Bank That Cares: We're a Living Wage employer, committed to flexible working, and dedicated to creating a fair, open, and safe working environment with compassion and inclusion at its core.

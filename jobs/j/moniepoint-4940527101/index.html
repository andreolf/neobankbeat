Regional Security Lead (North West)

Kano, NigeriaEngineeringOn-siteposted Aug 1

Who We Are Moniepoint Inc. is Africa’s all-in-one financial platform, helping 20 million businesses and individuals access seamless payments, banking, credit, cross-border, and business management tools each month. As Nigeria’s largest merchant acquirer, we power most of the country’s point-of-sale (POS) transactions. Through our subsidiaries, Moniepoint Inc. processes over $250 billion in digital payment transaction value annually. What We Do At Moniepoint, we are a customer-focused community dedicated to crafting solutions that redefine our industry. We leverage artificial intelligence and data-driven best practices to support our businesses, from providing credit and overdrafts to ensuring every transaction is secure.

Curious about what makes Moniepoint an incredible place to work? Check out our stories on how we cultivate a culture of innovation, teamwork, and growth. Job Purpose The Regional Security Lead ensures that our mission never stops. You are the guardian of our physical spaces, ensuring that every Moniepoint office is a sanctuary where our team can build and create without worry. By leading regional security operations, managing on-ground teams, and proactively identifying risks, you provide the stability and safety that allows financial happiness to thrive. You don't just react to incidents; you build the systems that prevent them.

Key Responsibilities

  • Safeguard our People and Assets: Lead the implementation of security policies that protect our team, offices, and equipment across your region.
  • Master the Environment: Conduct regular security assessments to spot vulnerabilities and design innovative strategies to reduce risk.
  • Lead the Front Line: Supervise and mentor private security guards and armed police personnel, ensuring they perform their duties with mastery and respect.
  • Build Strong Alliances: Act as our primary liaison with law enforcement and emergency services, ensuring we always have a seat at the table during emergencies.
  • Investigate and Solve: Lead the response to security breaches and accidents, providing clear, factual reports that help us learn and improve.
  • Empower the Team: Design and deliver training programs that equip our security personnel and employees with life-saving protocols.
  • Innovate for Safety: Stay ahead of security trends and technology (like CCTV and access control) to keep our regional operations modern and resilient.
  • Support Business Resilience: Provide expert guidance on asset recovery and fraud investigation when the business needs your mastery.

Qualifications

  • Professional Background: Should have at least 5 years of experience successfully leading a security team in a guard services/physical security company or have 5 years of experience as an ex-commissioned service personnel supervisor—either in the military, police, or paramilitary.
  • Education: A Bachelor’s degree in Criminology, Psychology, Criminal Justice, or a related field.
  • Certification: You hold a recognized security certification such as CPP, PSP, or CPO.
  • Integrity: A clean criminal record and the ability to pass a comprehensive background check are essential.
  • Language Culture: You speak the local dialects of the major tribes in your assigned region, allowing you to connect with the community.
  • Good experience working with automated security systems like CCTV cameras, burglary alarm systems, etc.

About You

  • You are a Hero: You are driven by mastery and the desire to protect others. People count on you to come through when things get tough.
  • You are Relatable: You don’t hide behind technical jargon. You communicate clearly and build trust with everyone, from the security guards to the executive team.
  • You are Proactive: You don’t wait for things to go wrong. You have the "Creator" spirit, always looking for better ways to secure our future.
  • You are composed: In high-pressure situations, you are the calmest person in the room. You lead with facts, not emotions.

What Success Looks Like

  • Zero Preventable Incidents: Our regional offices remain safe, secure, and fully operational.
  • High Readiness: Security personnel are well-trained and respond to drills or real-world events with 100% precision.
  • Strong Community Ties: You have built a network of local stakeholders that helps Moniepoint navigate regional risks effectively.
  • Operational Excellence: Security technology is 100% functional, and documentation is factual, honest, and actionable.

What we can offer you

  • Culture: We put our people first and prioritize the well-being of every team member. We’ve built a company where all voices are heard, and we look out for one another. Above all, we are human.
  • Learning: We have a learning- and development-focused environment with an emphasis on knowledge sharing and training.
  • Compensation: You’ll receive an attractive salary, pension, health insurance, and an annual bonus, plus other benefits.

What to expect in the hiring process

  • A preliminary phone call with the recruiter.
  • An interview with the hiring team.
  • An interview with a member of our executive team.

Moniepoint Inc. is an equal-opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees and candidates.

We’re not about selling products - we want to solve problems and change lives through Monzo ❤️ 📍London | 💰£98,600 - £108,000 ➕ Incentive Awards tied to your performance ➕ Benefits | Hear from the team ✨ ⭐ Our Communications team We're looking for an experienced communications professional for a Senior Consumer PR Manager (12 month fixed term contract) role to lead Monzo's earned consumer communications programme in the UK.

You'll identify the opportunities that matter most and develop earned communications that help more people discover, understand and trust Monzo. Working across Product, Marketing and Business teams, you'll bring products, customer insights and business priorities to life in ways that deliver real business impact. You'll build long-term communications plans while staying agile enough to respond to changing priorities, emerging opportunities and shifting timelines. Strong planning, judgement, adaptability and stakeholder management skills are essential to success in this role.

While this role is focused on the UK, you'll also act as a trusted advisor to our European Communications team, sharing expertise and helping shape consumer communications as we grow internationally. We're excited by how communications is evolving. Whether it's AI, changing search behaviour, creators or emerging platforms, we're looking for someone who's curious about what's next and excited to experiment with new ways of reaching audiences. 🔑 You’ll play a key role by…

  • Developing and delivering Monzo's UK earned consumer communications strategy and roadmap, aligned to business priorities.
  • Partnering across Product, Marketing, Business and other cross-functional teams to shape plans early, identify opportunities, understand the wider stakeholder landscape and manage reputational considerations from the outset.
  • Leading communications around our products, data, customer insights and business priorities—creating earned stories through product launches, original insight, reactive opportunities and integrated campaigns.
  • Developing creative communications ideas internally and with agency partners, recognising that the best ideas don't always need a major campaign, a large budget, an agency brief or a business-driven moment.
  • Exercising strong editorial judgement and prioritising the work that will have the greatest impact.
  • Building relationships with journalists, creators and emerging platforms, continually evolving how we reach audiences.
  • Using data, AI and audience insight to uncover opportunities, improve the way we work and evolve our communications approach.
  • Acting as a trusted advisor to our European Communications team, sharing expertise and helping shape consumer communications across our expansion markets.
  • Contributing to the leadership and continuous improvement of the Communications team, helping raise the quality of work through open, constructive feedback and thoughtful challenge.

🤩We’d love to hear from you if…

  • You have significant experience leading consumer communications or PR for a consumer-facing business.
  • You have a track record of developing earned communications strategies that deliver measurable business outcomes.
  • You're an excellent planner who can build long-term programmes while adapting confidently as priorities, timelines and business needs evolve.
  • You combine strategic thinking with hands-on execution.
  • You combine creativity with strong commercial and reputational judgement, considering the wider stakeholder landscape and the long-term impact of your work.
  • You have excellent editorial judgement and know what makes people pay attention.
  • You influence confidently across teams and seniority levels, building trusted relationships across the business.
  • You're curious about how AI, changing search behaviour, creators and new media are reshaping communications, and embrace new tools and technology to improve efficiency and effectiveness.
  • You have exceptional written and verbal communication skills.

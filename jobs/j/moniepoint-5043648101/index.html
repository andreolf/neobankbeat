Head of Operations & Compliance Audit

Remote, NigeriaCompliance & RiskRemote onlyposted today

Apply on Moniepoint →

About us Ranked in 2024 by the Financial Times, Moniepoint is Africa's fastest growing fintech, trusted by over 24 million business and individual accounts, processing billions of naira in transactions monthly. Our mission is to enable financial happiness for every African, everywhere. About this role The Head, Operations Compliance Audit is responsible for leading, managing, and executing comprehensive, risk-based internal audit engagements across operational, financial, compliance, and corporate support functions of Moniepoint Microfinance Bank. Grounded in professional standards (IIA, CBN Guidelines), you will provide independent, objective assurance and consulting services to Executive Management and the Board Finance, Audit Risk Committee (FARCO). The primary mandate is to evaluate and enhance the effectiveness of governance, risk management, internal controls, financial reporting integrity, regulatory compliance, and operational efficiency across all assigned domains.

Responsibilities

  • Develop, execute, and continuously update the annual risk-based internal audit plan for all assigned business units and supporting functions, aligning with the Bank's risk assessment framework.
  • Provide strategic insights to senior management and the Board Audit Committee on material risks, control weaknesses, and emerging exposures.
  • Drive continuous auditing, data analytics, and automation to improve audit coverage, detect revenue leakage, and identify duplicate or unauthorized ledger postings.
  • Lead and develop a high-performing team of auditors overseeing finance, fraud management, regulatory compliance, and branch operations.
  • Evaluate the design and operating effectiveness of Internal Control over Financial Reporting (ICFR) across core banking and ERP modules, as well as bank-wide cost optimization frameworks.
  • Oversee fraud risk screens, assess anti-fraud controls and whistle-blowing mechanisms, and ensure immediate escalation of established fraud cases.
  • Evaluate compliance with Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) regulations, the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), and Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing (AML/CFT) frameworks.
  • Manage routine and unannounced audits across the branch network, vendor lifecycle, corporate governance processes, and enterprise marketing spend.
  • Ensure all working papers, audit programs, compliance tests, and draft reports adhere strictly to IIA Standards.

Experience background

  • Minimum of 12-15 years progressive internal audit, control, or risk management experience within Financial Services (Fintech, Microfinance Banking, Deposit Money Banks).
  • Minimum of 5 years in a senior supervisory or managerial role leading internal audit teams.
  • Proven track record in revenue assurance, financial controls, branch operations audit, and regulatory compliance.
  • Minimum of a Bachelor's Degree (B.Sc. / HND) in Accounting, Finance, Economics, Business Administration, or related fields (Master's Degree is an added advantage).

Skills competencies

  • Mandatory professional qualification such as ACA, ACCA, CPA, or equivalent; specialized certifications (CIA, CISA, CFE, or CRISC) are highly preferred.
  • Deep understanding of CBN regulations, MFB Prudential Guidelines, Consumer Protection Framework, AML/CFT Regulations, NDPA, and IIA International Standards.
  • Advanced expertise in exploratory, predictive, and prescriptive data analytics and automated audit working paper tools.
  • Sound knowledge of core banking systems, general ledger integrity, financial statements, and payment workflows.
  • Uncompromising integrity, unwavering commitment to independence, and exceptional executive communication skills for board-level (FARCO) reporting.

What success looks like in the role

  • A comprehensively executed annual risk-based internal audit plan that aligns perfectly with the Bank's risk framework.
  • Quality assurance, working papers, and draft reports that adhere strictly to IIA Standards.
  • Proactive assessment of risks and accurate immediate escalation of material exposures and fraud cases to the CAO and Management.
  • High-performing and continuously developing internal audit teams delivering cross-functional process audits that successfully identify control gaps and bottlenecks.

Why join us

  • Shape the governance, risk management, and operational efficiency of one of Africa's fastest-growing fintechs.
  • Provide strategic, board-level impact by directly advising the Board Finance, Audit Risk Committee (FARCO) and Executive Management.
  • Lead diverse audit portfolios ranging from financial and regulatory compliance to enterprise marketing and branch operations.

Treasury Program Manager - Capital Allocation

United StatesProductRemote only

Apply on Kraken →

BUILDING THE FUTURE OF OPEN FINANCE Payward - the parent company behind Kraken, NinjaTrader, Breakout, xStocks, Payward Services and CF Benchmarks - has spent the last 15 years building one of the most modern and globally accessible financial infrastructure platforms in the industry, built to advance an open, global financial system. THE TEAM Founded in 2011, Kraken is one of the world's longest-standing crypto platforms, trusted by over 10 million individuals and institutions across the globe. It offers spot trading, margin, futures, staking, and OTC services, with products built for both individual investors and institutional clients.

As a Treasury Program Manager - Capital Allocation on our Capital Management team, you’ll own the operating process that takes every new Kraken product from idea to launch with its capital impact understood and signed off. You’ll run intake for the New Product Capital Assessment (NPCA) and New Product Approval Process (NPAP), learn how each product and business actually works, work with our Quant team to turn that understanding into rigorous capital calculations, and make sure the results are documented, repeatable, and ready for auditors and regulators. You care as much about how a product affects our clients as about how it affects our balance sheet.

THE OPPORTUNITY

  • Run NPCA and NPAP intake end to end: triage requests, gather product specifications, track milestones, and keep every launch on a clear, predictable timeline
  • Maintain the intake framework, including checklists, templates, risk-tiering criteria, and decision gates, and improve it as products and regulations evolve
  • Coordinate sign-offs across Product, Engineering, Quant, Risk, Legal, Compliance, Treasury, and Finance, escalating blockers early
  • Keep a complete audit trail of assumptions, approvals, and open items for every product reviewed
  • Report on pipeline status, cycle times, and capital impact to senior leadership
  • Learn each product in depth: how it’s built, how it settles, who uses it, where client assets sit, and what could go wrong
  • Work directly with Product Managers and business owners to map the full flow of client assets and exposures
  • Assess how each product and its risk controls affect clients, including liquidity, redemption, and settlement outcomes
  • Challenge assumptions constructively, and tell product teams early what a launch will require
  • Work with Quants to define, document, and validate methodologies for sizing RWA (credit, market, operational) and required capital (CET1 or GAAP equity) for each new product
  • Review and reconcile model inputs, assumptions, and outputs, and run independent sanity checks and sensitivity analysis
  • Apply capital floors and reserve requirements under frameworks such as the GENIUS Act, including operating expense backstops
  • Assess collateral adequacy, including asset quality, peg stability, and market depth, and review stress scenarios (e.g., a 50% drawdown in major crypto assets) for solvency and liquidation pathways
  • Cover crypto-specific risks in the assessment: smart-contract bugs, settlement latency, oracle manipulation, and run risk on tokenized liquidity pools
  • Map each new launch to applicable regulatory and prudential requirements with Legal and Compliance
  • Bridge the first line (Product, Quants) and second line (Risk, Compliance), and support defense of capital calculations to external auditors and banking regulators
  • Keep methodology documentation, control evidence, and process records current

WHAT YOU BRING

  • 6-8+ years in capital management, risk, treasury, finance, or product control at a bank, broker-dealer, or high-growth FinTech or crypto company
  • Hands-on experience running operational processes, such as product approvals, change management, or regulatory reporting, with clear controls and documentation
  • Solid grasp of capital and RWA concepts (Basel, CET1, liquidity and reserve requirements) and the ability to work through calculations and challenge model outputs
  • Comfort working with Quants and engineers, including reading methodologies and assumptions, and asking sharp questions
  • Ability to learn products and business models quickly, and explain their risk and client impact in plain language
  • Understanding of crypto-specific risks, including smart contracts, oracles, settlement finality, stablecoins, tokenization, and DeFi mechanics
  • Working knowledge of the regulatory environment for crypto, stablecoins, and tokenized assets
  • Strong project and program management skills, and the ability to drive consensus and deadlines across many teams
  • Proficiency in Excel and SQL; Python a plus
  • Excellent written and verbal communication, and rigorous attention to detail
  • Strong knowledge of, or demonstrated curiosity for, the cryptocurrency industry

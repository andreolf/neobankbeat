Associate Director, Customer Support (Operations Center)

Tempe, ArizonaCustomer SupportOn-siteposted 16d ago

About the Company Gemini is a global crypto and Web3 platform founded by Cameron and Tyler Winklevoss in 2014, offering a wide range of simple, reliable, and secure crypto products and services to individuals and institutions in over 70 countries. Our mission is to unlock the next era of financial, creative, and personal freedom by providing trusted access to the decentralized future. We envision a world where crypto reshapes the global financial system, internet, and money to create greater choice, independence, and opportunity for all — bridging traditional finance with the emerging cryptoeconomy in a way that is more open, fair, and secure. As a publicly traded company, Gemini is poised to accelerate this vision with greater scale, reach, and impact.

The Department: Support Gemini’s Customer Support Team is a highly skilled and dynamic group dedicated to providing exceptional customer service. Each team member plays a crucial role in ensuring that customers receive the best possible experience when seeking assistance, asking questions, or sharing feedback. The Gemini Credit Card and Exchange teams are expanding their presence with a regional office to better align customer support divisions. Collectively, these teams efficiently handle all inbound phone-based service requests and communications for the Gemini Credit Card and email-based requests for Gemini Exchange.

The Role

Associate Director, Customer Support (Operations Center) As an Associate Director of the regional team, your role will be a combination of “getting your hands dirty” and management responsibilities within an inbound contact center. You will be expected to lead the workforce management function, collaborate with the Operations team with forecasting and building team rapport. This role is required to be in person 5 days per week at our Tempe, AZ office.

Responsibilities

  • Lead workforce management activities, including scheduling, forecasting, headcount planning, and timecard management to ensure optimal staffing and service levels.
  • Lead local shift managers while partnering with global operations leaders to drive alignment and operational excellence across regions.
  • Build and sustain a high-performance, customer-centric culture rooted in accountability, continuous improvement, and operational excellence.
  • Coach, develop, and empower leaders and team members through performance management, targeted training, feedback, and recognition to achieve individual and team goals.
  • Translate organizational strategy into actionable operational plans, measurable outcomes, and scalable solutions that support business growth.
  • Identify operational inefficiencies and leverage data, analytics, and process improvements to optimize performance and drive continuous improvement.
  • Ensure operational readiness and business continuity for new products, business initiatives, organizational growth, and incident response.
  • Partner cross-functionally with Global Operations, Legal, Facilities, Office Management, and Security to ensure compliance with company policies and maintain a safe, secure, and productive workplace.
  • Manage site budgets and operating expenses while identifying opportunities to improve efficiency and optimize costs.

Minumum

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent practical experience.
  • Minimum 12 years of experience in a contact center, contact centers or financial services environment.
  • Minimum of 5 years of contact center workforce management experience.
  • Proficiency in Google applications (Sheets, Docs, Slides).
  • Proven success scaling operations during periods of rapid organizational growth.
  • Strong understanding of Workforce Management principles, forecasting, scheduling, and capacity planning.
  • Experience using operational metrics and data analytics to drive business decisions.
  • Experience leading change management and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Ability to thrive in a dynamic, high-growth environment with evolving priorities.
  • Strong communication skills and a dependable team-player attitude.
  • Ability to adapt quickly to new information and changing environments.
  • Passion for delivering excellent client service with integrity and a proactive approach.

Preferred

Qualifications

  • 15+ years of experience in the banking or financial industry.
  • 10+ years of client service experience.
  • Associate Degree or higher.
  • Experience within fintech, financial services, payments, or another regulated industry.

It Pays to Work Here

The compensation benefits package for this role includes

  • Competitive starting pay
  • A discretionary annual bonus
  • Long-term incentive in the form of a new hire equity grant
  • Comprehensive health plans
  • 401K with company matching
  • Paid Parental Leave
  • Flexible time off

Head of Compliance and Licensing

United States · United KingdomCompliance & RiskOn-sitevisa sponsorship

Apply on Plasma One →

ABOUT PLASMA Plasma is building the primary account for global money. Money used to belong to a country. Increasingly, it belongs to a person or business moving through many countries. People and businesses now earn, spend, save, hire and invest across borders. Traditional banks cannot serve this new world. Our first product, Plasma One, is a global money account: a place to receive, hold, send funds in seconds, earn yield and spend with a card in the local economy. Underneath is the Plasma network, built from first principles for stablecoin payments. Stablecoins make dollars faster, cheaper and more reliable to move.

The opportunity is to become where all global money lands first: where income arrives, balances grow, payments move, businesses operate, and new financial products are built. THE TEAM & OUR STORY https://x.com/Plasma/status/2067634709727719601?s=20 We are a global team of founders, operators and engineers, obsessed with building the best products. We work at the cutting edge of financial innovation, using stablecoins to create tools for consumers and businesses. We operate with trust, clarity, and ambition: own your craft, move with urgency, contribute beyond your lane, back your teammates, debate rigorously, and keep raising the bar for what great means.

THE ROLE

You'll report to our General Counsel and own compliance and licensing at Plasma. That means building and running our global compliance program, and leading the licensing work that gets us into new markets. This is a hands-on role. We're looking for an operator who's ready to own more. You'll set the priorities, write the docs yourself, work directly with partners and make sure things actually ship across Legal, Product and Engineering. GLOBAL COMPLIANCE PROGRAM

  • Build and own the global program and proportionate market implementations: AML/CFT, sanctions, KYC and KYB, transaction monitoring across fiat and on-chain flows, Travel Rule and consumer protection. Make clear which controls Plasma operates and which sit with regulated partners.
  • Own the financial crime risk assessments, company-wide and for each market, and the policies and controls that follow from them.
  • Set the compliance conditions for new products, features and markets before launch, working with Product and Engineering from the design stage. Turn requirements into practical controls, identify owners and verify readiness, escalating material risks and unresolved legal questions to the General Counsel.
  • Own the compliance relationships with our banking, card and other regulated partners. Oversee the controls they require of us, the controls we rely on them for, and their diligence requests and audits.
  • Choose and run the compliance tooling with Operations and Engineering, and automate first-line review as volume grows, with clear escalation paths and quality checks.
  • Run compliance testing, staff training and independent audit readiness.

GLOBAL LICENSING AND MARKET ENTRY

  • Work alongside Legal in setting the licensing roadmap: which markets, which license in each, if needed, and in what order.
  • For each market, recommend whether to build, buy or partner, with the cost, timeline, capital and headcount each option needs, so Leadership can decide how to appropriately enter new markets.
  • Where needed, run licensing applications end to end: draft the regulatory business plan, program of operations and policies, coordinate financial projections with Finance and fit-and-proper filings with the relevant officers, and manage the regulator's questions through to approval.
  • Lead the regulatory side of acquiring licensed entities: diligence on the target's licenses and compliance record, change-in-control filings, and integration after closing.
  • Recommend when a product or market can run on a partner’s license and when it needs our own, working with Legal to resolve the regulatory perimeter. Document the agreed position for each product and turn it into clear launch conditions, so the reasoning holds up with regulators and partners.

GOVERNANCE

  • Lead regulatory engagement for our licensing and compliance workstreams alongside Legal and the relevant local officers. Be Plasma's primary day-to-day contact for the compliance teams at our banking and card partners.
  • Report to Leadership on a regular cycle: license status, alerts and case backlog, suspicious activity filings, onboarding outcomes, open findings.
  • Track regulatory change in our markets and turn it into changes to the roadmap, the product and the program.
  • Work with Legal on regulatory analysis, outside counsel and consultants. Own the compliance and licensing budget within agreed priorities, and make the case for the people, tooling and specialist support needed to deliver.
  • Build the compliance and licensing function as Plasma grows, including recommending where to hire or use specialist support. Coordinate local officers and programs while preserving their jurisdiction-specific accountability and required independence.

THE BAR

  • You have 4+ years in compliance, licensing or regulatory roles in payments, e-money, neobanking or digital assets firms. You have owned substantive workstreams at a fast-moving fintech and are ready to lead a function. We care more about what you have delivered than your previous title.
  • You have personally written and implemented meaningful parts of a compliance program, and can explain how you tested the controls and supported a regulator review or partner bank audit.
  • You have played a substantial hands-on role in a licensing application, regulatory approval or market-entry process. You can show what you drafted, which issues you resolved and how you drove it forward, not just which licenses your employer holds.
  • You have strong practical knowledge of AML, sanctions and KYC, with depth in at least one relevant regulatory framework and experience adapting controls for another market or working closely with local specialists.
  • You understand how responsibilities are divided between a fintech, its banking and card partners and its technology providers. You bring relevant experience in card programs, stablecoin on- and off-ramps or wallet models, and can get up to speed on the rest.
  • You take work from an open question to a clear recommendation and completed implementation. You write well, manage dependencies and influence cross-functional teams without relying on a large team beneath you. Previous people-management experience is helpful, not required.

NICE TO HAVE

  • You have run compliance for a card issuing program with a sponsor bank.
  • You have worked directly with UAE regulators (such as VARA, the CBUAE, ADGM or DIFC) or Asian regulators (such as MAS in Singapore or the SFC and HKMA in Hong Kong).
  • You hold a Law degree, CAMS or a similar certification.

WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

  • Above-market salary for the level of the role, with token compensation.
  • A beautiful London HQ with gym access and daily food, for those who thrive in an office environment. Visa sponsorship and relocation support if you're joining from abroad.
  • Premium health insurance for you and your family, fully covered by Plasma.
  • Monthly wellness budget, whether that's the gym, therapy, sauna, or massage.
  • All the tools and tech you need to operate at your best.
  • Real ownership and responsibility from day one, with your work shipping to hundreds of thousands of people, moving millions of dollars every day.

Data Protection & Privacy We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal data. Any personal information you provide during the recruitment process will be processed in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable local data protection laws.

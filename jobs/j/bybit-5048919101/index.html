Head of Financial Cloud Product

About Us Established in 2018, Bybit is one of the world’s leading cryptocurrency exchanges and digital financial platforms, serving over 90 million users across more than 200 countries and regions. Powered by world-class technology and a user-first mindset, Bybit delivers a seamless ecosystem across trading, payments, wealth management, custody, institutional services, and Web3 — connecting users to the future of digital finance. Our core values define how we build. We listen, care and improve to create products and experiences that put users first. Backed by a global team of ambitious builders, problem-solvers, and innovators, we foster a high-performance and fast-moving environment where talent is empowered to drive real impact at the global scale. Supported by 24/7 multilingual customer service and a strong commitment to innovation, we are shaping the future of finance through technology, collaboration, and bold execution.

Today, Bybit is recognized as one of the most trusted and transparent platforms in the digital asset industry, continuing to expand its global presence while building the infrastructure for the next generation of financial services.

About the Role

We are looking for a senior product leader who combines technical product judgment, financial-services knowledge, and commercialization experience to turn existing financial and digital-asset technology capabilities into enterprise products for external customers. Financial Cloud aims to provide digital-asset and Web3 technology infrastructure to traditional financial institutions and other enterprise customers. Starting with customer needs, you will determine which capabilities, including trading technology, wallets, and custody-related technology, should be productized first. You will establish a focused product proposition, a viable commercial model, and a repeatable delivery approach, progressing from customer validation toward sustainable growth.

What You Will Own

  • Target customers and initial use cases. Conduct customer discovery and market validation to identify priority customer segments, critical problems, buying motivations, and decision-makers. Select a focused initial product and use case rather than commercializing every available capability at once.
  • Financial Cloud product strategy. Turn underlying technology into products that customers can evaluate, purchase, integrate, and use over time. Define the product portfolio, module catalog, value proposition, functional boundaries, API contracts, and roadmap.
  • Enterprise productization. Partner with technical teams to define tenant and data isolation, access controls, configuration, auditability, APIs and SDKs, customer-facing management interfaces, monitoring, and operational requirements. Choose appropriate hosted, private, or other deployment models based on validated customer needs.
  • Commercial models. Work with sales, finance, and relevant teams on product packaging, pricing, and billing. Validate willingness to pay, implementation economics, and ongoing service costs. Support presales, pilots, and the definition of product scope and commitments in customer contracts.
  • First-customer adoption. Partner with sales, solution, and delivery teams to move customers through proof of concept, pilot, acceptance, and production use. Define clear success criteria and turn customer learning into product improvements without allowing customization to expand without limits.
  • Repeatable delivery. Establish the boundary between standard products and customization. Improve documentation, onboarding, implementation standards, version compatibility, and lifecycle management to reduce per-customer delivery effort and improve the customer experience.
  • Technology-provider boundaries. Work with legal, compliance, security, and architecture teams to clarify service scope, data and access responsibilities, key-control responsibilities, customer regulatory obligations, and service commitments. Ensure product design and commercial representations remain consistent with approved activities.
  • Team and investment priorities. Build a product team suited to the zero-to-one stage and lead cross-functional execution. Align with the Internal Platform product leader on capability reuse, engineering dependencies, and priorities, avoiding customer-specific duplication of core systems.

What We Are Looking For

  • Senior enterprise product experience, with end-to-end ownership of product definition, delivery, and commercialization from zero to one. You can explain how you selected customers, established product boundaries, and validated paid demand.
  • Experience building platform or technical products such as financial infrastructure, developer platforms, API products, enterprise SaaS, financial cloud, payment infrastructure, or digital-asset infrastructure.
  • A track record commercializing products for external enterprise customers, with an understanding of procurement, security reviews, pilots, contracts, deployment, acceptance, and ongoing service requirements.
  • The ability to translate complex technology into customer value and engage deeply with engineering on architecture boundaries, APIs, data isolation, deployment models, and non-functional requirements.
  • Strong judgment in pricing, packaging, implementation economics, and standardization, including explicit trade-offs between customer customization and the long-term product roadmap.
  • An understanding of regulatory constraints in financial services and digital assets. You can identify matters requiring legal or compliance review and translate confirmed boundaries into product and delivery requirements.
  • Cross-functional leadership skills and the ability to make progress with a lean team while the product proposition and market are still being validated.
  • Professional working proficiency in English for product discussions with international customers and partners. Mandarin language skill is a big plus.

Strongly Preferred

  • Experience turning internal technology capabilities into standardized external products with paying customers.
  • Experience serving banks, brokerages, payment institutions, asset managers, or other regulated financial institutions.
  • Experience with trading technology, wallets, MPC, custody-related technology, digital-asset infrastructure, or asset tokenization.
  • Experience with multi-tenant products, private or hybrid deployments, international delivery, or channel and ecosystem partnerships.
  • A track record turning pilots or custom implementations into repeatable products and delivery models, demonstrated through adoption, revenue, or cost improvements.

Leadership Profile You understand technology and actively engage with customers to test demand. You can select the most valuable initial product from a broad set of capabilities, make evidence-based decisions, and decline customization that undermines the product direction. You distinguish validated customer needs from assumptions and explain both clearly. What Success Looks Like

  • A validated target customer segment, priority use case, and value proposition translate into concrete purchasing or paid-use behavior.
  • Initial customers integrate and use the product successfully, providing evidence of customer value and commercial viability.
  • Delivery becomes repeatable, onboarding improves, and per-customer customization and ongoing service costs remain controlled.
  • Product adoption, continued customer use, and commercial results show measurable progress, with revenue accountability and targets agreed with the commercial team.
  • Technology-service boundaries are clear, and security, data isolation, compliance, and contractual commitments are reflected in product delivery.

Why Join Us At Bybit, we are committed to fostering a supportive and enriching work environment. Our benefits include:

  • Study Growth Fund: We support your professional development and continuous learning.
  • Internal Events: Participate in regular team-building activities, workshops, and events designed to promote collaboration and innovation.
  • Global Collaboration: Be part of a diverse, international team, working alongside colleagues from around the world.
  • Career Advancement: Access opportunities for growth and advancement within a rapidly expanding global company.
  • Internal Mobility: Grow with us- Your long-term development is important to us. We offer internal job opportunities to help build your career path.

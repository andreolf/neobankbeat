Senior Manager, Global Compliance Strategy and Policies (UK)

LondonCompliance & RiskHybridposted today

Apply on Crypto.com →

About the Role

The Senior Manager, Global Compliance Strategy and Policies (UK) is a senior member of the central policy team, responsible for translating Crypto.com’s global compliance framework into operational policies, standards, and procedures with primary ownership of UK and EEA regulatory alignment. Working under the VP’s direction, you will draft, maintain, and drive adoption of policy to ensure that UK and EEA regulatory requirements are accurately reflected in the global framework.

This is a high-ownership individual contributor / small-team lead role with significant exposure to the CCO, regional compliance heads, and UK/EEA regulators. You will be the central team’s primary point of accountability for UK and EEA-facing policy content. Key Responsibilities Policy Development and Maintenance

  • Draft, review, and maintain assigned domains of the global policy suite — AML/CFT, sanctions, KYC/CDD, transaction monitoring, travel rule, market abuse, financial promotions, and recordkeeping — ensuring consistency with group drafting standards and tiering.
  • Lead the UK overlay: own UK-specific annexes and local procedures that implement global policy in line with UK requirements (MLRs 2017, Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, OFSI sanctions regime, FCA Handbook including SYSC, and the UK cryptoasset financial promotions regime).
  • Conduct gap analyses of existing UK policies against evolving regulatory expectations; propose and execute remediation.
  • Coordinate policy approval workflows, version control, and periodic review cadence for assigned domains.

Regulatory Change Management (UK / EEA)

  • Run the UK and EEA regulatory change pipeline for the central team: monitor FCA, HM Treasury, OFSI, JMLSG, and where relevant EEA supervisors; assess impact on policy; drive updates through the change process.
  • Translate UK and EEA rulemaking, FCA policy statements, enforcement actions, and supervisory letters into policy requirements and implementation tasks.
  • Support horizon scanning input to the VP for UK and EEA-related strategic planning, licensing, and product launches, including FCA cryptoasset registration, UK stablecoin regime, and MiCA adjacency for EEA operations.

Regional Implementation and Stakeholder Partnership

  • Partner with the UK and EEA Head of Compliance / MLRO and Legal, Risk, Product, and Operations teams to embed policy requirements into systems, onboarding flows, transaction monitoring rules, and product design.
  • Serve as the central policy team’s primary liaison to UK and EEA regional compliance; arbitrate first-line escalations where local practice diverges from global standard, escalating material conflicts to the VP.
  • Support FCA supervisory engagement, and independent audits with policy evidence, drafting responses, and remediation tracking.
  • Contribute to UK and EEA regulatory filings, FCA registration applications, and gateway reviews from a policy-content perspective.
  • Contribute to policy training content and compliance culture initiatives in partnership with HR/Learning.

Requirements

Required

  • 8+ years in compliance, financial crime, or regulatory affairs, with at least 2–3 years drafting or owning policy at a regulated financial institution (bank, payments, e-money, broker-dealer, or cryptoasset firm).
  • Deep working knowledge of UK AML/CFT and sanctions frameworks(MLRs 2017, POCA 2002, OFSI regime, JMLSG guidance), UK’s financial promotions regime, FCA regulations, and the MiCA framework. Practical experience with MiFID II is considered a strong plus.
  • Familiarity with the UK cryptoasset regime, and the MiCA framework
  • Demonstrated experience producing regulator-grade written work products (policy documents, FCA information requests, s.166 responses, registration applications).
  • Track record engaging with the FCA or other UK and EEA supervisors directly or through supervisory support.
  • Experience operating in a matrixed global organization; able to balance group standards with legitimate local tailoring.
  • Excellent written communication; able to draft board-grade and regulator-grade documents.

Preferred

  • Crypto/digital assets

experience

travel rule implementation, FCA cryptoasset registration, blockchain analytics tooling (Chainalysis, TRM, Elliptic).

  • Exposure to EEA regulatory frameworks including MiCA, EU AMLR/AMLA — particularly where the firm operates across both UK and EEA entities.
  • SM&CR awareness; prior experience holding or supporting an SMF16/SMF17 function is a plus.
  • ICA Diploma, CAMS, or equivalent certification; legal qualification (LPC/SQE or equivalent) a plus but not required.
  • Familiarity with GRC platforms and policy management tooling.

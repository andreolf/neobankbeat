Product Manager (AI & Blockchain) LATAM

LATAMEngineeringRemote onlyposted Mar 13

About us

Airtm is a financial-infrastructure company building the future of the online-work economy. We are on a mission to empower the world's growing number of Digital Entrepreneurs in the Global South, giving them the financial freedom to thrive. The problem is clear: in emerging markets, accessing the dollar economy is difficult. Cross-border payments are slow, expensive, and often lose value to inflation. This limits the potential of millions of talented individuals. Airtm’s solution is a swift and comprehensive financial platform that facilitates low-value cross-border payments and local cash-outs. As pioneers in stablecoin-payment infrastructure, Airtm has built the most advanced cross-border payment system available on the market.

As a company married to the world of online work, Airtm will go beyond payments to build the necessary infrastructure the online-work economy needs to thrive. We are fostering an entirely new economy, giving individuals, communities, and countries the tools to take control of their financial destinies. About

the role

This is a high-impact role for someone who can connect vision with execution. You'll shape the roadmap, align cross-functional teams, and build scalable solutions involving blockchain products, leveraging AI to solve critical needs for our users and move faster than the competition. Key Responsibilities Define a long-term product vision aligned with Airtm’s mission and goals. Conduct structured customer and market research to deeply understand pain points and opportunities. Translate vision into a realistic, high-leverage roadmap and backlog that balances short-term growth with long-term scalability.

Collaborate with engineering, design, legal, compliance, and commercial teams to build and ship features that drive user adoption. Work closely with growth and marketing to accelerate go-to-market adoption and unlock new revenue streams. Identify and champion opportunities to embed AI powered capabilities directly into the product, from intelligent automation and personalization to surfacing insights that help users make better financial decisions. Use AI tools actively in your day-to-day workflow for research, spec writing, competitive analysis, data synthesis and help raise the bar for how the broader team works.

Qualifications

5+ years of Product Management experience, ideally in blockchain, fintech or SaaS companies Proven success building and launching consumer facing products Experience in agile environments with cross-functional global teams Strong product intuition backed by analytical rigor Familiarity with crypto, stablecoins, wallets, or blockchain infrastructure Active, practical experience with AI tools (e.g. ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Perplexity, Notion AI, or equivalents) You'll be a great fit if you are

A confident, data driven, force of nature that knows how to move the needle in a high growth start-up environment. A strategic thinker who can zoom out to market dynamics and zoom in to execution. Genuinely excited about blockchain and crypto. A natural collaborator who communicates clearly across functions and levels. Hands-on with AI tools and always experimenting with what’s next. Highly analytical, but equally comfortable navigating ambiguity and taking ownership. Customer-obsessed, with a strong sense of product-market fit and value delivery.

Skilled at balancing long-term bets with short-term wins.

Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, India, Germany and France. Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love.

About the role

We’re looking for an exceptional Lead Country Commercial Manager to drive revenue growth from our existing Insurance products, to launch new products to market, and drive our external GTM (eg. ATL advertising with insurance products as the lead). You will sit at the heart of Tide’s commercial function, and be responsible for collaborating with cross-functional teams to understand product performance, uncover opportunities, and work across multiple areas to deliver. This will require a relentless focus on the numbers and our members, product prioritisation, and detailed planning to ensure revenue targets are hit and customer lifetime value is always increasing.

In this role, you will be responsible for the strategy and implementation of core activities to drive sales and renewals of Insurance. This requires close partnership with marketing teams to ensure seamless delivery of ‘always on’ campaign activity. This role will also own the strategy and launch approach for new product launches, ensuring that all channels and member touch points are considered. This includes end-to-end journeys and transaction channels. You’ll need to partner effectively with multiple stakeholders to make this happen –

Some of the things you’ll be doing

  • Revenue Ownership Forecasting: Partner closely with Finance and key stakeholders to set, track, and refine financial projections for Insurance products, ensuring revenue accountability and proactive identification of risks and opportunities.
  • Analytics-Driven Insight: with the support of the BI team you will analyse member behaviour, segment audiences, and measure subscription cohort performance. You will then translate this data into clear recommendations that shape product prioritisation, pricing, positioning and communications.
  • Product Development: Leveraging the commercial and analytics insights, you will partner with the Product teams to address areas of opportunity. This will include shaping product positioning, product led growth, and in-app UX – aligning on roadmaps and supporting successful product launches with tailored GTM strategies.
  • Launch Strategy: You’ll lead new product launches, partnering with excellent sales, and operations teams to deliver best-in-class experiments. You’ll facilitate brainstorm sessions / workshops to surface creative ideas and implementation plans.
  • Stakeholder Management: As you’ll be working in a highly collaborative way, it will be key to communicate regularly with a wide range of stakeholders. This will include Tide’s Senior Leadership Team as well as the C-Suite.
  • Commercial Competitor Intelligence: Lead ongoing commercial research and competitive analysis to monitor trends, and benchmark to translate it into actionable strategies that strengthen and differentiate Tide’s positioning and value proposition.
  • Member Obsession: You will be or become an expert in the core motivations of our members in order to focus on bringing their voice to commercial and product design discussions.

What we are looking for

  • Strong commercial acumen: Proven experience in driving commercial impact, partnering with finance to understand performance and identify opportunities to improve. Able to set and track clear KPIs across all initiatives to meet revenue goals.
  • 5+ years’ experience leading ‘always on’ activity to drive commercial performance. This will ideally be with specific experience in Insurance, ideally in a fintech environment – key is demonstrated ability to plan and deliver consistent activity that drives commercial impact.
  • Strong analytical mindset: Able to review and interpret data to develop hypotheses and assumptions, turning insights into clear, actionable plans.
  • Proven GTM track record – delivering holistic, high impact omni-channel campaigns (across sales, CRM, in-app contact points)
  • Exceptional project management skills, able to juggle multiple launches and tactical tests in a fast-moving environment.
  • Creative collaborator: Builds relationships across teams, crafts compelling messaging and navigates ambiguity with confidence.
  • Self-starter attitude: Works backwards from targets, removes blockers proactively and adapts when priorities shift.

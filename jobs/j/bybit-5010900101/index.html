About the Role

We are looking for a versatile, people-oriented Senior HR Operations Specialist to join our EMEA HR Operations team in Istanbul. Our team supports operations across the UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi), Georgia, Turkey, Kazakhstan, and Lithuania. In this role, you will be the primary local HR operations contact for Turkey while supporting employee services and regional projects across assigned EMEA countries and entities. You will also contribute to new-country setup, entity transfers, and the standardization and automation of HR processes.

This is a hands-on role combining local regulatory expertise, cross-border collaboration, and continuous improvement to deliver accurate, efficient, and consistent HR services. Key Responsibilities

  • Employee Lifecycle HR Operations
  • Manage onboarding for Turkey and assigned EMEA locations, including employment documentation, vendor registration, and coordination with internal teams.
  • Coordinate offboarding activities, including documentation, applicable statutory notifications, benefits cessation, and arrangements with the teams responsible for access removal and asset returns.
  • Provide accurate employee information and supporting records to the designated payroll team or vendor for offboarding.
  • HR Systems, Data Reporting
  • Maintain accurate, up-to-date employee records in HRIS and related systems, ensuring data integrity, confidentiality, and timely updates.
  • Conduct regular data checks and reconciliations to identify and resolve discrepancies.
  • Prepare HR operational reports and maintain audit-ready records.
  • Employee Support Documentation
  • Respond to employee queries on onboarding, employment documentation, benefits, immigration, offboarding, and other HR operational matters.
  • Prepare employment verification, reference, visa-support, and other employee letters using approved templates and in accordance with local requirements.
  • Track queries through resolution and escalate complex or sensitive matters to the relevant HR partner or EMEA HR Lead.
  • Support employee relations matters in Turkey, including grievances, disciplinary procedures, and performance improvement plans, in partnership with HR and Legal.
  • Visa Immigration
  • Coordinate visa, work permit, and residency processes for Turkey and assigned EMEA locations with external immigration providers and internal stakeholders.
  • Track application progress, renewal dates, and required documentation.
  • Provide clear guidance to employees on immigration requirements and timelines.
  • Escalate risks or delays that could affect start dates, employee transfers, or ongoing employment.
  • New-Country Setup Entity Transfers
  • Support HR workstreams for new-country setup, new-entity setup, and entity transfers, including vendor coordination, employment documentation, benefits arrangements, HRIS readiness, and operational processes.
  • Maintain project trackers, monitor dependencies, and escalate risks to support timely implementation.
  • Help establish scalable HR processes for new or growing locations in partnership with regional stakeholders and local advisers.
  • Benefits Administration
  • Administer benefits enrollment, changes, renewals, and cessation for assigned countries and entities.
  • Work with providers, brokers, and internal stakeholders to resolve employee queries and service issues.
  • Support benefits reviews and improvements in line with company policy, local requirements, and regional priorities.
  • HR Compliance Audits
  • Ensure HR operations in Turkey comply with applicable labor and social security requirements, and support compliance across assigned EMEA locations.
  • Monitor relevant regulatory changes and support updates to employment documentation, policies, and procedures.
  • Support internal and external HR audits by preparing records, responding to queries, and tracking corrective actions.
  • Process Improvement Regional Support
  • Develop and improve HR operations SOPs, checklists, templates, and service workflows.
  • Standardize regional processes while accommodating country-specific legal and operational requirements.
  • Identify process gaps, recurring employee issues, and opportunities to reduce manual work through automation.
  • Share best practices to support consistent service delivery across the EMEA HR Operations team.
  • Support regional HR projects and initiatives led by the EMEA HR Lead.
  • Assist with local recruitment coordination and employer branding activities as needed.
  • Monitor local hiring trends and compensation benchmarks to inform regional HR planning.

Required Qualifications

  • At least five years of progressive HR experience in HR operations, HR generalist, or HR management roles.
  • Comprehensive knowledge of Turkish labor law and local employment administration.
  • Demonstrated experience managing employee lifecycle processes, HRIS records, benefits administration, and employee queries.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively with regional teams, internal stakeholders, and external vendors across multiple countries.
  • Fluency in Turkish and English, both written and spoken.
  • Experience building HR processes, policies, and systems in a new or scaling environment.
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage competing priorities.
  • A proactive, self-driven approach, with a track record of identifying gaps and implementing practical solutions.
  • A continuous-improvement mindset and an interest in HR standardization and automation.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience supporting HR operations across multiple EMEA countries.
  • Exposure to new-country setup, entity transfers, or cross-border employee mobility.
  • Experience coordinating visa and work permit processes.
  • Prior involvement in HR compliance reviews, audits, or SOP development.
  • Experience with HRIS implementation, employee data workflows, or HR process automation.
  • Experience in the technology, fintech, or crypto/Web3 sector.

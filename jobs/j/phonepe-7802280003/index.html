About PhonePe Limited

Headquartered in India, its flagship product, the PhonePe digital payments app, was launched in Aug

  • As of April 2025, PhonePe has over 60 Crore (600 Million) registered users and a digital payments acceptance network spread across over 4 Crore (40+ million) merchants. PhonePe also processes over 33 Crore (330+ Million) transactions daily with an Annualized Total Payment Value (TPV) of over INR 150 lakh crore.

PhonePe’s portfolio of businesses includes the distribution of financial products (Insurance, Lending, and Wealth) as well as new consumer tech businesses (Pincode - hyperlocal e-commerce and Indus AppStore Localized App Store for the Android ecosystem) in India, which are aligned with the company’s vision to offer every Indian an equal opportunity to accelerate their progress by unlocking the flow of money and access to services.

WHY DOES THIS ROLE EXIST? At share.market, we’re building the future of intelligent trading investing for India. Finance can feel confusing. Broking can feel intimidating.

Our mission is simple

  • Make finance clear easy to understand
  • Make wealth creation accessible provide intelligent insights to power your wealth creation journey

This is where you come in. We’re looking for someone who can be the face and voice of modern financial storytelling, someone who is equally comfortable in front of the camera and has a knack for financial research and written visual storytelling. This is not just a content role. This is not just a scriptwriting role. This is a video first, creative marketing role. You will help us explain broking, trading, investing, fintech, capital markets in a way that builds trust and drives growth.

15–20%: AI-Powered Content Systems AI is not optional here. It’s core to the role.

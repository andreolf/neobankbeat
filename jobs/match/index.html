the job board · cv match

find roles that fit your CV

Drop a résumé or paste text — we score it against 3,730 live neobank openings. Everything runs in your browser; we never see your file.

privateYour CV never leaves this browser. We do not upload, store, or log it — no server, no database, no cookies. Matching runs locally against jobs/data.json. Close the tab and it is gone.

how matching works

No AI, no accounts. Your CV text is tokenised and scored against 3,730 live neobank roles using keyword overlap and TF‑IDF weighting — title matches count more than body text. It is a starting point, not career advice: always read the full posting and apply on the company's site.

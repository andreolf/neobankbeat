the job board

feature your job listings

Pin your neobank roles to the top of the jobs board with a visible "Featured" badge. Your roles stay pinned until their expiry date, clearly labeled and above all other listings.

What you get

  • Top placement — featured roles pin to the top of the jobs board, above the 3,500+ standard listings
  • Visible badge — a "Featured" label appears on every featured listing, both in the job list and on its detail page
  • Board-wide presence — featured jobs appear at the top of the homepage, department pages, and the RSS feed
  • Set duration — choose your expiry date (typical: 30–90 days); featured status expires automatically at the next build

How it works

Your roles are probably already on the board — we pull them directly from your public careers API (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, or bunq/Deel). Featured placements add a pin-to-top flag and a badge; they don't change what the listing says.

  1. Check the board — your company should already appear at neobankbeat.com/jobs. If not, submit your careers API link and we'll wire it in.
  2. Pick your roles — tell us which postings you want featured (by URL or title)
  3. Set your expiry — choose how long they stay pinned (30–90 days is typical)
  4. Go live — we add the feature flags and rebuild the board (usually within 24 hours)

Pricing

On request. Pricing depends on the number of roles, duration, and whether you want company-level promotion (your logo card gets a boost on the "who's hiring" grid).

Who sees it

The jobs board gets organic search traffic from fintech professionals looking for roles at neobanks — engineers, compliance specialists, product managers, and support teams. The board aggregates 3,500+ live postings from 69 tracked neobanks, refreshed daily.

Editorial independence

Featured listings are clearly labeled and have no effect on:

  • Whether your neobank gets tracked in the directory
  • How your profile page ranks in comparisons or search
  • What the blog or the monthly report say about your company

The directory and the dataset stay editorially independent. Featured placements buy visibility, not editorial coverage.

Get started

Reach out via the partner page or email neobanks@swissintech.com — mention which roles you want featured and your preferred duration.

Not hiring yet? Your roles will appear on the board automatically once your careers API is live. Submit your API link here.
